# 神農本草經 上經 001 丹沙 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dansha (cinnabar). Sweet, slightly cold. Governs the hundred illnesses of the body and the five zang; nourishes the essence and spirit, calms the hun and po, augments qi, brightens the eyes, and kills spectral demons and evil ghosts. Taken long, it gives access to the spirit light and one does not age. It can be transformed into mercury. It grows in mountain valleys. (Quotations in the *Taiping yulan* mostly have the three characters "grows in mountain valleys"; the *Daguan* edition reads "grows in the mountain valleys of Fuling", in black characters. On examination, "grows in mountain valleys" is the text of the Classic, and later people added the commandery and county names. It should be changed to white characters, with the place names in black characters. All the following entries are treated likewise.)

The *Wu Pu bencao* says: Dansha. Shennong: sweet; Huangdi: bitter, toxic; Bian Que: bitter; Mr Li: very cold. It may grow in Wuling. Collected at no fixed time. It can be transformed into mercury [base: 化未成]. It fears magnetite and abhors salt water. (*Taiping yulan*)

The *Mingyi* says: Ground to powder it is called "true vermilion". That with a gloss and color like mica, which can be split, is good. It grows in the mountain valleys of Fuling. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on dansha (cinnabar, generally identified as mercuric sulfide), the first upper-grade drug.
- 殺精魁 in the base is emended to 殺精魅 ("spectral demons"); the *Zhenglei bencao*, *Xinxiu bencao* and the Executive Yuan texts all read 精魅.
- The parenthetical note in the main text is Sun Xingyan's: only "grows in mountain valleys" is treated as text of the Classic, and the place names as later additions.
- In Wu Pu's quotation 能化未成水銀, the *Wu Pu bencao* compilation on Zhongyi Jicheng reads 能化朱成水銀; the translation follows the sense.
- Sun's note (案) is not translated (original only). It cites the *Shuowen*, *Guanzi*, *Huainanzi* and *Liexian zhuan*, and warns that taking metal and stone drugs together with meat is harmful.
