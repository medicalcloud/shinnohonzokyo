# 神農本草經 上經 002 雲母 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yunmu (unmo; mica). Sweet, neutral. Governs dead flesh of the skin of the body, and wind-stroke with cold and heat as though one were on a cart or boat. It dispels pathogenic qi, calms the five zang, augments the seminal essence and brightens the eyes. Taken long, it lightens the body and prolongs the years. Also called yunzhu, yunhua, yunying, yunye, yunsha and linshi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows among the rocks of Mount Tai, Mount Qilu, Langye and Mount Beiding. Collected in the second month. (The sayings of the *Mingyi* are recorded here because they are what [Zhang] Zhongjing, Yuanhua [Hua Tuo] and [Wu] Pu said; later people combined them so that they can no longer be distinguished. They also supplement what is missing from Pu's book.)

## Notes

The entry on yunmu (mica).
- "As though on a cart or boat" is generally understood as dizziness with a swaying sensation.
- The note after the *Mingyi* quotation is Sun Xingyan's, explaining why he records the *Mingyi* (*Bielu*) text.
- Sun's note (案) is not translated; it cites the *Baopuzi* on five kinds of mica.
