# 神農本草經 上經 004 石鐘乳 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shizhongru (stalactite). Sweet, warm. Governs cough with counterflow and rising qi. It brightens the eyes and augments essence, calms the five zang, opens the hundred joints, frees the nine orifices and brings down breast milk (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called liugongru"; the *Daguan* edition reads "also called gongru", in black characters). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Zhongru, also called xuzhong. Shennong: acrid; Tongjun, Huangdi, Yi He: sweet; Bian Que: sweet, non-toxic (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "Mr Li: very cold"). It grows in mountain valleys (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "the mountain valleys of Mount Tai"), formed by dripping liquid beneath cliffs in shady places (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "formed by the accumulation of dripping liquid"); it is like milk, yellowish-white, hollow inside and interconnected. Collected in the second and third months and dried in the shade. (In general, quotations of the *Wu Pu bencao* taken from Zhang Yuxi are not annotated; only those from the *Taiping yulan* and other books are annotated.)

The *Mingyi* says: Also called gongru, lushi and xiashi. It grows on Shaoshi and Mount Tai. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on shizhongru (stalactite).
- The note at the end of Wu Pu's quotation explains how Sun annotates the sources of Wu Pu's text.
- Sun's note (案) is not translated; it cites the *Fanzi jiran* and *Liexian zhuan*, and takes 鍾 as a loan character for 潼.
