# 神農本草經 上經 005 涅石 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Nieshi (alum) (the old editions write fanshi [banseki]; according to Guo Pu's note, the *Shanhai jing* quotes it as nieshi). Sour, cold. Governs cold and heat with diarrhea, white discharge and genital erosion, malign sores and eye pain. It firms the sinews, bones and teeth. Refined and taken as food, it lightens the body, prevents aging and adds years. Also called yunie. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Fanshi, also called yunie or yuze. Shennong, Qibo: sour; Bian Que: salty; Leigong: sour, non-toxic. It grows in Hexi, or Longxi, or Wudu and Shimen; collected at no fixed time. Qibo: taken long, it harms the bones (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called yuze. It grows in Hexi and in Longxi, Wudu and Shimen. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on nieshi (fanshi, an alum mineral). Sun changed the name to nieshi on the basis of old texts.
- 白沃 is understood as a white vaginal discharge.
- Sun's note (案) is not translated; it notes that the *Shuowen* lacks the character 礬 and cites the nieshi of the *Shanhai jing*.
