# 神農本草經 上經 006 硝石 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiaoshi (saltpetre). Bitter, cold. Governs accumulated heat in the five zang and distension and blockage of the stomach. It flushes out stored and bound food and drink, pushes out the old and brings in the new, and dispels pathogenic qi. When refined it becomes like paste. Taken long, it lightens the body (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called mangxiao (bosho)"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Xiaoshi. Shennong: bitter; Bian Que: sweet. (What comes from Zhang Yuxi's quotations and is also found in the *Taiping yulan* is not given a source.)

The *Mingyi* says: Also called mangxiao. It grows in Yizhou and in Wudu, Longxi and Xiqiang. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on xiaoshi (saltpetre).
