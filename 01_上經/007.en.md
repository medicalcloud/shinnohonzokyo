# 神農本草經 上經 007 朴硝 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Poxiao (bokusho; crude mirabilite). Bitter, cold. Governs the hundred illnesses. It dispels cold and heat and pathogenic qi, and expels accumulations in the six fu and bound, fixed, lodged aggregations. It can transform seventy-two kinds of stone. Refined and taken as food, it lightens the body and makes one an immortal. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Poxiaoshi. Shennong, Qibo, Leigong: non-toxic. It grows in Yizhou or Shanyin. Put in the earth, it does not change for a thousand years. If refining does not succeed, it must not be taken (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called xiaoshipu. It grows on the sunny side of salt waters in Yizhou. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes
