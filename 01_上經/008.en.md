# 神農本草經 上經 008 滑石 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huashi (kasseki; talc). Sweet, cold. Governs body heat with diarrhea, difficult childbirth in women, and dribbling urinary blockage. It frees urination, washes out accumulations and cold and heat in the stomach, and augments the essential qi. Taken long, it lightens the body, lets one endure hunger and lengthens the years. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called yeshi, gongshi, tuoshi and fanshi. It grows in Zheyang and on the shady side of Mount Tai, or on Mount Bai north of Ye, or on Mount Juan. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on huashi (talc).
- 乳難 is understood as difficult childbirth.
- 白山山 in the *Mingyi* quotation seems to repeat a character; it is translated as Mount Bai (not verified).
- Sun's note (案) is not translated.
