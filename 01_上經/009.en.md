# 神農本草經 上經 009 石胆 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shidan (chalcanthite). Sour, cold. Governs brightening the eyes and eye pain, metal wounds, the various epilepsies and convulsions, painful genital erosion in women, stone strangury with cold and heat, flooding and bleeding, and the various evil and toxic qi; it causes one to have children. Refined and taken as food, one does not age; taken long, it increases longevity and makes one an immortal. It can transform iron into copper and produce gold and silver (the *Taiping yulan* quotes 合成). Also called bishi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shidan. Shennong: sour, slightly cold; Mr Li: slightly cold; Tongjun: acrid, toxic; Bian Que: bitter, non-toxic (the *Taiping yulan* quotes: "Also called heishi or tongle. It grows in Qiangdao or [a place] called Qingshan. Collected on the gengzi and xinchou days of the second month").

The *Mingyi* says: Also called heishi, qishi and tongle. It grows in Qiangdao, Qiangli and Mount Gouqing. Collected on the gengzi and xinchou days of the second month.

## Notes

The entry on shidan (chalcanthite, copper sulfate).
- 瘐子 in Wu Pu's and the *Mingyi* quotations is taken as an error for the cyclical day 庚子 (the base is kept).
