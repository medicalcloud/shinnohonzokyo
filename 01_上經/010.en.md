# 神農本草經 上經 010 空青 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kongqing (hollow azurite). Sweet, cold. Governs clear-eyed blindness and deafness. It brightens the eyes, frees the nine orifices, opens the blood vessels and nourishes the essence and spirit. Taken long, it lightens the body, prolongs the years and prevents ageing. It can transform copper, iron, lead and tin into gold. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Kongqing. Shennong: sweet. One classic: sour. Taken long, immortals and jade maidens come and it raises a person's aspirations (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Yizhou and in the mountains of Yuexi where there is copper; when the essence of copper steams, kongqing is produced, and it is hollow inside. Collected in the middle of the third month, or at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on kongqing (hollow azurite).
- 眚盲 in the base is emended to 青盲 ("clear-eyed blindness"), as in the Executive Yuan text and the *Mingyi bielu* compilation; it is understood as loss of sight without visible change in the eye.
