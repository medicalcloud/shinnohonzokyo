# 神農本草經 上經 011 曽青 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cengqing (layered azurite). Sour, slightly cold. Governs eye pain; it stops tears, expels wind impediment, frees the joints, opens the nine orifices and breaks hard concretions and accumulations. Taken long, it lightens the body and prevents ageing. It can transform gold and copper. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Shu and Yuexi. Collected at no fixed time.
