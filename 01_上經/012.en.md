# 神農本草經 上經 012 禹餘糧 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuyuliang (limonite). Sweet, cold. Governs cough with counterflow, cold and heat with vexation and fullness, discharge of red and white (the *Taiping yulan* has the character 痢 "dysentery"), blood blockage with concretions, and great heat. Refined and taken as food, one is not hungry, the body is lightened and the years are prolonged. It grows in pools and marshes and on mountainous islands.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called baiyuliang. It grows by the Eastern Sea and in pools and marshes.

## Notes

The entry on yuyuliang (limonite).
- 下赤白 is understood as red and white dysentery (the *Taiping yulan* reads 下痢赤白).
