# 神農本草經 上經 013 太乙餘糧 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyi yuliang. Sweet, neutral. Governs cough with counterflow and rising qi, concretions, blood blockage and metrostaxis, and dispels pathogenic qi. Taken long, one endures cold and heat, is not hungry, the body is lightened, one flies a thousand li and becomes an immortal (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "like an immortal"). Also called shinao. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Taiyi yuyuliang, also called yuai. Shennong, Qibo, Leigong: sweet, neutral; Mr Li: slightly cold; Bian Que: sweet, non-toxic. It grows on Mount Tai. It has a shell; inside the shell there is white matter, and inside the white matter yellow matter, the color of egg yolk. Collected in the ninth month, or at no fixed time.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Taibai. Collected in the ninth month.

## Notes

The entry on taiyi yuliang, a mineral of the same kind as yuyuliang.
- The base heading 太乙餘食 is emended to 太乙餘糧, and 餘邪気 in the text to 除邪気 ("dispels pathogenic qi") (see the emendations).
