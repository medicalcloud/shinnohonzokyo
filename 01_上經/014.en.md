# 神農本草經 上經 014 白石英 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baishiying (white quartz). Sweet, slightly warm. Governs dispersion-thirst, impotence and insufficiency, cough with counterflow (the *Taiping yulan* quotes 嘔 "retching"), and long-standing cold in the chest and diaphragm; it augments qi and dispels wind-damp impediment (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "yin-excess impediment"). Taken long, it lightens the body (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "the body is light and strong") and lengthens the years. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Baishiying. Shennong: sweet; Qibo, Huangdi, Leigong, Bian Que: non-toxic. It grows on Mount Tai. In form it is like zishiying, white and lustrous, the long pieces two or three cun. Collected at no fixed time (the *Taiping yulan* quotes: "taken long, one is in communication with the light of sun and moon").

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Huayin and on Mount Tai.
