# 神農本草經 上經 015 紫石英 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zishiying (amethyst). Sweet, warm. Governs cough with counterflow from the heart and abdomen (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "retching counterflow") and evil qi, and supplements insufficiency; [it governs] wind-cold in the uterus of women, who have ceased to conceive and been childless for ten years. Taken long, it warms the centre, lightens the body and prolongs the years. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Zishiying. Shennong, Bian Que: sweet, neutral; Mr Li: very cold; Leigong: very warm; Qibo: sweet, non-toxic. It grows on Mount Tai or at Kuaiji. Collected at no fixed time. It should be as if whittled, with the purple colour reaching the tip, like a chupu die.

It also says: Qingshiying is like baishiying in form, blue at the tip and red behind. Chishiying is like baishiying in form, red at the tip and white behind; it is red, lustrous and shining, bitter in flavour, and supplements the heart qi. Huangshiying is like baishiying in form, yellow like gold, with a red tip. Heishiying is like baishiying in form, black, lustrous and shining (the *Taiping yulan* and Zhang Yuxi quote this passage).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Tai. Collected at no fixed time.
