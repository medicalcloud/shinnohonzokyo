# 神農本草經 上經 017 白青 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baiqing. Sweet, neutral. Governs brightening the eyes; it frees the nine orifices, [governs] deafness and pathogenic qi below the heart, makes one vomit, and kills the various toxins and the three worms. Taken long, it gives access to the spirit light, lightens the body, prolongs the years and prevents aging. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shennong: sweet, neutral; Leigong: sour, non-toxic. It grows in Yuzhang. It can be dissolved and made into copper (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Yuzhang. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on baiqing, said to be a copper mineral, though its identification is uncertain.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 生山谷中 (variant).
