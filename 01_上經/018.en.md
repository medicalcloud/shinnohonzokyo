# 神農本草經 上經 018 扁青 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bianqing (flat azurite). Sweet, neutral. Governs eye pain and brightens the eyes; [governs] fractures from falls, welling-abscesses and swellings and metal wounds that do not heal; it breaks accumulations, resolves toxic qi (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "wards off toxins") and benefits the essence and spirit. Taken long, it lightens the body and prevents ageing. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Bianqing. Shennong, Leigong: slightly cold, non-toxic. It grows in Shu Commandery. It treats internal exhaustion in men and causes one to have children (the *Taiping yulan* quotes: "treats welling-abscesses and spleen wind impediment; taken long, it lightens the body").

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Zhuya, Wudu and Zhuti. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on bianqing (flat azurite).
- 金創不療 in the base is emended to 金創不瘳 ("metal wounds that do not heal"), as in the Executive Yuan text and the *Xinxiu bencao*.
