# 神農本草經 上經 021 人参 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen (ninjin; ginseng). Sweet, slightly cold. Governs supplementing the five zang; it calms the essence and spirit, settles the hun and po, stops fright palpitations, dispels pathogenic qi, brightens the eyes, opens the heart and augments the intellect. Taken long, it lightens the body and prolongs the years. Also called renxian or guigai. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Renshen, also called tujing, shencao, huangshen, xueshen, renwei or yujing. Shennong: sweet, slightly cold; Tongjun, Leigong: bitter; Qibo, Huangdi: sweet, non-toxic; Bian Que: toxic. It grows in Handan. It puts out leaves in the third month, small and pointed; the seeds are black and the stem is hairy. The root is collected in the third and ninth months. The root has a head, feet and hands, and a face like a person (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called shencao, renwei, tujing or xueshen. That shaped like a person has spiritual power. It grows in Shangdang and Liaodong. The root is collected in the first ten days of the second, fourth and eighth months, scraped with a bamboo knife and dried in the sun without exposure to wind.

## Notes

The entry on renshen (ginseng).
- A drug widely used in the *Shanghan lun* and *Jingui yaolüe*; the Classic calls it slightly cold.
- Sun's note (案) is not translated.
