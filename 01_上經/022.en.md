# 神農本草經 上經 022 天門冬 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tianmendong (asparagus root). Bitter, neutral. Governs one-sided impediment from the various violent winds and damp. It strengthens the bones and marrow, kills the three worms and removes hidden corpse [disease]. Taken long, it lightens the body, augments qi and prolongs the years. Also called dianle (a quotation in the commentary on the *Erya* says "mendong, also called mandong"; this text is now lost). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Fenggao. The root is collected in the second, seventh and eighth months and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on tianmendong (asparagus root).
- "Hidden corpse" (伏屍) is understood as a latent disease of the corpse-infixation type.
