# 神農本草經 上經 024 乾地黄 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gan dihuang (kanjio; dried rehmannia). Sweet, cold. Governs fractures from falls, severed sinews and damage to the center. It drives out blood impediment, fills the bones and marrow and grows the flesh. Made into a decoction, it dispels cold and heat and accumulations and removes impediment. The fresh is even better. Taken long, it lightens the body and prevents ageing. Also called disui. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called hu or qi. That growing on the yellow earth of Xianyang is best. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on gan dihuang (dried rehmannia).
- 作湯 and what follows are read as describing its effects when made into a decoction.
- 二月八日 in the *Mingyi* quotation is taken, from other sources, to mean "the second and eighth months".
