# 神農本草經 上經 026 菟糸子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tusizi (dodder seed). Acrid, neutral. Governs joining severed injuries; it supplements insufficiency, augments vigour and makes one plump and strong. Its juice removes dark spots on the face. Taken long, it brightens the eyes, lightens the body and prolongs the years. Also called tulu. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Tusi, also called yunü, songluo, niaoluo, yaluo, fushi or chiwang. It grows in mountain valleys (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called tulü, tangmeng, yunü, chiwang or tulei. It grows in the fields of Chaoxian, spreading over grasses and trees. That which is yellow and fine is chiwang; that which is pale and large is tulei. The seeds are collected in the ninth month and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on tusizi (dodder seed).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 免糸子 and 兎芦 (variants).
