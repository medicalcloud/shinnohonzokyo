# 神農本草經 上經 027 牛膝 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Niuxi (achyranthes). Bitter, sour (the *Taiping yulan* reads acrid). Governs wilting and impediment from cold (the *Taiping yulan* reads "cold damage") and damp, hypertonicity of the four limbs, and painful knees that cannot bend or stretch. It drives out blood and qi, [treats] burns from heat and fire, and causes abortion. Taken long, it lightens the body and lets one withstand ageing (the *Taiping yulan* reads 能老). Also called baibei. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Niuxi. Shennong: sweet; one classic: sour; Huangdi, Bian Que: sweet; Mr Li: warm; Leigong: sour, non-toxic. It grows in Henei or Linqiong. The leaves are like summer indigo; the base of the stem is red. Collected in the second and eighth months (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Henei and Linqu. The root is collected in the second, eighth and tenth months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on niuxi (achyranthes).
- Since it "causes abortion", it is considered contraindicated in pregnancy.
