# 神農本草經 上經 028 充蔚子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chongweizi (motherwort seed). Acrid, slightly warm. Governs brightening the eyes and augmenting essence, and dispels water qi. Taken long, it lightens the body. The stem governs itching wheals and can be made into a bath. Also called yimu, yiming or dazha. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called zhenwei. It grows by the seashore. Collected in the fifth month.

## Notes

The entry on chongweizi (motherwort seed), also called yimu (yimucao).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 癮𤺋 (variant).
