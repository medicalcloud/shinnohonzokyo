# 神農本草經 上經 029 女萎 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Nüwei (Solomon's seal). Sweet, neutral. Governs wind-stroke with violent heat and inability to move, slack sinews and knotted flesh, and the various insufficiencies. Taken long, it removes black spots on the face, improves and moistens the complexion, lightens the body and prevents ageing. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Nüwei, also called weirui, yuma, dijie, chongchan, wuwei, ying or yuzhu. Shennong: bitter; one classic: sweet; Tongjun, Leigong, Bian Que: sweet, non-toxic; Huangdi: acrid. It grows in the mountain valleys of Mount Tai. The leaves face each other, blue and yellow, like ginger. Collected in the second and seventh months. It treats wind-stroke with violent heat. Taken long, it lightens the body (*Taiping yulan*). Also called zuomian. Taken long, it lightens the body and lets one withstand ageing (ibid.).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called ying, dijie, yuzhu or maxun. It grows on Mount Tai and in hills. Collected after the Beginning of Spring and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on nüwei, generally identified with weirui (yuzhu, Polygonatum).
- 跌筋結肉 is understood as slack sinews and knotted flesh, but this is not certain.
