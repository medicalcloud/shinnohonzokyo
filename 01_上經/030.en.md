# 神農本草經 上經 030 防葵 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fangkui. Acrid, cold. Governs mounting-conglomeration and intestinal diarrhoea, heat bind in the bladder with urine not coming down, cough with counterflow, warm malaria, epilepsy, and fright evil with manic running. Taken long, it firms the bones and marrow, augments qi and lightens the body. Also called ligai. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Fangkui, also called ligai, jueli, fangyuan, chencao, liru or fanggai. Shennong: acrid, slightly cold; Tongjun, Bian Que: non-toxic; Qibo, Leigong, Huangdi: bitter, non-toxic. The stem and leaves are like mallow, black-yellow above. The root forms in the second month, as large as platycodon, red and white inside. The flowers are white in the sixth month and the fruit white in the seventh and eighth months. The root is collected on the third day of the third month (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called fangci, jueli, nongguo, liru or fanggai. It grows in Linzi and on Mount Song, Mount Tai and Shaoshi. The root is collected on the third day of the third month and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on fangkui; its later identification is uncertain.
