# 神農本草經 上經 034 車前子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cheqianzi (shazenshi; plantago seed). Sweet, cold, non-toxic. Governs qi dribbling urinary blockage; it stops pain, frees the waterways and urination, and dispels damp impediment. Taken long, it lightens the body and lets one withstand ageing. Also called dangdao (the *Taiping yulan* has "also called niushe"; the *Daguan* edition reads niuyi, in black characters). It grows in plains and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called fuyi, hamayi, niuyi or shengxi. It grows on hills and slope paths in Zhending. Collected on the fifth day of the fifth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on cheqianzi (plantago seed).
- "Non-toxic" in the main text is as in Sun's edition for this entry (it is not common in other entries).
