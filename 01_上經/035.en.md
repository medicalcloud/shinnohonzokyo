# 神農本草經 上經 035 木香 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Muxiang (mokko; costus root). Acrid. Governs pathogenic qi; it wards off toxic epidemics and warm ghosts and strengthens the will. It governs dribbling (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "governs insufficiency of qi"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters). Taken long, one does not have nightmares or wake with terror (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called miqing" and "lightens the body and leads to immortality"; the *Daguan* edition has both in black characters). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called mixiang. It grows in Yongchang.

## Notes

The entry on muxiang (costus root).
- The text gives no thermal nature.
- In the parenthesis, 一名：軽身，致神仙 is taken to mean that after "also called miqing" the phrase "lightens the body and leads to immortality" is also quoted.
