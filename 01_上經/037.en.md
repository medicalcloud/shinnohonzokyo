# 神農本草經 上經 037 薏苡仁 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yiyiren (coix seed). Sweet, slightly cold. Governs tension of the sinews, hypertonicity with inability to bend and stretch, and wind-damp impediment, and brings down qi. Taken long, it lightens the body and augments qi. Its root expels the three worms. Also called jieli. It grows in plains, marshes and fields.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called wutan, qishi or gan. It grows in Zhending. The seeds are collected in the eighth month; the root at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on yiyiren (coix seed).
- 屈神 in the base is emended to 屈伸 ("bend and stretch").
