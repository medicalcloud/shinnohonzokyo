# 神農本草經 上經 038 沢瀉 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zexie (takusha; alisma). Sweet, cold. Governs wind-cold-damp impediment and difficult childbirth. It disperses water, nourishes the five zang, augments vigour and makes one plump and strong. Taken long, the ears and eyes become keen, one is not hungry, the years are prolonged, the body is lightened, the face becomes radiant, and one can walk on water. Also called shuixie, mangyu or huxie. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Runan. The root is collected in the fifth, sixth and eighth months and dried in the shade.
