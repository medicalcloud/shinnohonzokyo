# 神農本草經 上經 039 遠志 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuanzhi (onji; polygala). Bitter, warm. Governs cough with counterflow and damage to the center; it supplements insufficiency, dispels pathogenic qi, frees the nine orifices, augments wisdom, makes the ears and eyes keen, prevents forgetfulness, strengthens the will and doubles strength. Taken long, it lightens the body and prevents aging. The leaves are called xiaocao. Also called jiwan (Lu Deming's *Erya yinyi* quotes 莧), yaorao (the *Taiping yulan* writes 要繞) or xicao. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Tai and in Yuanju. The root and leaves are collected in the fourth month and dried in the shade.
