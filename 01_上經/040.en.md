# 神農本草經 上經 040 竜胆 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Longdan (ryutan; gentian). Bitter, astringent. Governs cold and heat between the bones and fright epilepsy with pathogenic qi; it joins severed injuries, settles the five zang and kills gu toxins. Taken long, it augments the intellect, prevents forgetfulness, lightens the body and lets one withstand aging. Also called lingyou. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Qiqu and Yuanju. The root is collected in the second, eighth, eleventh and twelfth months and dried in the shade.
