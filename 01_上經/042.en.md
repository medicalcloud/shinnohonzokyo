# 神農本草經 上經 042 石斛 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shihu (sekkoku; dendrobium). Sweet, neutral. Governs damage to the center; it removes impediment, brings down qi, supplements deficiency taxation and emaciation of the five zang, and strengthens yin. Taken long, it thickens the intestines and stomach, lightens the body and prolongs the years. Also called linlan (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called jinsheng"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shihu. Shennong: sweet, neutral; Bian Que: sour; Mr Li: cold (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called jinsheng, dulan or shizhu. It grows on rocks beside the water in Liu'an. The stem is collected in the seventh and eighth months and dried in the shade.
