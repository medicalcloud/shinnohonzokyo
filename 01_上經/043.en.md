# 神農本草經 上經 043 巴戟天 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bajitian (morinda root). Acrid, slightly warm. Governs great wind and evil qi and impotence; it strengthens the sinews and bones, calms the five zang, supplements the centre, increases the will and augments qi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Ba Commandery and Xiapi. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the shade.
