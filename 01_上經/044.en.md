# 神農本草經 上經 044 白英 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baiying. Sweet, cold. Governs cold and heat, the eight kinds of jaundice and dispersion-thirst; it supplements the centre and augments qi. Taken long, it lightens the body and prolongs the years. Also called gucai (the Yuan edition wrongly has it in black characters). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called baicao. It grows in Yizhou. The leaves are collected in spring, the stems in summer, the flowers in autumn and the root in winter.

## Notes

The entry on baiying; its later identification (perhaps a Solanum) is uncertain.
- "The eight kinds of jaundice" (八疽) is said to refer to eight types of jaundice, but their content is unclear.
