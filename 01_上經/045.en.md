# 神農本草經 上經 045 白蒿 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baihao (white mugwort). Sweet, neutral. Governs evil qi of the five zang and wind-cold-damp impediment; it supplements the centre and augments qi, grows the hair and makes it black, and treats a suspended heart, poor appetite and constant hunger. Taken long, it lightens the body, makes the ears and eyes keen and prevents ageing. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Zhongshan. Collected in the second month.

## Notes

The entry on baihao (white mugwort).
- 久服 is supplied in the base's 常飢。軽身 (see the emendation).
- "Suspended heart" (心懸) is understood as a sensation of hollowness, as if the heart were hanging.
