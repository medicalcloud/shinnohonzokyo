# 神農本草經 上經 046 赤箭 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chijian (gastrodia). Acrid, warm. Governs killing ghostly spectral things, gu toxins and malign qi. Taken long, it augments vigour, strengthens yin, makes one plump and strong, lightens the body and adds years. Also called limu or guiduyou. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Huanduyou, also called shencao or yangou. It grows on Mount Tai or Shaoshi. The stem is red like an arrow and leafless; the root is like taro corms. The root is collected in the third, fourth and eighth months and dried in the sun. It treats welling-abscesses and swellings (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Chencang and Yongzhou and on Mount Tai and Shaoshi. The root is collected in the third, fourth and eighth months and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on chijian, generally identified with the aerial part or whole plant of tianma (gastrodia).
- Wu Pu's name huanduyou differs in writing from guiduyou in the main text.
