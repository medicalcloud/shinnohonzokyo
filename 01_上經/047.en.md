# 神農本草經 上經 047 奄閭子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Anlüzi (the old editions write 庵閭; the *Taiping yulan* writes 奄閭, which is correct). Bitter, slightly cold. Governs static blood in the five zang, water qi in the abdomen, distended belly with lingering heat, wind-cold-damp impediment and the various pains of the body. Taken long, it lightens the body, prolongs the years and prevents aging. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Anlü. Shennong, Leigong, Tongjun, Qibo: bitter, slightly warm, non-toxic; Mr Li: warm. It may grow in Shangdang. The leaves are blue-green and thick, in opposite pairs. The flowers are white [base: 曰] in the seventh month and the fruit black in the ninth month. Collected in the seventh, ninth and tenth months. Donkeys and horses that eat it become immortals and depart (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: When the juxu [a horse-like beast] eats it, it becomes an immortal. It grows in Yongzhou, and has spread to Shangdang and roadsides. The root is collected in the tenth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on anlüzi.
- 花曰 in Wu Pu's text is taken as an error for 花白 ("white flowers"); the base is kept.
