# 神農本草經 上經 048 析蓂子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ximingzi (thlaspi seed). Acrid, slightly warm. Governs brightening the eyes, eye pain and tearing; it removes impediment, supplements the five zang and augments the light of the essence. Taken long, it lightens the body and prevents ageing. Also called miexi, daji (daigeki) or maxin. It grows in rivers and marshes and beside roads.

The *Wu Pu* says: Ximing, also called ximu, rongming or maxing. Leigong, Shennong, Bian Que: acrid; Mr Li: slightly warm. Collected in the fourth month and dried; twenty days [to dry]. It grows beside roads. It is good with xixin (saishin); it fears dried ginger, kushen (kujin) and jishi. Shennong: non-toxic. It grows in wild fields. Collected on the fifth day of the fifth month and dried in the shade. It treats abdominal distension (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called daji. It grows in Xianyang. Collected in the fourth and fifth months and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on ximingzi.
- "Twenty days" in Wu Pu's text probably continues "collected and dried" (dried in twenty days), but this is unclear.
