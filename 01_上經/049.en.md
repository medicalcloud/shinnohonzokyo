# 神農本草經 上經 049 蓍実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shishi (yarrow seed). Bitter, neutral. Governs augmenting qi; it fills the flesh and skin, brightens the eyes, makes one keen and wise and gives foreknowledge. Taken long, one is not hungry, does not age and the body is light. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shishi is bitter and sour, neutral and non-toxic. It governs augmenting qi; it fills the flesh and skin, brightens the eyes, makes one keen and wise and gives foreknowledge. Taken long, one is not hungry, does not age and the body is light. It grows in the mountain valleys of Shaoshi. The fruit is collected in the eighth and ninth months and dried in the sun (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Shaoshi. The fruit is collected in the eighth and ninth months and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on shishi, the seed of the shi plant used in divination; "foreknowledge" is probably connected with that use.
