# 神農本草經 上經 050 赤芝 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chizhi (red ganoderma). Bitter, neutral. Governs binding in the chest; it augments the heart qi, supplements the centre, increases wisdom and prevents forgetfulness. Eaten long, it lightens the body, prevents ageing, prolongs the years and makes one an immortal. Also called danzhi.

## Notes

The entry on chizhi, the first of the six zhi (red, black, blue-green, white, yellow and purple). Their places of growth and collection times are given together in the *Mingyi* quotation in the zizhi entry.
- The six zhi are matched with the five colours and the five zang (red—heart, black—kidney, blue-green—liver, white—lung, yellow—spleen).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition repeats 智 (variant).
