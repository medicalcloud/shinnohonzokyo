# 神農本草經 上經 051 黒芝 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Heizhi (black ganoderma). Salty, neutral. Governs dribbling urinary blockage; it frees the waterways, augments the kidney qi, opens the nine orifices and makes one keen and perceptive. Eaten long, it lightens the body, prevents ageing, prolongs the years and makes one an immortal. Also called yuanzhi.

## Notes

The entry on heizhi. In the Zhongyi Jicheng text it has no heading and continues the chizhi paragraph; it is separated as a unit.
- Yuanzhi (元芝) probably replaces 玄芝 to avoid a Qing imperial taboo.
