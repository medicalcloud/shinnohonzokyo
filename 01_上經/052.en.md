# 神農本草經 上經 052 青芝 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qingzhi (seishi; blue-green ganoderma). Sour, neutral. Governs brightening the eyes; it supplements the liver qi, calms the essence and hun, and [makes one] benevolent and forgiving. Eaten long, it lightens the body, prevents ageing, prolongs the years and makes one an immortal. Also called longzhi.
