# 神農本草經 上經 053 白芝 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baizhi (white ganoderma). Acrid, neutral. Governs cough with counterflow and rising qi; it augments the lung qi, frees the mouth and nose, strengthens the will and intent, makes one brave and calms the po. Eaten long, it lightens the body, prevents ageing, prolongs the years and makes one an immortal. Also called yuzhi.
