# 神農本草經 上經 054 黄芝 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangzhi (yellow ganoderma). Sweet, neutral. Governs the five evils of the heart and abdomen; it augments the spleen qi, calms the spirit, and [makes one] loyal, trustworthy, harmonious and joyful. Eaten long, it lightens the body, prevents ageing, prolongs the years and makes one an immortal. Also called jinzhi.
