# 神農本草經 上經 056 巻柏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Juanbai (selaginella). Acrid, warm. It grows in mountain valleys. Governs pathogenic qi of the five zang, cold, heat and pain in the vagina, concretions, blood blockage and childlessness. Taken long, it lightens the body and harmonizes the complexion. Also called wansui. It grows among rocks in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Juanbai. Shennong: acrid; Tongjun, Leigong: sweet (the *Taiping yulan* quotes: "also called baozu, qiugu, wansui or shenzhishi. It grows in mountain valleys").

The *Mingyi* says: Also called baozu, qiugu or jiaoshi. It grows on Mount Chang. Collected in the fifth and seventh months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on juanbai (selaginella).
- The main text mentions "grows in mountain valleys" twice (as in the base).
