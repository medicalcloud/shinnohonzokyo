# 神農本草經 上經 057 藍実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lanshi (indigo seed). Bitter, cold. Governs resolving the various toxins; it kills gu and crawling vermin, infixation ghosts and sting toxins. Taken long, the hair does not turn white and the body is light. It grows in plains and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Its stems and leaves can dye blue. It grows in Henei.
