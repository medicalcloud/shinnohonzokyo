# 神農本草經 上經 059 蘼蕪 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Miwu (ligusticum sprouts). Acrid, warm. Governs cough with counterflow; it settles fright qi, wards off evil and malign influences, dispels gu toxins and ghost infixation and removes the three worms. Taken long, it gives access to the spirits. Also called weiwu. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Miwu, also called xiongqiong (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called jiangli; it is the sprout of xiongqiong. It grows in Yongzhou and Yuanju. The leaves are collected in the fourth and fifth months and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on miwu, the sprouts of xiongqiong.
