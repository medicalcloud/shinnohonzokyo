# 神農本草經 上經 060 黄連 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huanglian (coptis). Bitter, cold. Governs eye pain from heat qi and injury to the canthus with tearing, and brightens the eyes (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "governs injury to the stalk"; not in the *Daguan* edition); [it governs] intestinal afflux, abdominal pain with diarrhoea, and swelling and pain of the vagina. Taken long, it keeps one from forgetting. Also called wanglian. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Huanglian. Shennong, Qibo, Huangdi, Leigong: bitter, non-toxic; Mr Li: slightly cold. It may grow in Shu Commandery and on the sunny side of Mount Tai (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Wuyang, Shu Commandery and Mount Tai. Collected in the second and eighth months.
