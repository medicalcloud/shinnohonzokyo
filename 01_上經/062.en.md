# 神農本草經 上經 062 蒺藜子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jilizi (shitsurishi; tribulus fruit). Bitter, warm. Governs malign blood; it breaks bound concretions and accumulations, [and governs] throat impediment and difficult childbirth. Taken long, it grows the flesh, brightens the eyes and lightens the body. Also called pangtong, quren, zhixing, chaiyu or shengtui (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called junshuixiang"; not in the *Daguan* edition). It grows in plains and marshes or beside roads.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called jili or ci. It grows in Pingyi. The fruit is collected in the seventh and eighth months and dried in the sun.
