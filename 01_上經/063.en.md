# 神農本草經 上經 063 黄耆 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangqi (ogi; astragalus). Sweet, slightly warm. Governs welling- and flat-abscesses and long-standing ruined sores; it expels pus and stops pain. [It governs] great-wind lai disease, the five kinds of hemorrhoids and scrofulous fistulas. It supplements deficiency. [It governs] the hundred illnesses of children. Also called daishen. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called daishen, dushen, jicao, shuzhi or baiben. It grows in Baishui of Shu Commandery and Hanzhong. Collected in the second and tenth months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on huangqi (astragalus).
- 大風痢疾 in the base is emended to 大風癩疾 (great-wind lai disease, a severe skin disease including leprosy).
