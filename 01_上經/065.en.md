# 神農本草經 上經 065 防風 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia). Sweet, warm, non-toxic. Governs great wind, dizziness and headache, aversion to wind and pathogenic wind, blindness with nothing seen, wind travelling through the whole body, aching impediment of the joints (the *Taiping yulan* reads "pain"), and vexation and fullness. Taken long, it lightens the body. Also called tongyun (the *Taiping yulan* reads 芒). It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Fangfeng, also called huiyun, huicao, baizhi, jiangen, baijiu or baizhong. Shennong, Huangdi, Qibo, Tongjun, Leigong, Bian Que: sweet, non-toxic; Mr Li: slightly cold. It may grow in Handan and Shangcai. The leaves appear in the first month, fine and round, bluish-black, yellow or white; the flowers are yellow in the fifth month and the fruit black in the sixth month. The root is collected in the third and tenth months and dried in the sun; that from Langye is good (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called huicao, baizhi, pingfeng, jiangen or baifei. It grows in Shayuan and in Handan, Langye and Shangcai. The root is collected in the second and tenth months and dried in the sun.
