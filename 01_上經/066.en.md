# 神農本草經 上經 066 蒲黄 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Puhuang (hoo; typha pollen). Sweet, neutral. Governs cold and heat of the heart, abdomen and bladder; it frees urination, stops bleeding and disperses static blood. Taken long, it lightens the body, augments vigour, prolongs the years and makes one an immortal. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hedong. Collected in the fourth month.
