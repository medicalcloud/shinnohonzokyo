# 神農本草經 上經 067 香蒲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiangpu (koho; cattail). Sweet, neutral. Governs pathogenic qi of the five zang and below the heart and putrid odor in the mouth; it firms the teeth, brightens the eyes and sharpens the ears. Taken long, it lightens the body and lets one withstand ageing (the *Taiping yulan* reads 能老). Also called sui (the *Taiping yulan* says suipu). It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Sui, also called suishi or xiangpu. Shennong, Leigong: sweet. It grows in the pools and marshes of Nanhai (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called jiao. It grows in Nanhai.
