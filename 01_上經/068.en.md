# 神農本草經 上經 068 続断 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xuduan (dipsacus). Bitter, slightly warm. Governs cold damage; it supplements insufficiency, [governs] metal wounds and abscess injuries and fractures from falls, and joins the sinews and bones. [It governs] difficult childbirth (the *Taiping yulan* reads "breast abscess" and has "flooding and spotting of blood"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters). Taken long, it augments vigour. Also called longdou or shuzhe. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called jiegu, nancao or huai. It grows on Mount Chang. Collected in the seventh and eighth months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on xuduan (dipsacus), regarded, as its name ("joining the broken") suggests, as a drug for fractures and injured sinews.
