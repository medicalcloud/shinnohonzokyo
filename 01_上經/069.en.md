# 神農本草經 上經 069 漏芦 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Loulu (rhaponticum). Bitter, salty, cold. Governs heat of the skin, malign sores, flat-abscesses and haemorrhoids and damp impediment, and brings down breast milk. Taken long, it lightens the body and augments qi, makes the ears and eyes keen, prevents ageing and prolongs the years. Also called yelan. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Qiao. The root is collected in the eighth month and dried in the shade.
