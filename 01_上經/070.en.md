# 神農本草經 上經 070 営実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yingshi (multiflora rose hip). Sour, warm. Governs welling- and flat-abscesses and malign sores, knotted flesh and slack sinews, ruined sores, heat qi, and genital erosion that does not heal, and frees the joints. Also called qiangwei, qiangma or niuji. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Qiangwei, also called niule, niuxi, qiangwei or shanzao (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called niule, qiangmi or shanji. It grows in Lingling and Shu Commandery. Collected in the eighth and ninth months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on yingshi (rose hip).
- 陰蝕不療 in the base is emended to 陰蝕不瘳 ("genital erosion that does not heal").
