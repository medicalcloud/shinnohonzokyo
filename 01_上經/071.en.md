# 神農本草經 上經 071 天名精 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tianmingjing (tenmeisei; carpesium). Sweet, cold. Governs static blood, blood conglomeration with near death, and bleeding; it stops bleeding and frees urination. Taken long, it lightens the body and lets one withstand ageing. Also called maijujiang, hamalan or shishou. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called tianmenjing, yumenjing, zhilu, chanchulan or jin. It grows on plains. Collected in the fifth month.
