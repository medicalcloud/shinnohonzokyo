# 神農本草經 上經 072 決明子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Juemingzi (ketsumeishi; cassia seed). Salty, neutral. Governs clear-eyed blindness, excessive eye discharge, red skin [of the eye], white membranes, red and painful eyes and tearing. Taken long, it augments the light of the essence (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "regulates the essence of the eyeball"; 理 stands for 治) and lightens the body. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Juemingzi, also called cao jueming or Yangming (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows at Longmen. Shi jueming grows in Yuzhang. Collected in the tenth month and dried in the shade for a hundred days.

## Notes

The entry on juemingzi (cassia seed).
- "理 stands for 治" refers to the avoidance of the Tang emperor Gaozong's personal name 治.
