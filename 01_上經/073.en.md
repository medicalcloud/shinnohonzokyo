# 神農本草經 上經 073 丹参 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Danshen (salvia root). Bitter, slightly cold. Governs evil qi of the heart and abdomen, rumbling intestines gurgling like running water, and cold and heat with accumulations; it breaks concretions and removes conglomerations, stops vexation and fullness and augments qi. Also called quechancao. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Danshen, also called chishen, muyangru or quechancao. Shennong, Tongjun, Huangdi, Leigong, Bian Que: bitter, non-toxic; Mr Li: slightly cold; Qibo: salty. It grows on Tongbai or on the shady side of hills of Mount Tai. The stem and flowers are small and square like perilla, hairy; the root is red. The flowers are purple in the fourth month; the root is collected in the fifth month and dried in the shade. It treats pain of the heart and abdomen (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called chishen or muyangru. It grows on Mount Tongbai and Mount Tai. The root is collected in the fifth month and dried in the sun.
