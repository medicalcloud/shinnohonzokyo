# 神農本草經 上經 074 茜根 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qiangen (madder root). Bitter, cold. Governs cold-damp, wind impediment and jaundice, and supplements the centre. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It can dye deep red. Also called dixue, rulü, maosou or qian. It grows on Mount Qiao. The root is collected in the second and third months and dried in the shade.
