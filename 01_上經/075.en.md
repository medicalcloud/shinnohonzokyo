# 神農本草經 上經 075 飛廉 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Feilian (plumeless thistle). Bitter, neutral. Governs heat of the bones and joints and heaviness, soreness and pain of the shins. Taken long, it makes the body light. Also called feiqing (the four characters above are in black characters in the original edition). It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called futu, feizhi or muhe. It grows in Henei. The root is collected in the first month and the flowers in the seventh and eighth months; dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on feilian.
- 已以上四字 in the parenthesis is read as "the four characters above" (一名飛軽).
