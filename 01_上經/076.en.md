# 神農本草經 上經 076 五味子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuweizi (schisandra). Sour, warm. Governs augmenting qi; [it governs] cough with counterflow and rising qi, taxation damage and emaciation. It supplements insufficiency, strengthens yin and augments the semen of men (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called huiji"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Wuweizi, also called yuanji (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called huiji or yuanji. It grows on Mount Qi and in Dai Commandery. The fruit is collected in the eighth month and dried in the shade.
