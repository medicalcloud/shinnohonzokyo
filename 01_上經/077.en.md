# 神農本草經 上經 077 旋華 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xuanhua (bindweed flower). Sweet, warm. Governs augmenting qi; it removes dark spots (the *Taiping yulan* reads 䵟) and blackness of the face and makes the complexion attractive (the *Taiping yulan* reads "makes one's complexion pleasing and glossy"). Its root is acrid; it governs cold and heat and evil qi in the abdomen and frees urination. Taken long, one is not hungry and the body is light. Also called jingenhua or jinfei (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called meicao"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters). It grows in plains and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Yuzhou. Collected in the fifth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on xuanhua (bindweed flower), a different drug from xuanfuhua in the Lower Classic.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 旋覆花 (variant).
