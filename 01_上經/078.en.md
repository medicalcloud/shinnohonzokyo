# 神農本草經 上經 078 蘭草 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lancao (ranso; thoroughwort). Acrid, neutral. Governs freeing the waterways; it kills gu toxins and wards off inauspicious influences. Taken long, it augments qi, lightens the body, prevents aging and gives access to the spirit light. Also called shuixiang. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Dawu. Collected in the fourth and fifth months.
