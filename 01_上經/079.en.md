# 神農本草經 上經 079 蛇床子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shechuangzi (cnidium seed). Bitter, neutral. Governs swelling and pain in the vagina, and impotence with damp itching in men; it removes impediment qi, frees the joints, and [governs] epilepsy and malign sores. Taken long, it lightens the body. Also called shemi. It grows in river valleys and fields.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shechuang, also called shezhu (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called shesu, huichuang, siyan, shengdu, zaoji or qiangmi. It grows in Linzi. The seeds are collected in the fifth month and dried in the shade.
