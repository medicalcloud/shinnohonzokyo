# 神農本草經 上經 080 地膚子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Difuzi (kochia seed). Bitter, cold. Governs heat in the bladder; it frees urination, supplements the centre and augments the essential qi. Taken long, it makes the ears and eyes keen, lightens the body and lets one withstand ageing. Also called dikui (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called dihua or dimai"; the *Daguan* edition lacks the four characters "also called dihua" and writes 脈 as 麦, all in black characters). It grows in plains, marshes and fields.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called dimai. It grows in Jingzhou. The seeds are collected in the eighth and tenth months and dried in the shade.
