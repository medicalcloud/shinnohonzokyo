# 神農本草經 上經 081 景天 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jingtian (stonecrop). Bitter, neutral. Governs great heat, burns, body heat with vexation, and evil and malign qi. The flower governs metrostaxis with red and white discharge in women; it lightens the body and brightens the eyes. Also called jiehuo or shenhuo (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called shuimu"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters with 水 written 火). It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called huomu, jiuhuo or juhuo. It grows on Mount Tai. Collected on the fourth day of the fourth month and the seventh day of the seventh month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on jingtian (stonecrop), regarded as a drug for burns, hence names such as "guarding against fire".
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 華 (variant).
