# 神農本草經 上經 082 茵陳 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yinchen (capillary wormwood) (the *Taiping yulan* reads 茵蒿). Bitter, neutral. Governs wind-damp, cold and heat and pathogenic qi, heat bind and jaundice. Taken long, it lightens the body, augments qi and lets one withstand ageing (the *Taiping yulan* reads 能老). It grows on hills, slopes and banks.

The *Wu Pu* says: Yinchen. Shennong, Qibo, Leigong: bitter, non-toxic; Huangdi: acrid, non-toxic. It grows in fields; the leaves are like indigo. Collected in the eleventh month (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: A white hare ate it and became an immortal. It grows on Mount Tai. Collected in the fifth month and at the Beginning of Autumn and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on yinchen (yinchenhao), known as a drug for jaundice.
