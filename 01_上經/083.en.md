# 神農本草經 上經 083 杜若 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Duruo. Acrid, slightly warm. Governs counterflow qi below the chest and ribs and warms the centre; [it governs] wind entering the Brain's Door with swelling and pain of the head, much snivel and tearing. Taken long, it augments essence (the *Yiwen leiju* quotes "augments qi"), brightens the eyes and lightens the body. Also called duheng (the *Yiwen leiju* quotes 蘅, which is wrong). It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called dulian, bailian, bailing or ruozhi. It grows in Wuling and Yuanju. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on duruo, whose identification is uncertain.
- "Brain's Door" (腦戸) is a site at the back of the head (also an acupoint name).
