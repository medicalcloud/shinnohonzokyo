# 神農本草經 上經 085 白兎藿 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baituhuo. Bitter, neutral. Governs [bites of] snakes and vipers, bees and scorpions and rabid dogs, [poisoning from] vegetables and meat, gu toxins and infixation. Also called baige. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Baituhuo, also called baigegu (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Jiaozhou.

## Notes

The entry on baituhuo, regarded as an antidote for bites and poisons; its identification is uncertain.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 猘狗 (variant).
