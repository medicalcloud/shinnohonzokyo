# 神農本草經 上經 086 徐長卿 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xuchangqing (paniculate swallowwort). Acrid, warm. Governs ghostly things and the hundred spectres, gu toxins, epidemics, pathogen and malign qi and warm malaria. Taken long, it makes one strong and bold and lightens the body. Also called guiduyou. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Xuchangqing, also called shixia changqing. Shennong, Leigong: acrid. It may grow in Longxi. Collected in the third month (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Tai and in Longxi. Collected in the third month.
