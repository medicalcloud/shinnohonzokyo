# 神農本草經 上經 087 石竜芻 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shilongchu (rush). Bitter, slightly cold. Governs pathogenic qi of the heart and abdomen, inhibited urination, strangury and blockage, wind-damp, ghost infixation and malign toxins. Taken long, it supplements deficiency and emaciation, lightens the body, makes the ears and eyes keen and prolongs the years. Also called longxu, cao xuduan or longzhu. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Longchu, also called longduo, longxu, xuduan, longben, caodu, longhua or xuanguan. Shennong, Mr Li: slightly cold; Leigong, Huangdi: bitter, non-toxic; Bian Que: acrid, non-toxic. It grows in Liangzhou. Collected on the seventh day of the seventh month (the *Taiping yulan* wrongly attaches this entry to xuduan).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called longhua, xuanguan or caodu. It grows in damp ground in Liangzhou. The stems are collected in the fifth and seventh months and dried in the sun.
