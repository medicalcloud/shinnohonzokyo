# 神農本草經 上經 088 薇銜 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Weixian. Bitter, neutral. Governs wind-damp impediment, pain running through the joints, fright epilepsy, protruding tongue, palpitating qi, bandit wind, scrofulous fistulas and welling-abscesses and swellings. Also called mixian. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Weixian, also called mibi, wudian, chenggao, chou or wuxin (*Taiping yulan*). The *Mingyi* says: Also called chenggao, chengji, wuxin or wudian. It grows in Hanzhong, Yuanju and Handan. The stems and leaves are collected in the seventh month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on weixian; its identification is uncertain.
- In the base, the *Wu Pu* and *Mingyi* quotations stand in the same paragraph.
