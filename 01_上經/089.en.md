# 神農本草經 上經 089 雲実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yunshi (caesalpinia seed). Acrid, warm. Governs diarrhea (the old editions write 痢; the *Taiping yulan* writes 泄利) and intestinal afflux; it kills worms and gu toxins, removes evil and malign bound qi, stops pain and dispels heat. Its flower [base: 平] governs seeing ghostly spectral things; eaten in quantity, it makes one run about madly. Taken long, it lightens the body and gives access to the spirit light. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Yunshi, also called yuanshi or tiandou. Shennong: acrid, slightly warm; Huangdi: salty; Leigong: bitter. The leaves are like hemp, in opposite pairs; it is four or five chi tall, with large hollow stems. Flowers in the sixth month, fruit in the eighth and ninth months, collected in the tenth month (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called yuanshi, yunying or tiandou. It grows in Hejian. Collected in the tenth month and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on yunshi (Caesalpinia seed).
- 平 in 平，主見鬼精物 is read as "flower", since the Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 華 and the *Zhenglei bencao* also attributes ghost-seeing (hallucination) to the flower (the base is kept).
