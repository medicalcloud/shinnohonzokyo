# 神農本草經 上經 090 王不留行 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wangbuliuxing (ofurugyo; vaccaria seed). Bitter, neutral. Governs metal wounds; it stops bleeding and drives out pain, draws out thorns, and dispels wind impediment and internal cold. Taken long, it lightens the body, lets one withstand ageing (the *Taiping yulan* reads 能老) and increases longevity. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Wangbuliuxing, also called wangbuliuxing (written 流). Shennong: bitter, neutral; Qibo, Leigong: sweet. Collected in the third and eighth months (*Taiping yulan*).
