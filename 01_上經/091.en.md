# 神農本草經 上經 091 升麻 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shengma (shoma; cimicifuga). Sweet, acrid (the *Daguan* edition reads sweet, neutral). Governs resolving the hundred toxins; it kills the hundred old things and calamitous ghosts and wards off warm diseases, miasmic pathogens and toxic gu. Taken long, one does not die young (the *Daguan* edition reads: "governs resolving the hundred toxins, kills the hundred spectres, old things and calamitous ghosts, and wards off pestilence, miasmic qi, pathogenic qi and worm toxins". Here the *Taiping yulan* text is used). Also called zhou shengma (the *Daguan* edition reads zhouma). It grows in mountain valleys (formerly in black characters. Since the *Wu Pu* says "Shennong: sweet", the Classic must have contained this drug, and it is now added).

The *Wu Pu* says: Shengma. Shennong: sweet (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Yizhou. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on shengma (cimicifuga). Sun added shengma, which the *Daguan bencao* has in black (*Bielu*) characters, to the Classic on the basis of Shennong's opinion cited by Wu Pu.
- 障邪 in the base is emended to 瘴邪 ("miasmic pathogens").
