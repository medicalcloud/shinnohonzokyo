# 神農本草經 上經 092 青蘘 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qingrang (sesame sprouts). Sweet, cold. Governs pathogenic qi of the five zang and wind-cold-damp impediment; it augments qi, supplements the brain and marrow and firms the sinews and bones. Taken long, it makes the ears and eyes keen, one is not hungry, does not age and longevity increases. It is the sprout of jusheng [sesame]. It grows in river valleys (formerly in the grain section, which is wrong).

The *Wu Pu* says: Qingrang, also called mengshen. Shennong: bitter; Leigong: sweet (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Zhongyuan.

## Notes

The entry on qingrang (sesame sprouts), which Sun moved from the grains to the herbs.
