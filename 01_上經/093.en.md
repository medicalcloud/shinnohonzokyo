# 神農本草經 上經 093 姑活 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guhuo. Sweet, warm. Governs great wind and pathogenic qi and damp impediment with cold pain. Taken long, it lightens the body, increases longevity and lets one withstand ageing. Also called dongkuizi (tokishi; formerly in the *Tang bencao*, non-toxic; now added).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hedong.

## Notes

The entry on guhuo, one of the drugs relegated in the *Tang bencao* (*Xinxiu bencao*) to those "named but no longer used", which Sun restored to the Classic. Its identification is unknown.
