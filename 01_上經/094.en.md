# 神農本草經 上經 094 別羈 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bieji. Bitter, slightly warm. Governs wind-cold-damp impediment, heaviness of the body, aching and soreness of the four limbs and pain running through the joints from pathogenic cold. It grows in river valleys (formerly in the *Tang bencao*, non-toxic; now added).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called biezhi, bieqi or bieji (written 鼈羈). It grows in Lantian. Collected in the second and eighth months. Note: Tao Hongjing says, "Physicians sometimes used it, but now it has died out even in common use."

## Notes

The entry on bieji, another drug relegated in the *Tang bencao* and restored by Sun; its identification is unknown.
- The "Note" at the end of the *Mingyi* paragraph is Sun's; since it stands in the same paragraph in the base, it is translated.
