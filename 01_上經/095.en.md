# 神農本草經 上經 095 屈草 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qucao. Bitter. Governs pain below the chest and ribs, pathogenic qi, and cold and heat in the abdomen with yin impediment. Taken long, it lightens the body, augments qi and lets one withstand ageing (the *Taiping yulan* reads "supplements and augments, 能老"). It grows in rivers and marshes (formerly in the *Tang bencao*, non-toxic; now added).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hanzhong. Collected in the fifth month.

## Notes

The entry on qucao, another drug relegated in the *Tang bencao* and restored by Sun; its identification is unknown.
