# 神農本草經 上經 096 淮木 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huaimu. Bitter, neutral. Governs long-standing cough with rising qi, deficiency and emaciation in the intestines, genital erosion in women, and metrostaxis with red and white discharge. Also called baisui chengzhongmu ("wood from a hundred-year-old city wall"). It grows in mountain valleys (formerly in the *Tang bencao*, non-toxic; now added).

The *Wu Pu* says: Huaimu. Shennong, Leigong: non-toxic. It grows in the plains and marshes of Pingyang and Hedong in Jin. It treats long-standing cough with rising qi and damage to the center with emaciation and deficiency, and supplements the center and augments qi (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called tanmu. It grows on Mount Tai. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on huaimu, another drug relegated in the *Tang bencao* and restored by Sun; its identification is unknown.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 傷中, as does Wu Pu's text (variant); 腸中 in the base may be an error for 傷中.
