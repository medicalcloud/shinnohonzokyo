# 神農本草經 上經 097 牡桂 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mugui (bokei; cassia bark). Acrid, warm. Governs rising qi and cough with counterflow, bound qi, throat impediment with difficulty inhaling; it frees the joints, supplements the center and augments qi. Taken long, it gives access to the spirits, lightens the body and prevents aging. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Nanhai.

## Notes

The entry on mugui, the first upper-grade tree; with jungui it is a kind of gui (cinnamon/cassia).
- 吐吸 is understood as difficulty in breathing in.
- Sun's note (案) is not translated.
