# 神農本草經 上經 098 菌桂 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jungui (cassia). Acrid, warm. Governs the hundred illnesses; it nourishes the essence and spirit, harmonizes the complexion and acts as the leading envoy for the other drugs. Taken long, it lightens the body, prevents ageing, gives the face a radiant glow, and one is always as lovely as a child. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows among the cliffs of Jiaozhi and Guilin. It has no core and is perfectly round like bamboo. Collected at the Beginning of Autumn.
