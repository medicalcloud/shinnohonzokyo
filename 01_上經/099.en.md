# 神農本草經 上經 099 松脂 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Songzhi (pine resin). Bitter, warm. Governs flat-abscesses, malign sores, scalp sores, white baldness, and scabies itch and wind qi. It calms the five zang and dispels heat. Taken long, it lightens the body, prevents ageing and prolongs the years. Also called songgao or songfang. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Tai. Collected in the sixth month.
