# 神農本草經 上經 100 槐実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huaishi (sophora fruit). Bitter, cold. Governs evil qi and heat of the five internal [organs]; it stops drooling and spitting, supplements severed injuries, and [governs] the five kinds of haemorrhoids, burns, breast conglomeration in women and acute pain of the uterus. It grows in plains and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Henan.
