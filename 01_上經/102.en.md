# 神農本草經 上經 102 柏実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baishi (arborvitae seed). Sweet, neutral. Governs fright palpitations; it calms the five zang, augments qi and dispels damp impediment. Taken long, it makes one cheerful and glossy, beautifies the complexion, makes the ears and eyes keen, prevents hunger and ageing, lightens the body and prolongs the years. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Tai. The leaves of the arborvitae are even better. They are collected in each of the four seasons according to the direction and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on baishi (arborvitae seed).
- 田四時 in the *Mingyi* quotation is taken as an error for 四時 ("the four seasons"); the base is kept.
