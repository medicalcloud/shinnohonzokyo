# 神農本草經 上經 104 楡皮 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yupi (elm bark). Sweet, neutral. Governs blockage of urine and stool; it frees the waterways and dispels evil qi. Taken long, it lightens the body and prevents hunger. Its fruit is even better. Also called lingyu. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Yingchuan. The bark is collected in the third month, the white part taken and dried in the sun; the fruit is collected in the eighth month.
