# 神農本草經 上經 105 酸棗 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suanzao (sanso; sour jujube). Sour, neutral. Governs cold and heat of the heart and abdomen, bound pathogen and gathered qi, soreness and aching of the four limbs, and damp impediment. Taken long, it calms the five zang, lightens the body and prolongs the years. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hedong. The fruit is collected in the eighth month and dried in the shade; it is ready in forty days.

## Notes

The entry on suanzao (sour jujube; later the seed, suanzaoren, is used).
