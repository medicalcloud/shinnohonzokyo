# 神農本草經 上經 106 蘖木 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bomu (phellodendron bark). Bitter, cold. Governs bound heat in the five zang, intestines and stomach, jaundice and intestinal hemorrhoids, and stops diarrhea; [it governs] metrostaxis with red and white discharge in women and erosive sores of the genitals. Also called tanhuan. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hanzhong and Yongchang.

## Notes

The entry on bomu (huangbo (obaku), phellodendron bark).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 檗木 and has an extra 下 (variants).
