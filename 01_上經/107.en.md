# 神農本草經 上經 107 乾漆 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ganqi (kanshitsu; dried lacquer). Acrid, warm, non-toxic. Governs severed injuries; it supplements the center, joins the sinews and bones, fills the marrow and brain, calms the five zang, and [governs] the five slacknesses and six tensions and wind-cold-damp impediment. Fresh lacquer removes long worms. Taken long, it lightens the body and lets one withstand aging. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hanzhong. Collected after the Summer Solstice and dried.

## Notes

The entry on ganqi (dried lacquer).
