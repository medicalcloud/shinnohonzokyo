# 神農本草經 上經 108 五加皮 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wujiapi (gokahi; acanthopanax bark). Acrid, warm. Governs mounting qi of the heart and abdomen and abdominal pain; it augments qi, treats lameness, and [governs] children who cannot walk, jaundiced sores and genital erosion. Also called chaiqi.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called chaijie. It grows in Hanzhong and Yuanju. The stems are collected in the fifth and tenth months and the root in the tenth month; dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on wujiapi (acanthopanax bark).
- 孕辛 in the base is emended to 味辛 ("acrid in flavor").
- The main text has no clause on where it grows. The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 疽創 (variant).
