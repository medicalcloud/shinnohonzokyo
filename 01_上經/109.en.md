# 神農本草經 上經 109 蔓荊実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Manjingshi (vitex fruit). Bitter, slightly cold. Governs cold, heat and impediment between the sinews and bones and hypertonicity; it brightens the eyes, firms the teeth, frees the nine orifices and removes white worms. Taken long, it lightens the body and lets one withstand aging. Xiaojingshi is the same. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hejian, Nanyang and Yuanju, or on high banks in Duxiang of Pingshou and in fields. The fruit is collected in the eighth and ninth months and dried in the shade.
