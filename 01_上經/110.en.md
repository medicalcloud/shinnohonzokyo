# 神農本草經 上經 110 辛夷 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xinyi (shini; magnolia bud). Acrid, warm. Governs cold wind in the five zang and body, pain of the head and brain, and dark spots on the face. Taken long, it brings down qi, lightens the body, brightens the eyes, adds years and lets one withstand ageing. Also called xinshen (the *Taiping yulan* reads 引), houtao or fangmu. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: The fruit is collected in the ninth month and dried in the sun.
