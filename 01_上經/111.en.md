# 神農本草經 上經 111 桑上寄生 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sangshang jisheng (sojokisei; mulberry mistletoe). Bitter, neutral. Governs lumbar pain, stiffness of the back in children and welling-abscesses and swellings; it calms the fetus, fills the flesh and skin, firms the hair and teeth and grows the beard and eyebrows. Its fruit brightens the eyes, lightens the body and gives access to the spirits. Also called jixie, yumu or wantong. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called niao. It grows on mulberry trees in Hongnong. The stems are collected on the third day of the third month and dried in the shade.
