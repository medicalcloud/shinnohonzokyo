# 神農本草經 上經 112 杜仲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Duzhong (eucommia bark). Acrid, neutral. Governs pain of the lumbus and spine; it supplements the centre, augments the essential qi, firms the sinews and bones, strengthens the will, and removes itching and dampness of the genitals and post-micturition dribbling. Taken long, it lightens the body and lets one withstand ageing. Also called sixian. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Duzhong, also called mumian or sizhong (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called sizhong (written 腫) or mumian. It grows in Shangyu, Shangdang and Hanzhong. The bark is collected in the second, fifth, sixth and ninth months.
