# 神農本草經 上經 113 女貞実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Nüzhenshi (ligustrum fruit). Bitter, neutral. Governs supplementing the centre; it calms the five zang, nourishes the essence and spirit and dispels the hundred diseases. Taken long, it makes one plump and strong, lightens the body and prevents ageing. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Wuling. Collected at the Beginning of Winter.
