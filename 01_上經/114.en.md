# 神農本草經 上經 114 木蘭 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mulan (mokuren; magnolia bark). Bitter, cold. Governs great heat of the body within the skin; it removes facial heat, red pimples, rosacea, aversion to wind with madness, and itching and dampness of the genitals. It brightens the ears and eyes. Also called linlan. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called dulan. The bark resembles cinnamon and is fragrant. It grows in Lingling and on Mount Tai. The bark is collected in the twelfth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on mulan (magnolia bark).
- 瘨 is the archaic form of 癲 (madness); Sun uses archaic forms. The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 癲 (variant).
