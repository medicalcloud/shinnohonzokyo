# 神農本草經 上經 115 蕤核 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ruihe (prinsepia kernel). Sweet, warm. Governs pathogenic qi of the heart and abdomen; it brightens the eyes and [treats] red, painful and injured eyes with tearing. Taken long, it lightens the body, augments qi and prevents hunger. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Ruihe, also called rui (𦳚). Shennong, Leigong: sweet, neutral, non-toxic. It grows in pools and marshes. Collected in the eighth month. It supplements the center, strengthens the will and brightens the eyes. Taken long, it prevents hunger (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hangu and Baxi.
