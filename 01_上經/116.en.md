# 神農本草經 上經 116 橘柚 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Juyou (tangerine and pomelo). Acrid, warm. Governs conglomeration heat and counterflow qi in the chest and frees water and grain. Taken long, it removes odour, brings down qi and gives access to the spirits. Also called jupi. It grows in river valleys (formerly in the fruit section, which is wrong).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Nanshan and Jiangnan. Collected in the tenth month.

## Notes

The entry on juyou (tangerine peel, chenpi), which Sun moved from the fruits to the trees.
