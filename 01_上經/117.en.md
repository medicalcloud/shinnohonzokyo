# 神農本草經 上經 117 髪髲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fabi (human hair). Bitter, warm. Governs the five kinds of dribbling urinary blockage and block-and-repulsion; it frees urination and the waterways and treats epilepsy in children and tetany in adults. It still returns of itself to spiritual transformation.

## Notes

The entry on fabi (human hair), the only upper-grade drug from the human body.
- 仍自還神化 is hard to construe; it is taken to mean that hair, produced from a person's essence, returns to a spiritual function when used (not verified).
- There is no *Mingyi* quotation. The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 痓 (variant).
