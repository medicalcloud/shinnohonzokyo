# 神農本草經 上經 118 竜骨 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Longgu (dragon bone). Sweet, neutral. Governs ghost infixation of the heart and abdomen, spectral things and old demons, cough with counterflow, diarrhoea with pus and blood, spotting and hard bound concretions in women, and heat qi with fright epilepsy in children. The teeth govern fright epilepsy and madness with manic running in children and adults, bound qi below the heart with inability to breathe, and the various tetanies, and kill spectral things. Taken long, it lightens the body, gives access to the spirit light and prolongs the years. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Longgu grows in the land of Jin, on the shady side of mountain valleys where great waters pass; it is the bone of dead dragons. The blue-white is good. Collected in the twelfth month, or at no fixed time. Longgu fears ganqi, shujiao and lishi. Longchi (dragon teeth): Shennong, Mr Li: very cold. It treats fright epilepsy; taken long, it lightens the body (abridged from the *Taiping yulan* and the *Daguan* edition).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in the land of Jin and on Mount Tai, in caves on rocks and riverbanks where dragons died. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on longgu (dragon bone, considered to be fossil bones of ancient mammals); dragon teeth are included.
- 精物老魁 in the base is emended to 老魅 ("old demons").
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 癲疾 (variant).
