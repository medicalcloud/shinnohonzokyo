# 神農本草經 上經 119 麝香 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shexiang (musk). Acrid, warm. Governs warding off malign qi; it kills ghostly spectral things, [governs] warm malaria, gu toxins and epilepsy with tetany, and removes the three worms. Taken long, it dispels evil and one does not have nightmares or wake with terror. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from the mountains of Zhongtai, Yizhou and Yongzhou. It is taken at the Spring Equinox. The fresh is even better.

## Notes

The entry on shexiang (musk), an upper-grade animal drug.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 癇痓 (variant).
