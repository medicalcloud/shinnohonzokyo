# 神農本草經 上經 121 熊脂 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiongzhi (yushi; bear fat). Sweet, slightly cold. Governs wind impediment with numbness, tension of the sinews, accumulations in the five zang and abdomen, cold and heat with emaciation, scalp sores and white baldness, and dark spots and pimples on the face. Taken long, it strengthens the will, prevents hunger and lightens the body. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Yongzhou. Taken in the eleventh month.
