# 神農本草經 上經 122 白膠 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baijiao (deer-antler glue). Sweet, neutral. Governs damage to the centre with taxation exhaustion, lumbar pain and emaciation; it supplements the centre and augments qi, [treats] blood blockage with childlessness in women, stops pain and calms the foetus. Taken long, it lightens the body and prolongs the years. Also called lujiaojiao.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Yunzhong. It is made by boiling deer antlers.

## Notes

The entry on baijiao (deer-antler glue).
