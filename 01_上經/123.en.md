# 神農本草經 上經 123 阿膠 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ejiao (hide glue). Sweet, neutral. Governs internal collapse of the heart and abdomen, extreme taxation, shivering like malaria, pain of the lumbus and abdomen, soreness and aching of the four limbs, and bleeding in women; it calms the foetus. Taken long, it lightens the body and augments qi. Also called fuzhijiao.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Pingdong Commandery. It is made by boiling cowhide. It is produced at Dong'e.

## Notes

The entry on ejiao; the *Mingyi* says it is made from cowhide (later donkey hide was used).
