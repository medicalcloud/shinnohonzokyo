# 神農本草經 上經 124 丹雄鶏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Danxiongji (red cock). Sweet, slightly warm. Governs flooding and spotting with red and white discharge in women; it supplements depletion, warms the centre, stops bleeding, gives access to the spirits, kills toxins and wards off inauspicious influences. The head governs killing ghosts; that from above the east gate is even better. The fat governs deafness. The intestines govern enuresis. The yellow lining of the gizzard governs diarrhoea. The white of the droppings governs dispersion-thirst and the cold and heat of cold damage. The black hen governs wind-cold-damp impediment and the five slacknesses and six tensions, and calms the foetus. The wing feathers govern bringing down blood blockage. The egg governs dispelling heat, [treats] burns and epilepsy with tetany, and can be made into the spiritual substance amber. Jibaidu: fat. It grows in plains and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: The eggs of the red fowl can be made into amber (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Chaoxian.

## Notes

The entry on danxiongji (red cock), listing the effects of each part: head, fat, intestines, gizzard lining, droppings, black hen, feathers and eggs.
- 尿白 is taken as jishibai, the white part of chicken droppings.
- 鶏白蠹：肥脂 is hard to construe (the Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 鶏白橐); the translation keeps the characters.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 肶胵裡黄皮 (variant).
