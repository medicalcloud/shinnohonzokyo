# 神農本草經 上經 125 雁肪 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yanfang (goose fat). Sweet, neutral. Governs wind cramping and hypertonicity, hemiplegia, and blocked qi. Taken long, it augments qi, prevents hunger, lightens the body and lets one withstand aging. Also called wufang. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Yanfang. Shennong, Qibo, Leigong: sweet, non-toxic (the *Taiping yulan* has the two characters 鶩肪, which should read "also called wufang"). It kills the toxins of the various mineral drugs (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "collected at no fixed time").

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Jiangnan. Taken at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on yanfang (goose fat).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 偏活 (variant), probably an error.
