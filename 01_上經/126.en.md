# 神農本草經 上經 126 石蜜 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shimi (rock honey). Sweet, neutral. Governs pathogenic qi of the heart and abdomen and the various frights, tetanies and epilepsies; it calms the five zang, [supplements] the various insufficiencies, augments qi and supplements the center, stops pain and resolves toxins, dispels the multitude of illnesses and harmonizes the hundred drugs. Taken long, it strengthens the will, lightens the body, prevents hunger and ageing. Also called shiyi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shimi. Shennong, Leigong: sweet; its qi is neutral. It comes from Heyuan or Heliang (another quotation in the *Taiping yulan* says "it comes from the mountain valleys of Wudu").

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Heyuan in Wudu and from among the rocks of various mountains. That which is white like paste is good.

## Notes

The entry on shimi (honey made among rocks).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition has an extra 痓 (variant).
