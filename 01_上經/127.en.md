# 神農本草經 上經 127 蜂子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fengzi (bee larvae). Sweet, neutral. Governs head wind; it dispels gu toxins and supplements deficiency, emaciation and damage to the center. Taken long, it makes one glossy, improves the complexion and prevents ageing. Large yellow bee larvae govern distension, fullness and pain of the heart and abdomen [base: 復], and lighten the body and augment qi. Earth bee larvae govern welling-abscesses and swellings. Also called feiling. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Wudu.

## Notes

The entry on fengzi (bee larvae), with large yellow bee and earth bee larvae appended.
- 復 in 心腹復満痛 is translated as "distension" following 張 (脹) in the Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition (the base is kept).
