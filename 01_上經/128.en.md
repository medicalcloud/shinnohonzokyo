# 神農本草經 上經 128 蜜蝋 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mila (mitsuro; beeswax). Sweet, slightly warm. Governs diarrhea with pus and blood; it supplements the center and joins severed injuries and metal wounds. It augments qi, prevents hunger and lets one withstand aging. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from among trees and rocks with honeycombs in Wudu.

## Notes

The entry on mila (beeswax).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 蜜臘 (variant).
