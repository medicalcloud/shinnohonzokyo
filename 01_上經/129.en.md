# 神農本草經 上經 129 牡蠣 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Muli (oyster shell). Salty, neutral. Governs cold and heat of cold damage, warm malaria with shivering, and fright, anger and irate qi; it removes contraction and slackness, scrofulous fistulas, and vaginal discharge with red and white in women. Taken long, it strengthens the bones and joints, kills evil qi and prolongs the years. Also called lige. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called muge. It comes from the Eastern Sea. Collected at no fixed time.
