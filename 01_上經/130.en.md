# 神農本草經 上經 130 亀甲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guijia (tortoise shell). Salty, neutral. Governs spotting with red and white discharge; it breaks concretions and [treats] intermittent malaria, the five kinds of haemorrhoids and genital erosion, damp impediment, heaviness and weakness of the four limbs, and failure of the fontanelle to close in children. Taken long, it lightens the body and prevents hunger. Also called shenwu. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Nanhai and from lakes. Collected at no fixed time.
