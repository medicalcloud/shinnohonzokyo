# 神農本草經 上經 131 桑螵蛸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sangpiaoxiao (mantis egg case). Salty, neutral. Governs damage to the center, mounting-conglomeration and impotence; it augments essence and lets one beget children. [It governs] blood blockage and lumbar pain in women, opens the five strangury conditions and frees urination and the waterways. Also called shiyou. It grows on mulberry twigs. It is collected and steamed.

The *Wu Pu* says: Sangxiaotiao, also called (these two characters are missing in current editions) shiyou, haijiao or zhi. Shennong: salty, non-toxic (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It is the young of the mantis. Collected in the second and third months and roasted over fire.

## Notes

The entry on sangpiaoxiao (mantis egg case).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 桑蜱蛸, 要痛 and 蝕肬 (variants).
