# 神農本草經 上經 132 海蛤 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Haige (sea clam shell). Bitter, neutral. Governs cough with counterflow and rising qi, panting with vexation and fullness, and chest pain with cold and heat. Also called kuige.

The *Wu Pu* says: Haige. Shennong: sweet; Qibo: sweet; Bian Que: salty. The head of the large joint has patterns like ground teeth. Collected at no fixed time.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Nanhai.

## Notes

The entry on haige (sea clam shell).
- The main text has no clause on where it grows.
