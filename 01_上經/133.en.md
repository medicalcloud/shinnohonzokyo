# 神農本草經 上經 133 文蛤 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wenge (patterned clam shell). Governs malign sores, erosion (the *Taiping yulan* reads "removes genital erosion") and the five kinds of haemorrhoids (the *Taiping yulan* adds "bleeding from the great orifice [anus]"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters).

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from the Eastern Sea. It has patterns on the surface. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on wenge.
- The text gives no flavour or nature.
