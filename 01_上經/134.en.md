# 神農本草經 上經 134 蠡魚 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Liyu (snakehead fish) (the *Chuxue ji* quotes 鱧魚). Sweet, cold. Governs damp impediment and puffy swelling of the face and eyes, and drains great water. Also called tongyu. It grows in pools and marshes.

## Notes

The entry on li fish (liyu, the snakehead). In the Zhongyi Jicheng text it has no heading and follows the wenge (bunko) entry; it corresponds to an item in the list of names and is treated as a unit.
- 味苦 in the base is emended to 味甘 ("sweet").
- There is no *Mingyi* quotation; Sun's note (案) is not translated.
