# 神農本草經 上經 135 鯉魚胆 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Liyudan (carp gall). Bitter, cold. Governs heat, redness and pain of the eyes and clear-eyed blindness, and brightens the eyes. Taken long, it makes one strong and bold and augments the will and qi. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Jiujiang. Collected at no fixed time.
