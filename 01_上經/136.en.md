# 神農本草經 上經 136 藕実茎 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Oushijing (lotus seed and stem). Sweet, neutral. Governs supplementing the centre and nourishing the spirit; it augments vigour and dispels the hundred diseases. Taken long, it lightens the body, lets one withstand ageing, prevents hunger and prolongs the years. Also called shuizhidan. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called lian. It grows in Runan. Collected in the eighth month.

## Notes

The entry on oushijing (lotus), the first upper-grade fruit.
