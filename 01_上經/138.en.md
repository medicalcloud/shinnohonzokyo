# 神農本草經 上經 138 葡萄 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Putao (grape). Sweet, neutral. Governs damp impediment of the sinews and bones; it augments qi, doubles strength, strengthens the will, makes one plump and strong, lets one endure hunger and withstand wind and cold. Eaten long, it lightens the body, prevents aging and prolongs the years. It can be made into wine. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Wuyuan and Dunhuang of Longxi.

## Notes

The entry on putao (grape).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 蒲萄 (variant).
