# 神農本草經 上經 139 蓬藟 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Penglei (bramble). Sour, neutral. Governs calming the five zang; it augments the essential qi, enlarges and firms the genitals, strengthens the will, doubles strength and lets one have children. Taken long, it lightens the body and prevents ageing. Also called fupen. It grows in plains and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Quepen, also called juepen (*Taiping yulan*). The *Zhen shi bencao* says: Fupenzi, also called malou or lujing (ibid.).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called linglei or yinyao. It grows on Mount Jing and in Yuanju.

## Notes

The entry on penglei (bramble), also called fupen (raspberry).
