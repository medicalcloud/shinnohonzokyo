# 神農本草經 上經 140 鶏頭実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jitoushi (euryale seed). Sweet, neutral. Governs damp impediment and pain of the lumbus, spine and knees; it supplements the center, dispels sudden illnesses, augments the essential qi, strengthens the will and makes the ears and eyes keen. Taken long, it lightens the body, prevents hunger, lets one withstand aging and makes one an immortal. Also called yanzhuoshi. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called qian. It grows in Leize. Collected in the eighth month.

## Notes

The entry on jitoushi (qianshi, euryale seed).
- 味苦 in the base is emended to 味甘 ("sweet"). The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 雁喙実 (variant).
