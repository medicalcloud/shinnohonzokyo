# 神農本草經 上經 141 胡麻 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huma (goma; sesame). Sweet, neutral. Governs damage to the center with deficiency and emaciation; it supplements the five internal [organs] (the *Taiping yulan* reads 臓), augments vigour, grows the flesh and fills the marrow and brain. Taken long, it lightens the body and prevents ageing. Also called jusheng. Its leaves are called qingrang. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Huma, also called fangjin. Shennong, Leigong: sweet, non-toxic. Also called goushi. Collected at the Beginning of Autumn.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called goushi, fangjing or hongcang. It grows in Shangdang.

## Notes

The entry on huma (sesame), an upper-grade grain.
