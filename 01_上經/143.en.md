# 神農本草經 上經 143 冬葵子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dongkuizi (tokishi; mallow seed). Sweet, cold. Governs cold and heat of the five zang and six fu, emaciation and the five kinds of dribbling urinary blockage, and frees urination. Taken long, it firms the bones, grows the flesh, lightens the body and prolongs the years.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Shaoshi. Collected in the twelfth month.

## Notes

The entry on dongkuizi (mallow seed), the first upper-grade vegetable.
- The main text has no clause on where it grows.
