# 神農本草經 上經 144 莧実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xianshi (amaranth seed). Sweet, cold. Governs clear-eyed blindness; it brightens the eyes, dispels pathogen, frees urine and stool and removes cold and heat. Taken long, it augments vigour, prevents hunger and lightens the body. Also called maxian.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called moshi. It grows in Huaiyang and in fields; the leaves are like indigo. Collected in the eleventh month.
