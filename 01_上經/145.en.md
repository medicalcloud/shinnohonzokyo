# 神農本草經 上經 145 瓜蒂 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guadi (katei; melon stalk). Bitter, cold. Governs great water with puffy swelling of the body, face and four limbs; it drains water and kills gu toxins. Cough with counterflow and rising qi, and illness in the chest and abdomen from eating various fruits, are all expelled by vomiting or purging. It grows in plains and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Song. Collected on the seventh day of the seventh month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on guadi (melon stalk), known as an emetic (Guadi san [Kateisan] in the *Shanghan lun*).
- 蒿高 in the *Mingyi* quotation is taken as an error for 嵩高 (Mount Song).
