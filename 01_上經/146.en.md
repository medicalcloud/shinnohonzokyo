# 神農本草經 上經 146 瓜子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guazi (kashi; wax gourd seed). Sweet, neutral. Governs making one glossy [base: 閲] and improving the complexion; it augments qi and prevents hunger. Taken long, it lightens the body and lets one withstand ageing. Also called shuizhi (suitetsu; the *Taiping yulan* reads tuzhi). It grows in plains and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Guazi, also called bian. Collected on the seventh day of the seventh month. It can be made into face cream (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called baiguazi. It grows on Mount Song. It is the kernel of the wax gourd. Collected in the eighth month.

## Notes

The entry on guazi (wax gourd seed).
- 令人閲沢 is read as "make glossy" (悦沢), following 令心悦沢 in the Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition (the base is kept).
