# 神農本草經 上經 147 苦菜 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kucai (kusai; sow thistle). Bitter, cold. Governs pathogenic qi of the five zang, aversion to grain, and stomach impediment. Taken long, it calms the heart and augments qi, makes one keen and perceptive with little sleep, lightens the body and lets one withstand ageing. Also called tucao or xuan. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called youdong. It grows on hills and beside roads in Yizhou and survives the winter without dying. Collected on the third day of the third month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on kucai, the last drug of the Upper Classic.
- 厭谷 is read as 厭穀, "aversion to grain (food)".
