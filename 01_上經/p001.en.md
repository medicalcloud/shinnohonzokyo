# 神農本草經 上經 p001 上薬一百二十種，為君，主養命以応天，無毒… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The upper drugs number one hundred and twenty. They serve as sovereigns, govern the nourishing of life and correspond to heaven; they are without toxicity. Taken in quantity or over a long time they do not harm a person. Those who wish to lighten the body, augment qi, not age and prolong their years should base themselves on the Upper Classic.

## Notes

The introductory statement at the head of the Upper Classic, describing the nature of the upper drugs.
- Volumes 2 and 3 open with parallel statements about the middle and lower drugs.
- 本上經 is read as "base oneself on [the drugs of] the Upper Classic".
