# 神農本草經 上經 p002 丹砂　雲母　玉泉　石鐘乳　涅石　硝石　朴… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dansha (cinnabar), yunmu (unmo; mica), yuquan (jade spring), shizhongru (stalactite), nieshi (alum), xiaoshi (saltpetre), poxiao (bokusho; crude mirabilite), huashi (kasseki; talc), shidan (chalcanthite), kongqing (hollow azurite), cengqing (layered azurite), yuyuliang (uyoryo; limonite), taiyi yuliang, baishiying (hakusekiei; white quartz), zishiying (murasakisekiei; amethyst), wuse shizhi (five-colored halloysite), baiqing, bianqing.

## Notes

The list of upper-grade jade and stone drugs.
- The heading in the text has 丹沙; the list writes 丹砂.
- The English glosses follow common identifications and are given only as an aid.
