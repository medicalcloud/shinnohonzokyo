# 神農本草經 上經 p002 丹砂　雲母　玉泉　石鐘乳　涅石　硝石　朴… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dansha (cinnabar), yunmu (mica), yuquan (jade spring), shizhongru (stalactite), nieshi (alum), xiaoshi (saltpetre), poxiao (crude mirabilite), huashi (talc), shidan (chalcanthite), kongqing (hollow azurite), cengqing (layered azurite), yuyuliang (limonite), taiyi yuliang, baishiying (white quartz), zishiying (amethyst), wuse shizhi (five-coloured halloysite), baiqing, bianqing.

## Notes

The list of upper-grade jade and stone drugs.
- The heading in the text has 丹沙; the list writes 丹砂.
- The English glosses follow common identifications and are given only as an aid.
