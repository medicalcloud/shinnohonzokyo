# 神農本草經 上經 p004 菖蒲　鞠華　人参　天門冬　甘草　乾地黄　… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Changpu (shobu; acorus), juhua (chrysanthemum), renshen (ninjin; ginseng), tianmendong (tenmondo; asparagus root), gancao (kanzo; licorice), gan dihuang (kanjio; dried rehmannia), zhu (atractylodes), tusizi (toshishi; dodder seed), niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes), chongweizi (motherwort seed), nüwei, fangkui, chaihu (saiko; bupleurum), maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon), duhuo (dokukatsu; angelica pubescens), cheqianzi (shazenshi; plantago seed), muxiang (mokko; costus root), shuyu (shoyo; Chinese yam), yiyiren (yokuinin; coix seed), zexie (takusha; alisma), yuanzhi (onji; polygala), longdan (ryutan; gentian), xixin (saishin; asarum), shihu (sekkoku; dendrobium), bajitian (hagekiten; morinda), baiying, baihao (byakuko), chijian (sekisen; gastrodia), anlüzi, ximingzi, shishi (yarrow seed), the red, black, blue-green, white, yellow and purple zhi (ganoderma), juanbai (selaginella), lanshi (ranjitsu), xiongqiong (ligusticum), miwu, huanglian (oren; coptis), luoshi (rakuseki), jilizi (shitsurishi; tribulus), huangqi (ogi; astragalus), roucongrong (cistanche), fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia), puhuang (hoo; typha pollen), xiangpu (koho), xuduan (zokudan; dipsacus), loulu, yingshi (eijitsu; rose hip), tianmingjing (tenmeisei), juemingzi (ketsumeishi; cassia seed), danshen (tanjin; salvia root), qiangen (senkon; madder root), feilian, wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra), xuanhua, lancao (ranso), shechuangzi (jashoshi; cnidium seed), difuzi (jifushi; kochia seed), jingtian, yinchen (artemisia capillaris), duruo, shashen (shajin; adenophora), baituhuo, xuchangqing, shilongchu, weixian, yunshi, wangbuliuxing (ofurugyo; vaccaria seed), shengma (shoma; cimicifuga), qingrang, guhuo, bieji, qucao, huaimu.

## Notes

The list of upper-grade herbs.
- Some separating spaces are missing in the base list (巴戟天白英, 茵陳杜若); the translation separates the names.
- Some names in the list differ in writing from the headings in the text (菴䕡子 vs 奄閭子, 薯蕷 vs 署予, 肉蓯蓉 vs 肉松蓉, etc.).
