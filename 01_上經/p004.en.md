# 神農本草經 上經 p004 菖蒲　鞠華　人参　天門冬　甘草　乾地黄　… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Changpu (acorus), juhua (chrysanthemum), renshen (ginseng), tianmendong (asparagus root), gancao (licorice), gan dihuang (dried rehmannia), zhu (atractylodes), tusizi (dodder seed), niuxi (achyranthes), chongweizi (motherwort seed), nüwei, fangkui, chaihu (bupleurum), maimendong (ophiopogon), duhuo (angelica pubescens), cheqianzi (plantago seed), muxiang (costus root), shuyu (Chinese yam), yiyiren (coix seed), zexie (alisma), yuanzhi (polygala), longdan (gentian), xixin (asarum), shihu (dendrobium), bajitian (morinda), baiying, baihao, chijian (gastrodia), anlüzi, ximingzi, shishi (yarrow seed), the red, black, blue-green, white, yellow and purple zhi (ganoderma), juanbai (selaginella), lanshi, xiongqiong (ligusticum), miwu, huanglian (coptis), luoshi, jilizi (tribulus), huangqi (astragalus), roucongrong (cistanche), fangfeng (saposhnikovia), puhuang (typha pollen), xiangpu, xuduan (dipsacus), loulu, yingshi (rose hip), tianmingjing, juemingzi (cassia seed), danshen (salvia root), qiangen (madder root), feilian, wuweizi (schisandra), xuanhua, lancao, shechuangzi (cnidium seed), difuzi (kochia seed), jingtian, yinchen (artemisia capillaris), duruo, shashen (adenophora), baituhuo, xuchangqing, shilongchu, weixian, yunshi, wangbuliuxing (vaccaria seed), shengma (cimicifuga), qingrang, guhuo, bieji, qucao, huaimu.

## Notes

The list of upper-grade herbs.
- Some separating spaces are missing in the base list (巴戟天白英, 茵陳杜若); the translation separates the names.
- Some names in the list differ in writing from the headings in the text (菴䕡子 vs 奄閭子, 薯蕷 vs 署予, 肉蓯蓉 vs 肉松蓉, etc.).
