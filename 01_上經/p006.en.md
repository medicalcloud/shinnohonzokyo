# 神農本草經 上經 p006 牡桂　菌桂　松脂　槐実　枸杞　柏実　茯苓… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mugui (bokei), jungui (cinnamon barks), songzhi (matsuyani; pine resin), huaishi (kaijitsu; sophora fruit), gouqi (kuko; lycium), baishi (hakujitsu; arborvitae seed), fuling (bukuryo; poria), yupi (elm bark), suanzao (sanso; sour jujube), baimu (phellodendron), ganqi (kanshitsu; dried lacquer), wujiapi (gokahi; acanthopanax bark), manjingshi (vitex fruit), xinyi (shini; magnolia flower), sangshang jisheng (sojokisei; mulberry mistletoe), duzhong (tochu; eucommia), nüzhenshi (ligustrum fruit), mulan (mokuren), ruihe, juyou (kitsuyu; tangerine and pomelo).

## Notes

The list of upper-grade trees.
- 茯苓 and 柏木 in the list are written 伏苓 and 蘖木 in the headings of the text.
