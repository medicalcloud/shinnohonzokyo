# 神農本草經 上經 p006 牡桂　菌桂　松脂　槐実　枸杞　柏実　茯苓… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mugui, jungui (cinnamon barks), songzhi (pine resin), huaishi (sophora fruit), gouqi (lycium), baishi (arborvitae seed), fuling (poria), yupi (elm bark), suanzao (sour jujube), baimu (phellodendron), ganqi (dried lacquer), wujiapi (acanthopanax bark), manjingshi (vitex fruit), xinyi (magnolia flower), sangshang jisheng (mulberry mistletoe), duzhong (eucommia), nüzhenshi (ligustrum fruit), mulan, ruihe, juyou (tangerine and pomelo).

## Notes

The list of upper-grade trees.
- 茯苓 and 柏木 in the list are written 伏苓 and 蘖木 in the headings of the text.
