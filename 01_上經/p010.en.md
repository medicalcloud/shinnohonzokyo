# 神農本草經 上經 p010 竜骨、麝香、牛黄、熊脂、白膠、阿膠 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Longgu (dragon bone), shexiang (musk), niuhuang (bezoar), xiongzhi (bear fat), baijiao (deer-antler glue), ejiao (donkey-hide glue).

## Notes

The list of upper-grade animal drugs.
