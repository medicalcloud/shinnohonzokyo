# 神農本草經 上經 p014 石蜜、蜂子、蜜蝋、牡蠣、亀甲、桑螵蛸、海… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shimi (rock honey), fengzi (bee larvae), mila (mitsuro; beeswax), muli (borei; oyster shell), guijia (kiko; tortoise shell), sangpiaoxiao (mantis egg case), haige, wenge (bunko; clam shells), lishi (snakehead fish), liyudan (rigyotan; carp gall).

## Notes

The list of upper-grade insects and fish.
