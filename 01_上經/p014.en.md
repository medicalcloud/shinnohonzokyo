# 神農本草經 上經 p014 石蜜、蜂子、蜜蝋、牡蠣、亀甲、桑螵蛸、海… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shimi (rock honey), fengzi (bee larvae), mila (beeswax), muli (oyster shell), guijia (tortoise shell), sangpiaoxiao (mantis egg case), haige, wenge (clam shells), lishi (snakehead fish), liyudan (carp gall).

## Notes

The list of upper-grade insects and fish.
