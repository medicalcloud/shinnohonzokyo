# 神農本草經 上經 p016 藕実茎、大棗、葡萄、蓬藟　鶏頭実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Oushijing (gujitsukei; lotus seed and stem), dazao (taiso; jujube), putao (grape), penglei (bramble), jitoushi (euryale seed).

## Notes

The list of upper-grade fruits.
