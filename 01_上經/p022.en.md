# 神農本草經 上經 p022 上，玉、石，上品一十八種，旧同。 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: eighteen upper-grade jade and stone drugs; the same as in the old [edition].

## Notes

A tally in the text, in which Sun Xingyan compares the count with the arrangement of the old edition (the *Daguan bencao*). The upper-grade jade and stone drugs end here.
