# 神農本草經 上經 p023 上草，上品七十三種，旧七十二種，考門芝当… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: seventy-three upper-grade herbs; the old [edition] has seventy-two. On examination, menzhi should be raised by one, and shengma should be in white characters; qingrang was mistakenly put in the grain section; of the six [drugs set aside in] the *Tang bencao*, guhuo, qucao and huaimu should all be put here.

## Notes

The tally for upper-grade herbs, with Sun Xingyan's explanation of his rearrangement.
- 門芝當一升 is hard to construe; it is tentatively translated as "should be raised by one", presumably concerning the counting of the six zhi as one item (not verified).
- "White characters" refers to the *Daguan bencao*, where the text of the Classic was printed in white-on-black characters.
