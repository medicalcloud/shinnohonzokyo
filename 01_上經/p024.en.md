# 神農本草經 上經 p024 上木，上品二十種，旧一十九種。考果部，橘… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: twenty upper-grade trees; the old [edition] has nineteen. On examination, juyou (kitsuyu) of the fruit section should be put here.

## Notes

The tally for upper-grade trees.
