# 神農本草經 上經 p030 上米、穀，上品二種。旧三種。今以青蘘入草… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: two upper-grade grains; the old [edition] has three. Now qingrang has been moved to the herbs.

## Notes

The tally for upper-grade grains.
