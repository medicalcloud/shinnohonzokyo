# 神農本草經 上經 p031 上菜，上品五種。旧同。 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: five upper-grade vegetables; the same as in the old [edition].

## Notes

The tally for upper-grade vegetables. The drugs of the Upper Classic end here.
