# 神農本草經 中經 001 雄黄 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xionghuang (yuo; realgar). Bitter, neutral, cold. Governs cold and heat, scrofulous fistulas and malign sores, flat-abscesses, hemorrhoids and dead flesh; it kills spectral things, malign ghosts, pathogenic qi and the toxins of the hundred worms, and overcomes the five weapons. Refined and eaten, it lightens the body and makes one an immortal. Also called huangshishi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Xionghuang. Shennong: bitter. On the shady side of mountains there is red xionghuang; because it grows on the sunny side it is called xiong ("male"); it is the male of cinnabar, hence the name xionghuang.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on the sunny side of Wudu and Dunhuang. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on xionghuang (realgar, arsenic sulfide), the first middle-grade drug.
- 勝五兵 is understood as overcoming (wounds from) the five kinds of weapons.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition repeats 物 (variant).
