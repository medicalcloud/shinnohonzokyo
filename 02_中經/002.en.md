# 神農本草經 中經 002 石流黄 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shiliuhuang (sulfur) (流 was formerly written 硫; the *Taiping yulan* quotes 流, which is correct). Sour, warm. Governs genital erosion in women and flat-abscesses, hemorrhoids and malign blood; it firms the sinews and bones, removes baldness and can transform gold, silver, copper, iron and other rare things (the *Taiping yulan* quotes: "Shiliuqing is white and governs augmenting the liver qi and brightening the eyes; shiliuchi grows in the mountain valleys of Qiangdao"). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Liuhuang, also called shiliuhuang (written 留). Shennong, Huangdi, Leigong: salty, toxic; Yi He, Bian Que: bitter, non-toxic. It grows in Yiyang or Hexi. It may be of five colors; the yellow is the liquor of panshui stone (潘 is the archaic form of 礬), [good when] it burns with a purple flame. Collected in the eighth and ninth months. It treats blood binding in women (the *Taiping yulan* says: "treats exhausted yin in women and can combine gold, silver, copper and iron").

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Muyang by the Eastern Sea and in the mountains of Mount Tai and Hexi. It is the liquor of alum.
