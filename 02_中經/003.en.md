# 神農本草經 中經 003 雌黄 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cihuang (orpiment). Acrid, neutral. Governs malign sores, baldness, scabs and scabies; it kills toxic worms and lice, and [governs] itching of the body, evil qi and the various toxins. It is refined. Taken long, it lightens the body, adds years and prevents ageing. It grows in mountain valleys.

Wu Pu says: Cishi, also called cijun.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Wudu, on the same mountains as xionghuang. Gold lies on the shady side of the mountain; when the essence of gold steams, cihuang is produced. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on cihuang (orpiment, arsenic sulfide).
- Wu Pu's "Cishi, also called cijun" is the same as Wu Pu's text in the cishi entry (unit 6) and has apparently strayed into this entry (translated as in the base).
