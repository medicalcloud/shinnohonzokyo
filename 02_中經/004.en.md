# 神農本草經 中經 004 水銀 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shuiyin (suigin; mercury). Acrid, cold. Governs scabies fistulas, scabby sores and white baldness; it kills lice in the skin, causes abortion, dispels heat and kills the toxins of gold, silver, copper and tin. Melted and transformed, it returns again to cinnabar. Taken long, one becomes an immortal and does not die. It is found in flat land.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called gong. It comes from Fuling (bukuryo) and is produced from cinnabar.

## Notes

The entry on shuiyin (mercury).
- 之死 in the base is emended to 不死 ("does not die"). The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 鎔化 (variant).
