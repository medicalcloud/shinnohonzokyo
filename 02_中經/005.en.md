# 神農本草經 中經 005 石膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shigao (gypsum). Acrid, slightly cold. Governs cold and heat of wind-stroke, counterflow qi below the heart with fright and panting, dry mouth and parched tongue with inability to breathe, and hard pain in the abdomen; it dispels evil ghosts, and [governs] childbirth and metal wounds. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called xishi. It grows on Mount Qi, Mount Qilu and Mount Mengshan in Lu. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on shigao (gypsum).
- 口乾苦焦 in the base is emended to 口乾舌焦 ("dry mouth and parched tongue").
- 産乳 is understood as childbirth.
