# 神農本草經 中經 006 磁石 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cishi (magnetite). Acrid, cold. Governs generalized impediment and wind-damp with pain in the limbs and joints, inability to hold things, and shivering with soreness and wasting; it dispels great heat with vexation and fullness, and deafness. Also called yuanshi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Cishi, also called cijun.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called chushi. It grows on the shady side of Mount Tai and Mount Ci; where there is iron, it grows on the sunny side. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on cishi (magnetite).
- 洗洗酸消 is understood as shivering with soreness and wasting, but this is not certain.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 慈石 (variant).
