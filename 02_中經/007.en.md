# 神農本草經 中經 007 凝水石 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ningshuishi (gyosuiseki; calcite). Acrid, cold. Governs body heat, accumulations and pathogenic qi in the abdomen, a feeling as though the skin were being burned by fire, and vexation and fullness. It is taken with water. Taken long, it prevents hunger. Also called baishuishi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shennong: acrid; Qibo, Yi He, Bian Que: sweet, non-toxic; Mr Li: very cold. It may grow in Handan. Collected at no fixed time. It is colored like mica (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called hanshuishi [kansuiseki]").

The *Mingyi* says: Also called hanshuishi or lingshuishi. It is the essence of salt. It grows on Mount Chang [base: 常案] and Mount Ning, and in Zhongshui County and Handan. The *Fanzi jiran* says: Shuishi comes from Hedong; that with a lustrous color is good.

## Notes

The entry on ningshuishi (hanshuishi).
- 常案 in the *Mingyi* quotation may be an error for the place name 常山 (not verified).
- The *Fanzi jiran* quotation at the end of the *Mingyi* paragraph is Sun's; since it stands in the same paragraph in the base, it is translated.
