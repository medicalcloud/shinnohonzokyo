# 神農本草經 中經 008 陽起石 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangqishi (actinolite). Salty, slightly warm. Governs flooding and spotting; it breaks blood in the uterus, concretions and bound qi, [and governs] cold and heat with abdominal pain, childlessness, and impotence (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "yin and yang do not unite"), and supplements insufficiency (the *Taiping yulan* quotation has the two characters 句攣). Also called baishi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Yangqishi. Shennong, Bian Que: sour, non-toxic; Tongjun, Leigong, Qibo [base: 岐後]: salty, non-toxic; Mr Li: slightly cold. It may grow on Mount Tai (the *Taiping yulan* quotes: "or it is said, on [Mount] Yangqi. Collected at no fixed time").

The *Mingyi* says: Also called shisheng or yangqishi (written 羊). It is the root of mica. It grows on Mount Qi and in Langye, or on Mount Yun and Mount Yangqi. Collected at no fixed time.
