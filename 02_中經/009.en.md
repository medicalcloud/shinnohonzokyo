# 神農本草經 中經 009 孔公孽 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Konggongnie (stalactite base). Acrid, warm. Governs food damage with indigestion, bound evil qi, malign sores, flat-abscesses, fistulas and haemorrhoids; it frees the nine orifices and brings down breast milk (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called tongshi"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Konggongnie. Shennong: acrid; Qibo: salty; Bian Que: sour, non-toxic. The colour is bluish-yellow.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called tongshi; it is the root of yinnie. Bluish-yellow in colour. It grows on Mount Liang.
