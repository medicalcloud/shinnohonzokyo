# 神農本草經 中經 010 殷孽 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yinnie. Acrid, warm. Governs festering wounds and static blood, diarrhea with cold and heat, scrofulous fistulas [base: 鼠寒瘻], and concretions and bound qi. Also called jiangshi. It grows in mountain valleys (note: this should form one entry with konggongnie).

The *Mingyi* says: It is the root of stalactites. It is found in the land of Yue, on Mount Liang and in Nanhai. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on yinnie; Sun holds that it should be combined with konggongnie.
- 寒 in 鼠寒瘻 seems superfluous; it is translated as "scrofulous fistulas" (not verified).
