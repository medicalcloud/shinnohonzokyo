# 神農本草經 中經 011 鉄精 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tiejing (iron essence). Neutral. Governs brightening the eyes and transforms copper. Tieluo (iron flakes): acrid, neutral; governs wind-heat with malign sores, ulcers, flat-abscesses and scabs, and scabies qi in the skin. Tie (iron): governs firming the flesh and enduring pain. It is found in plains and marshes (formerly three entries; now combined).

The *Mingyi* says: Tiejing, also called tieye. It can be used to dye colors. It is found in Muyang and in Bengcheng or Xicheng. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on tiejing, with tieluo and tie (iron) appended.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 鉄精落 (variant).
