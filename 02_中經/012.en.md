# 神農本草經 中經 012 理石 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lishi (fibrous gypsum). Acrid, cold. Governs body heat; it benefits the stomach and resolves vexation, augments essence and brightens the eyes, breaks accumulations and removes the three worms. Also called lizhishi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called jishi. It is like gypsum, fine and following the grain. It grows in Hanzhong and on Mount Lu. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on lishi, a gypsum-like mineral.
- 一名石立制石 in the text repeats 石; it is translated as lizhishi (the base is kept).
