# 神農本草經 中經 013 長石 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Changshi (anhydrite). Acrid, cold. Governs body heat and cold reversal of the four limbs; it frees urination, opens the blood vessels, brightens the eyes, removes nebulae and squint, expels the three worms and kills gu toxins. Taken long, it prevents hunger. Also called fangshi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Changshi, also called fangshi or zhishi. It grows in the mountain valleys of Zhangzi. It is like horse teeth, glossy, jade-coloured, long and bright. Taking it prevents hunger (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called tushi or zhishi. Its grain is like horse teeth, square and glossy, jade-coloured. It grows on Mount Zhangzi, Mount Tai and in Linzi. Collected at no fixed time.
