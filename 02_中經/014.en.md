# 神農本草經 中經 014 膚青 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fuqing. Acrid, neutral. Governs gu toxins and the various toxins of snakes, vegetables and meat, and malign sores. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called tuiqing or tuishi. It grows in Yizhou.

## Notes

The entry on fuqing; its identification is unknown.
