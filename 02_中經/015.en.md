# 神農本草經 中經 015 乾薑 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ganjiang (dried ginger). Acrid, warm. Governs chest fullness and cough with counterflow and rising qi; it warms the centre, stops bleeding, brings out sweat, and drives out wind-damp impediment, intestinal afflux and diarrhoea. The fresh is even better. Taken long, it removes foul odour and gives access to the spirit light. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Jianwei and in Jingzhou and Yangzhou. Collected in the ninth month.

## Notes

The entry on ganjiang (dried ginger), the first middle-grade herb; fresh ginger is noted as "even better".
