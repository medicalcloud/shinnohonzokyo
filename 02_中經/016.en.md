# 神農本草經 中經 016 枲耳実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xi'ershi (xanthium fruit). Sweet, warm. Governs cold pain from head wind, wind-damp with generalized impediment, hypertonicity and pain of the four limbs, and malign and dead flesh. Taken long, it augments qi, makes the ears and eyes keen, strengthens the will and lightens the body. Also called huxi or dikui. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called shi or changsi. It grows in the fields of Anlu and Liu'an. Collected when the fruit is ripe.
