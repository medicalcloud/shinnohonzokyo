# 神農本草經 中經 017 葛根 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gegen (pueraria root). Sweet, neutral. Governs dispersion-thirst, great heat of the body, vomiting and the various impediments; it raises yin qi and resolves the various toxins. Gegu (pueraria seed) governs diarrhoea of more than ten years. Also called jiqigen. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Gegen. Shennong: sweet. It grows on Mount Tai (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called luhuo or huangjin. It grows on Mount Wen. The root is collected in the fifth month and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on gegen (pueraria root), with gegu (seed) appended.
- 生種穀 in the base is emended to 生川谷 ("grows in river valleys").
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 已上 (variant).
