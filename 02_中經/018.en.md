# 神農本草經 中經 018 栝蔞根 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gualougen (karokon; trichosanthes root). Bitter, cold. Governs wasting-thirst, body heat with vexation and fullness, and great heat; it supplements deficiency, calms the center and joins severed injuries. Also called dilou. It grows in river valleys and on the shady side of mountains.

The *Wu Pu* says: Gualou, also called zeju or zegu (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called guoluo, tiangua or zegu. The fruit is called huanggua. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the sun; it is ready in thirty days. It grows in Hongnong.

## Notes

The entry on gualougen (trichosanthes root).
