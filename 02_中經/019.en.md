# 神農本草經 中經 019 苦参 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kushen (kujin; sophora root). Bitter, cold. Governs bound qi of the heart and abdomen, concretions and accumulations, jaundice and post-micturition dribbling; it expels water, removes welling-abscesses and swellings, supplements the center, brightens the eyes and stops tearing. Also called shuihuai or kushi. It grows in mountain valleys and fields.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called dihuai, tuhuai, jiaohuai, baijing, huma, qinjing, luyue or linglang. It grows in Runan. The root is collected in the third, eighth and tenth months and dried in the sun.
