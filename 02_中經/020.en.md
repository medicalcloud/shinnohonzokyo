# 神農本草經 中經 020 当帰 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Danggui (Chinese angelica). Sweet, warm. Governs cough with counterflow and rising qi, warm malaria with cold and heat shivering in the skin (the *Daguan* edition notes "洗 is pronounced like 癬"), spotting with childlessness in women, the various malign sores and ulcers, and metal wounds. It is boiled and drunk. Also called gangui. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Danggui. Shennong, Huangdi, Tongjun, Bian Que: sweet, non-toxic; Qibo, Leigong: acrid, non-toxic; Mr Li: slightly warm. It may grow in the lands of the Qiang and Hu.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Longxi. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on danggui (Chinese angelica).
- 洗在皮膚中 could also be read, following the *Daguan* phonetic note, as "tinea (癬) in the skin". The translation takes it as the shivering (洗洗) of cold and heat, but this is not certain.
