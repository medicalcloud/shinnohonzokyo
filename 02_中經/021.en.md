# 神農本草經 中經 021 麻黄 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mahuang (mao; ephedra). Bitter, warm. Governs wind-stroke, headache of cold damage and warm malaria; it releases the exterior and brings out sweat, removes pathogenic heat qi, stops cough with counterflow and rising qi, dispels cold and heat and breaks hard concretions and accumulations. Also called longsha.

The *Wu Pu* says: Mahuang, also called beixiang or beijian. Shennong, Leigong: bitter, non-toxic; Bian Que: sour, non-toxic; Mr Li: neutral. It may grow in Hedong. Collected in the fourth month and at the Beginning of Autumn (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called beixiang or beiyan. It grows in the land of Jin and Hedong. The stems are collected at the Beginning of Autumn and dried in the shade so that they stay green.

## Notes

The entry on mahuang (ephedra), used to induce sweating in the *Shanghan lun*.
- The main text has no clause on where it grows.
