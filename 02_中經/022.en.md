# 神農本草經 中經 022 通草 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tongcao (tsuso; akebia stem). Acrid, neutral. Governs removing malign worms; it dispels cold and heat of the spleen and stomach, frees the nine orifices, blood vessels and joints, and keeps one from forgetting. Also called fuzhi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Tongcao, also called dingweng or fuzhi. Shennong, Huangdi: acrid; Leigong: bitter. It grows in the mountain valleys of Shicheng; the leaves are green and spreading. It stops sweating. Collected from the first month (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called dingweng. It grows in Shicheng and Shanyang. The branches are collected in the first month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on tongcao, considered to correspond to later mutong (akebia).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 関結 (variant).
