# 神農本草經 中經 024 蠡実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lishi (iris seed). Sweet, neutral. Governs cold and heat of the skin, heat qi in the stomach and wind-cold-damp impediment; it firms the sinews and bones and gives one an appetite. Taken long, it lightens the body. The flowers and leaves remove white worms. Also called jucao, sanjian or shishou. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Lishi, also called jucao, sanjian, julihua (*Taiping yulan*), zelan or shishou. Shennong, Huangdi: sweet, acrid, non-toxic. It grows in Yuanju. Collected in the fifth month (ibid.).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called lishi (written 茘実). It grows in Hedong. The seeds are collected in the fifth month and dried in the shade.
