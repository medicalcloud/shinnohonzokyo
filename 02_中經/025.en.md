# 神農本草經 中經 025 瞿麦 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qumai (kubaku; dianthus). Bitter, cold. Governs block-and-repulsion and the various dribbling blockages with urine not passing; it draws out thorns, opens welling-abscesses and swellings, brightens the eyes and removes nebulae, breaks the fetus and causes abortion, and brings down blocked blood. Also called jujumai. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called daju or dalan. It grows on Mount Da. The seeds are collected at the Beginning of Autumn and dried in the shade.
