# 神農本草經 中經 027 秦艽 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qinjiao (large-leaf gentian). Bitter, neutral. Governs cold and heat and pathogenic qi, cold-damp, wind impediment and pain of the limbs and joints; it drains water and frees urination. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Feiwu. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the sun.
