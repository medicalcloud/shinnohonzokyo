# 神農本草經 中經 028 百合 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baihe (lily bulb). Sweet, neutral. Governs abdominal distension from evil qi and heart pain; it frees urine and stool, supplements the centre and augments qi. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Baihe, also called chongmai or zhongting. It grows in Guanqu and on Mount Jing (the *Yiwen leiju* quotes "also called chongkuang").

The *Mingyi* says: Also called chongxiang, moluo, zhongfenghua or qiangqu. It grows in Jingzhou. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the sun.
