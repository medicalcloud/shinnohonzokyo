# 神農本草經 中經 029 知母 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhimu (chimo; anemarrhena). Bitter, cold. Governs wasting-thirst and heat in the center; it dispels pathogenic qi and puffy swelling of the limbs and body, drains water, supplements insufficiency and augments qi. Also called chimu, lianmu, yeliao, dishen, shuishen, shuijun, huomu or timu. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Zhimu. Shennong, Tongjun: non-toxic. It supplements insufficiency and augments qi (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called timu").

The *Mingyi* says: Also called nülei, nüli, ercao, lulie, jiupeng, erzhongcao, donggen, shuixu, chenfan or hao. It grows in Henei. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the sun.
