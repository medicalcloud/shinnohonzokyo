# 神農本草經 中經 030 貝母 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Beimu (baimo; fritillaria). Acrid, neutral. Governs vexing heat of cold damage, dribbling strangury, pathogenic qi, mounting-conglomeration, throat impediment, difficult childbirth, metal wounds and wind tetany. Also called kongcao.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called yaoshi, kuhua, kucai (kusai), shangcao (the character 𦯶) or qinmu. It grows in the land of Jin. The root is collected in the tenth month and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on beimu (fritillaria).
- The main text has no clause on where it grows. The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 空艸 (variant).
