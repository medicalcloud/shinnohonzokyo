# 神農本草經 中經 031 白芷 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baizhi (byakushi) (Dahurian angelica). Acrid, warm. Governs metrostaxis with red and white discharge in women, blood blockage, genital swelling, cold and heat, and head wind invading the eyes with tearing. It grows the flesh and skin, makes them glossy, and can be made into face cream. Also called fangxiang. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Baizhi, also called xiao, fuli, zefen or li (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called baizhi, xiao, guan, fuli or zefen. The leaves, also called lima, can be made into a bath. It grows in the lowland marshes of Hedong. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on baizhi (Dahurian angelica).
