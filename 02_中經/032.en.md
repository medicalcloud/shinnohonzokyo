# 神農本草經 中經 032 淫羊藿 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yinyanghuo (epimedium). Acrid, cold. Governs impotence and severed injuries and pain in the penis; it frees urination, augments vigor and strengthens the will. Also called gangqian. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Yinyanghuo. Shennong, Leigong: acrid; Mr Li: slightly cold. It firms the bones (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Yang in Shangjun.
