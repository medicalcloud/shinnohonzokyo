# 神農本草經 中經 034 狗脊 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gouji (kuseki; cibotium). Bitter, neutral. Governs [disorders of] the lumbus and back; it strengthens the joints and [treats] slackness and tension, generalized impediment from cold-damp and knee pain. It is quite beneficial to the elderly. Also called baizhi. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Gouji, also called gouqing or chijie. Shennong, Tongjun, Huangdi, Qibo, Leigong, Bian Que: sweet, non-toxic; Mr Li: slightly warm. It is like bixie; the stem nodes are like bamboo with thorns, the leaves round and red, the root yellowish-white, also like bamboo root, with thorny hairs. The *Qibo jing* says: the stem is long and jointed, the leaf tips round, blue-red, the bark white with red veins.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called qianglü, fugai or fujin. It grows on Mount Chang. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the sun.
