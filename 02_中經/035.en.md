# 神農本草經 中經 035 石竜芮 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shilongrui (celery-leaved buttercup). Bitter, neutral. Governs wind-cold-damp impediment and pathogenic qi of the heart and abdomen; it frees the joints and stops vexation and fullness. Taken long, it lightens the body, brightens the eyes and prevents ageing. Also called luguoneng (the *Taiping yulan* reads shiguo) or dishen. It grows by rocks in rivers and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Longrui, also called jiangtai or tiandou. Shennong: bitter, neutral; Qibo: sour; Bian Que, Mr Li: very cold; Leigong: salty, non-toxic. Collected on the fifth day of the fifth month (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called shineng, penggen or tiandou. It grows on Mount Tai. The seeds are collected on the fifth day of the fifth month and the bark in the second and eighth months; dried in the shade.
