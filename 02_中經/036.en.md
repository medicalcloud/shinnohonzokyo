# 神農本草經 中經 036 茅根 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Maogen (chigaya; imperata root). Sweet, cold. Governs taxation damage with deficiency and emaciation; it supplements the center and augments qi, dispels static blood, blood blockage and cold and heat, and frees urination. Its shoots govern draining water. Also called langen or rugen. It grows in mountain valleys and fields.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called diguan, dijin or jiandu. It grows in the land of Chu. The root is collected in the sixth month.
