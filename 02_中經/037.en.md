# 神農本草經 中經 037 紫菀 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ziwan (aster root). Bitter, warm. Governs cough with counterflow and rising qi and cold, heat and bound qi in the chest; it removes gu toxins and wilting and lameness, and calms the five zang. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Ziwan, also called qingyuan (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called ziqian or qingyuan. It grows in Fangling, Zhending and Handan. The root is collected in the second and third months and dried in the shade.
