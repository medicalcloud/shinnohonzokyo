# 神農本草經 中經 038 紫草 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zicao (shiso; lithospermum root). Bitter, cold. Governs pathogenic qi of the heart and abdomen and the five kinds of jaundice; it supplements the center and augments qi, frees the nine orifices and opens the waterways. Also called zidan or ziyao (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called dixue"; not in the *Daguan* edition). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Zicao has red nodes. It flowers in the second month (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Dang and in the land of Chu. The root is collected in the third month and dried in the shade.
