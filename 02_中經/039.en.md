# 神農本草經 中經 039 敗醤 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baijiang (patrinia). Bitter, neutral. Governs violent heat and burns, red qi, scabies itch, jaundiced haemorrhoids and saddle-sore heat. Also called luchang. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called lushou, macao or zebai. It grows in Jiangxia. The root is collected in the eighth month and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on baijiang (patrinia).
- 馬鞍熱気 is understood as a heated sore caused by saddle chafing.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 疥掻疽痔 (variant).
