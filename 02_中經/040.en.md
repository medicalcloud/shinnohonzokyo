# 神農本草經 中經 040 白蘚 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baixian (dictamnus root bark). Bitter, cold. Governs head wind, jaundice, cough with counterflow, dribbling strangury, swelling and pain of the vagina, and damp impediment and dead flesh with inability to bend and stretch, stand up and walk. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Shanggu and Yuanju. The root is collected in the fourth and fifth months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on baixian (baixianpi).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 白鮮皮 (variant).
