# 神農本草經 中經 041 酸醤 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suanjiang (Chinese lantern). Sour, neutral. Governs heat with vexation and fullness; it settles the will, augments qi and frees the waterways. In difficult childbirth, swallowing its fruit brings immediate delivery. Also called cujiang. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Suanjiang, also called cujiang (written 酢) (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Jingchu and in the gardens of households. Collected in the fifth month and dried in the shade.
