# 神農本草經 中經 043 藁本 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gaoben (kohon) (Chinese lovage root). Acrid, warm. Governs mounting-conglomeration in women, cold, swelling and pain in the vagina and tension in the abdomen; it dispels headache from wind, grows the flesh and skin and brightens the complexion. Also called guiqing or dixin. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called weijing (ikei). It grows on Mount Chong. The root is collected in the first and second months and dried in the sun; it is ready in thirty days.
