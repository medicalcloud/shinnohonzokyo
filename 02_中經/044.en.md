# 神農本草經 中經 044 石葦 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shiwei (sekii; pyrrosia leaf). Bitter, neutral. Governs taxation heat and pathogenic qi and the five kinds of dribbling urinary blockage that do not pass; it frees urination and the waterways. Also called shize. It grows on rocks in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called shipi. It grows in the mountain valleys of Huayin. That which does not hear the sound of water or of people is good. The leaves are collected in the second month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on shiwei (pyrrosia).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 石韋 and repeats 生 (variants).
