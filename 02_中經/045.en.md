# 神農本草經 中經 045 萆薢 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bixie (dioscorea). Bitter, neutral. Governs pain of the lumbus and back; it strengthens the bones and joints, and [treats] wind-cold-damp with generalized impediment, malign sores that do not heal, and heat qi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called chijie. It grows in Zhending. The root is collected in the eighth month and dried in the sun.
