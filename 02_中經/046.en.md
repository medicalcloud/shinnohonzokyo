# 神農本草經 中經 046 白薇 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baiwei (cynanchum root). Bitter, neutral. Governs sudden wind-stroke, body heat with fullness of the limbs, abstraction with loss of consciousness, mania and delusion, evil qi, cold and heat with soreness and aching, and warm malaria with shivering, occurring at fixed times. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called baimu, weicao, chuncao or gumei. It grows on plains. The root is collected on the third day of the third month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on baiwei.
- 酸痋 in the base is emended to 酸疼 ("soreness and aching").
