# 神農本草經 中經 048 王瓜 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wanggua (snake gourd). Bitter, cold. Governs dispersion-thirst, internal impediment and static blood, menstrual blockage, cold and heat and soreness and aching; it augments qi and cures deafness. Also called tugua. It grows in plains and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in the fields of the land of Lu and between the walls of households. The root is collected in the third month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on wanggua (Trichosanthes cucumeroides).
- 兪聾 in the base is emended to 愈聾 ("cures deafness").
