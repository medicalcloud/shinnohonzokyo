# 神農本草經 中經 049 地楡 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diyu (chiyu; sanguisorba root). Bitter, slightly cold. Governs spasmodic pain of the breasts in women, the seven damages and vaginal discharge; it stops pain, removes malign flesh, stops sweating and treats metal wounds (the *Taiping yulan* has above "governs dispersing wine" and also "brightens the eyes"; the *Daguan* edition has "dispersing wine" in black characters and lacks "brightens the eyes"). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Tongbai and Yuanju. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the sun.

## Notes
