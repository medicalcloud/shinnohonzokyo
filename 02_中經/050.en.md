# 神農本草經 中經 050 海藻 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Haizao (sargassum). Bitter, cold. Governs goitre and tumour qi and nodes below the neck; it breaks and disperses bound qi, welling-abscesses and swellings, concretions and hard qi, [treats] rumbling up and down in the abdomen, and drains the twelve kinds of water swelling. Also called luoshou. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called tan. It grows in the Eastern Sea. Collected on the seventh day of the seventh month and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on haizao (sargassum), known as a drug for goitre.
