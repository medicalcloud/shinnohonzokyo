# 神農本草經 中經 051 沢蘭 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zelan (lycopus). Bitter, slightly warm. Governs internal bleeding in nursing mothers (the *Taiping yulan* reads "bleeding"), lingering illness after wind-stroke, abdominal enlargement with water swelling, puffy swelling of the body, face and four limbs, water within the joints, metal wounds, welling-abscesses and swellings and pus of sores. Also called hulan or longzao. It grows beside large marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Zelan, also called shuixiang. Shennong, Huangdi, Qibo, Tongjun: sour, non-toxic; Mr Li: warm. It grows beside water on low ground. The leaves are like thoroughwort; it sprouts in the second month, is fragrant, with red nodes and four leaves facing one another between the branch nodes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called hupu. It grows in Runan. Collected on the third day of the third month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on zelan (lycopus).
- 乳婦内衄 is understood as internal bleeding in women after childbirth.
