# 神農本草經 中經 052 防己 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fangji (stephania root). Acrid, neutral. Governs wind-cold and warm malaria and heat qi with the various epilepsies; it dispels evil and frees urine and stool. Also called jieli (the *Taiping yulan* reads shijie and quotes "opens the interstices and frees the nine orifices"; the *Daguan* edition has these six characters in black). It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Mufangji, also called jieli or jieyan. Shennong: acrid; Huangdi, Qibo, Tongjun: bitter, non-toxic; Mr Li: very cold. It is like reng, with spreading stems, like jiao; the white root is yellow outside like platycodon, black inside and split like the spokes of a wheel. The root is collected in the second, eighth and tenth months (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hanzhong. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on fangji (stephania root).
- 諸癰 in the base is emended to 諸癇 ("the various epilepsies").
- 結梗 in Wu Pu's text is taken as 桔梗 (platycodon).
