# 神農本草經 中經 053 款冬花 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kuandonghua (coltsfoot flower). Acrid, warm. Governs cough with counterflow and rising qi, frequent panting, throat impediment, the various fright epilepsies and cold, heat and evil qi. Also called tuowu (the *Taiping yulan* reads shi), kedong (the *Taiping yulan* reads 顆冬), huxu or mianxi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Kuandong flowers yellowish-white in the twelfth month (*Yiwen leiju*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called didong. It grows on Mount Chang and beside the waters of [base: 目常]. The flowers are collected in the eleventh month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on kuandonghua (coltsfoot flower).
