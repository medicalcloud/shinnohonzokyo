# 神農本草經 中經 055 馬先蒿 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Maxianhao (lousewort). Flavor [missing], neutral [?]. Governs cold and heat with ghost infixation, wind-stroke with damp impediment, vaginal discharge disease in women and childlessness. Also called mashihao. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Nanyang.

## Notes

The entry on maxianhao (Pedicularis).
- 味平 seems to lack the flavor character (the four-volume Executive Yuan text and others read 味苦，平); marked [?].
- 馬尿蒿 in the base is emended to 馬屎蒿.
