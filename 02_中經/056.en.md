# 神農本草經 中經 056 積雪草 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jixuecao (gotu kola). Bitter, cold. Governs great heat, malign sores, welling- and flat-abscesses, spreading sores and red erysipelas, redness of the skin and body heat. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Jingzhou.

## Notes

The entry on jixuecao (Centella).
- 浸淫赤熛 is understood as spreading eczema and red erysipelas-like conditions.
