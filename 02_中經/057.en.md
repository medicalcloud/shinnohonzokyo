# 神農本草經 中經 057 女菀 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Nüwan. Acrid, warm. Governs shivering from wind, cholera with diarrhea, rumbling intestines moving up and down without fixed place, fright epilepsy and the hundred illnesses of cold and heat. It grows in river valleys or on the sunny side of mountains.

The *Wu Pu* says: Nüwan, also called baiwan or shinüyuan (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called baiwan, zhinüwan or mao. It grows in Hanzhong. Collected in the first and second months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on nüwan; its identification is uncertain.
