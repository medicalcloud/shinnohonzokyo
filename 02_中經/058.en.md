# 神農本草經 中經 058 王孫 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wangsun. Bitter, neutral. Governs evil qi of the five zang, cold-damp impediment, aching and soreness of the four limbs and cold pain of the knees. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Huangsun, also called wangsun, manyan, gongcao or haisun. Shennong, Leigong: bitter, non-toxic; Huangdi: sweet, non-toxic. It grows in the mountain valleys of Xihai and under the walls of the city of Runan. It spreads, with red markings and stems and leaves facing each other (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: In Wu it is called baigongcao, in Chu wangsun, in Qi changsun. Also called huangsun, huanghun, haisun or manyan. It grows in Haixi and under the city walls of Runan.

## Notes

The entry on wangsun; its identification is uncertain.
