# 神農本草經 中經 059 蜀羊泉 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shuyangquan. Bitter, slightly cold. Governs baldness and malign sores, scabies and itchy scabs from heat qi and ringworm, and treats dental caries. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called yangquan or yi. It grows in Shu Commandery.

## Notes

The entry on shuyangquan; its identification is uncertain.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 掻痂 (variant).
