# 神農本草經 中經 060 爵床 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Juechuang (justicia). Salty, cold. Governs pain of the lumbus and spine so that one cannot lie on the bed and has difficulty bending forward and back, and dispels heat. It can be made into a bath. It grows in river valleys and fields.

The *Wu Pu* says: Juechuang, also called jueqing (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hanzhong.

## Notes

The entry on juechuang (Justicia procumbens).
