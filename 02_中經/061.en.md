# 神農本草經 中經 061 仮蘇 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiasu (schizonepeta). Acrid, warm. Governs cold and heat with scrofulous fistulas and scrofula with sores; it breaks bound and gathered qi, brings down static blood and dispels damp impediment. Also called shuming. It grows in rivers and marshes (formerly in the vegetable section; now moved).

The *Wu Pu* says: Jiasu, also called shushi or jiangjie (*Taiping yulan*). It is called jingjie; the leaves resemble luoli but are finer, and in Shu it is eaten raw (commentary of the *Shu bencao*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called jiangjie. It grows in Hanzhong.

## Notes

The entry on jiasu (jingjie, schizonepeta), which Sun moved from the vegetables to the herbs.
