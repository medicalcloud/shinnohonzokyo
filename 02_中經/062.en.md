# 神農本草經 中經 062 翹根 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qiaogen (forsythia root). Sweet, cold, neutral (the *Taiping yulan* reads "bitter, neutral"). Governs bringing down heat qi; it augments yin essence, makes one cheerful and comely [base: 而説好] and brightens the eyes. Taken long, it lightens the body and lets one withstand ageing. It grows in plains and marshes (formerly among those set aside in the *Tang bencao*; now moved).

The *Wu Pu* says: Qiaogen. Shennong, Leigong: sweet, toxic. Collected in the third and eighth months; it is steamed, [for] people ill from drinking wine (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Song. Collected in the second and eighth months.

## Notes

The entry on qiaogen, considered the root of lianqiao (forsythia).
- 令人而説好 is translated following 令人悦好 in the Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition (the base is kept).
- Wu Pu's 以作蒸，飲酒病人 is hard to construe.
