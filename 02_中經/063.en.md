# 神農本草經 中經 063 桑根白皮 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sanggen baipi (mulberry root bark). Sweet, cold. Governs damage to the centre, the five taxations and six extremes, emaciation, and flooding with severed vessels; it supplements depletion and augments qi. The leaves govern dispelling cold and heat and bringing out sweat. Black mulberry ear [fungus] governs spotting with red and white fluid in women, blood disorders, concretions and accumulations, genital pain, cold and heat of yin and yang, and childlessness. The ears of the five trees are called ru; they augment qi, prevent hunger, lighten the body and strengthen the will. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Sang'er, also called sangjun or mumai. It grows in Qianwei. Collected in the rainy season of the sixth month and immediately dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on sanggen baipi (mulberry root bark), with the leaves, mulberry ear fungus and the ears of the five trees appended.
- 赤白汗, 陰補 and 名糯 in the base are emended to 赤白汁, 陰痛 and 名檽 (see the emendations).
