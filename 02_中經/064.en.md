# 神農本草經 中經 064 竹葉 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhuye (chikuyo; bamboo leaf). Bitter, neutral. Governs cough with counterflow, rising qi and overflow [?], tension of the sinews and malign ulcers, and kills small worms. The root, made into a decoction, augments qi and stops thirst, supplements deficiency and brings down qi. The juice governs wind tetany. The fruit gives access to the spirit light, lightens the body and augments qi.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Yizhou.

## Notes

The entry on zhuye (bamboo leaf), with the root, juice (zhuli) and fruit appended.
- 溢 in 咳逆上気溢 is hard to construe and is marked [?].
- The main text has no clause on where it grows.
