# 神農本草經 中經 065 呉茱萸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuzhuyu (evodia fruit) (the *Taiping yulan* quotation lacks 呉, which is correct). Acrid, warm. Governs warming the centre; it brings down qi, stops pain, [treats] cough with counterflow and cold and heat, dispels damp and blood impediment, drives out wind evil and opens the interstices (formerly written 腠; the *Taiping yulan* writes 涛; 湊 is correct). The root kills the three worms. Also called yi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Yuanju. Collected on the ninth day of the ninth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on wuzhuyu (evodia fruit).
- 開湊理 means opening the interstices (couli); Sun writes 湊 following old texts.
