# 神農本草經 中經 066 卮子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhizi (gardenia fruit) (formerly written 梔; the *Yiwen leiju* and *Taiping yulan* quote 支, which is correct). Bitter, cold. Governs evil qi of the five internal [organs], heat qi in the stomach, red face, wine pimples, red nose, white and red lai disease, and sores and ulcers. Also called mudan. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called yuetao. It grows in Nanyang. The fruit is collected in the ninth month and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on zhizi (gardenia fruit).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 梔子 (variant).
