# 神農本草經 中經 067 蕪荑 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuyi (elm seed). Acrid. Governs evil qi of the five internal [organs]; it disperses toxins creeping warmly through the skin, bones and joints, removes the three worms and digests food. Also called wugu or diantang (written 𦽄䕋) (the *Taiping yulan* quotes: "expels cun-white worms and disperses warm panting in the abdomen"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters). It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called diantang. It grows on Mount Jin. The fruit is collected in the third month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on wuyi.
- The alternative name 殿塘 in the main text is emended to 𦽄䕋 (see the emendation); 殿塘 in the *Mingyi* quotation is kept.
- The text gives no thermal nature.
