# 神農本草經 中經 068 枳実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhishi (immature bitter orange). Bitter, cold. Governs great wind in the skin with bitter itching like hemp seeds or beans (the *Taiping yulan* reads "phlegm", which is wrong); it dispels cold and heat binding and stops diarrhoea (formerly written 痢; the *Taiping yulan* writes 利, which is correct). It grows the flesh, benefits the five zang, augments qi and lightens the body. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Zhishi: bitter. Leigong: sour, non-toxic; Mr Li: very cold. Collected in the ninth and tenth months and dried in the shade (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Henei. Collected in the ninth and tenth months and dried in the shade.
