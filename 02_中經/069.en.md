# 神農本草經 中經 069 厚朴 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Houpo (koboku; magnolia bark). Bitter, warm. Governs wind-stroke, cold damage, headache, cold and heat, fright palpitation qi, and blood impediment with dead flesh, and removes the three worms.

The *Wu Pu* says: Houpo. Shennong, Qibo, Leigong: bitter, non-toxic; Mr Li: slightly warm (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called houpi; it grows in Jiaozhi").

The *Mingyi* says: Also called houpi or chipu. The tree is called zhen and its seed zhu. It grows in Jiaozhi and Yuanju. The bark is collected in the ninth and tenth months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on houpo (magnolia bark).
- The main text has no clause on where it grows.
