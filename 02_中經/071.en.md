# 神農本草經 中經 071 秦菽 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qinjiao (Qin pepper). Acrid, warm. Governs pathogenic wind qi; it warms the center, dispels cold impediment, firms the teeth and hair and brightens the eyes. Taken long, it lightens the body, improves the complexion, lets one withstand aging, adds years and gives access to the spirits. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Tai and the Qin Mountains, or in Langye. The fruit is collected in the eighth and ninth months.

## Notes

The entry on qinjiao (Zanthoxylum), written 秦菽 in Sun's heading.
