# 神農本草經 中經 072 山茱萸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shanzhuyu (cornus fruit). Sour, neutral. Governs evil qi below the heart and cold and heat; it warms the centre, drives out cold-damp impediment and removes the three worms. Taken long, it lightens the body. Also called shuzao. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shanzhuyu, also called bashi, shushi or jizu. Shennong, Huangdi, Leigong, Bian Que: sour, non-toxic; Qibo: acrid; one classic: sour. It may grow in Yuanju and Langye, or in Cheng County of Donghai. The leaves are like plum with thorny hairs; in the second month the flowers are like apricot; in the fourth month the fruit is like sour jujube, red. The fruit is collected in the fifth month (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called jizu or bashi. It grows in Hanzhong, Langye, Yuanju and Cheng County of Donghai. The fruit is collected in the ninth and tenth months and dried in the shade.
