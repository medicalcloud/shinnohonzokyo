# 神農本草經 中經 073 紫葳 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ziwei (trumpet creeper). Sour (the *Taiping yulan* reads salty), slightly cold. Governs lingering illness after childbirth, flooding, concretions with blood blockage, and cold and heat with emaciation, and nourishes the fetus. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Ziwei, also called wuwei, qumai (kubaku), lingjufu, guimu or longhua. Shennong, Leigong: sour; Qibo: acrid; Bian Que: bitter, salty; Huangdi: sweet, non-toxic. It is like wheat with a black root. Collected in the first and eighth months. It may grow in Zhending (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called lingtiao or longhua. It grows in Xihai and Shanyang.
