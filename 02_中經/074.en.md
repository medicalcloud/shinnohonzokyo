# 神農本草經 中經 074 豬苓 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhuling (chorei; polyporus). Sweet, neutral. Governs intermittent malaria; it resolves toxins, gu infixation (the *Taiping yulan* reads 蛀) and inauspicious influences, and frees the waterways. Taken long, it lightens the body and lets one withstand ageing (the *Taiping yulan* reads 能老). Also called jiazhuniao. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Zhuling. Shennong: sweet; Leigong: bitter, non-toxic (the *Taiping yulan* quotes: "like fuling (bukuryo); it may grow in Yuanju; collected in the eighth month").

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Heng and in Jiyin and Yuanju. Collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on zhuling (polyporus sclerotium).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes the alternative name 猳豬屎 (variant).
