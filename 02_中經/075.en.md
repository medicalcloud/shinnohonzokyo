# 神農本草經 中經 075 白棘 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baiji (jujube thorn). Acrid, cold. Governs pain of the heart and abdomen and ruptured, suppurating welling-abscesses and swellings, and stops pain. Also called jizhen. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called jici. It grows in Yongzhou.

## Notes

The entry on baiji, considered to be the thorns of the sour jujube.
- 漬膿 in the base is emended to 潰膿 ("ruptured and suppurating").
