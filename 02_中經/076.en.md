# 神農本草經 中經 076 竜眼 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Longyan (ryugan; longan). Sweet, neutral. Governs pathogenic qi of the five zang; it calms the will and [treats] aversion to food. Taken long, it strengthens the hun, makes one keen, lightens the body, prevents aging and gives access to the spirit light. Also called yizhi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Longyan, also called yizhi. The *[Qimin] yaoshu* says "also called bimu" (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: The large ones resemble areca nuts. It grows on pine trees [?] in Nanhai. Collected in the fifth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on longyan (longan).
- 生南海松樹上 in the *Mingyi* quotation does not fit how longan grows and may contain an error (marked [?]).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 軽耳 (variant).
