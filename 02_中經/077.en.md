# 神農本草經 中經 077 松羅 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Songluo (usnea). Bitter, neutral. Governs rage and evil qi; it stops depletion sweating, head wind, and genital cold and swelling in women. Also called nüluo. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Xiong'er.

## Notes

The entry on songluo (Usnea).
- 嗔怒 in the base is emended to 瞋怒. The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 松蘿 (variant).
