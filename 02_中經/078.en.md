# 神農本草經 中經 078 衛矛 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Weimao (nishikigi; winged euonymus). Bitter, cold. Governs flooding and bleeding in women and abdominal fullness with sweating; it dispels pathogen and kills ghost toxins and gu infixation. Also called guijian. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Guijian, also called weimao. Shennong, Huangdi, Tongjun: bitter, non-toxic. The leaves are like peach and like feathers. Collected in the first, second and seventh months and dried in the shade. It may grow in fields (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Huo. Collected in the eighth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on weimao (Euonymus alatus).
- 虫注 in the base is emended to 蠱注 ("gu infixation").
