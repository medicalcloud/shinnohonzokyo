# 神農本草經 中經 079 合歓 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hehuan (albizia bark). Sweet, neutral. Governs calming the five zang; it benefits the heart and will (the *Yiwen leiju* reads "harmonizes the heart and will", the *Taiping yulan* "harmonizes the heart qi") and makes one joyful and free of worry. Taken long, it lightens the body, brightens the eyes and lets one obtain what one desires. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Yizhou.
