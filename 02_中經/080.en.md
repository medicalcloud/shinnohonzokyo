# 神農本草經 中經 080 白馬茎 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baimajing (white horse penis). Salty, neutral. Governs damage to the center with severed vessels and impotence; it strengthens the will and augments qi, grows the flesh, makes one plump and strong and lets one beget children. The eyes govern fright epilepsy, abdominal fullness and malaria; they are used from a slaughtered animal. The hanging hoof governs fright pathogen, tugging and slackening and difficult childbirth, and wards off malign qi, ghost toxins, gu infixation and inauspicious influences. It is found in plains and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Yunzhong.

## Notes

The entry on baimajing (penis of a white horse), with the eyes and hoof appended; the first middle-grade animal drug.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 瘈瘲 (variant).
