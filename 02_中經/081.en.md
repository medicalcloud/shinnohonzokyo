# 神農本草經 中經 081 鹿茸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lurong (rokujo; deer velvet). Sweet, warm. Governs metrostaxis with malign blood, cold and heat and fright epilepsy; it augments qi, strengthens the will, makes teeth grow and prevents ageing. The antler governs malign sores and welling-abscesses and swellings, drives out evil and malign qi, and [removes] blood lodged in the genitals. The *Mingyi* says: The velvet is taken in the fourth and fifth months when the antlers are shed, dried in the shade and turned from time to time. The antlers are collected in the seventh month.

## Notes

The entry on lurong (deer velvet), with the antler appended.
- In the base the *Mingyi* text stands in the same paragraph as the main text. There is no clause on where it grows.
