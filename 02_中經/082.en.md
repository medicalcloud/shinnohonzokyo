# 神農本草經 中經 082 牛角䚡 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Niujiaosai (ox horn core). It brings down blocked blood and [treats] static blood with aching pain and vaginal discharge with blood in women. The marrow supplements the centre and fills the bones and marrow; taken long, it adds years. The gall can be made into pills.

## Notes

The entry on niujiaosai (the bony core of ox horn), with the marrow and gall appended.
- The text gives no flavour or nature and begins with 下閉血. There is no *Mingyi* quotation.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 牛角鰓 and 瘀痛 (variants).
