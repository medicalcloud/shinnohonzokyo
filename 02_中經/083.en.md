# 神農本草經 中經 083 羖羊角 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guyangjiao (ram horn). Salty, warm. Governs clear-eyed blindness; it brightens the eyes, kills scabies worms, stops cold diarrhoea, wards off malign ghosts, tigers and wolves, and stops fright palpitations. Taken long, it calms the heart, augments qi and lightens the body. It is found in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Hexi. Taken at no fixed time.
