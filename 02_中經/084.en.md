# 神農本草經 中經 084 牡狗陰茎 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mugou yinjing (dog penis). Salty, neutral. Governs damage to the centre and impotence; it makes [the penis] strong, hot and large, lets one beget children, and dispels the twelve diseases of vaginal discharge in women. Also called goujing. The gall governs brightening the eyes.

The *Mingyi* says: Taken during the first fu period of the sixth month and dried in the shade for a hundred days.

## Notes

The entry on mugou yinjing (penis of a male dog), with the gall appended.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition places 胆主明目 before 一名狗精 (variant).
