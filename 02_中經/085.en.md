# 神農本草經 中經 085 羚羊角 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lingyangjiao (reiyokaku; antelope horn). Salty, cold. Governs brightening the eyes; it augments qi, raises yin, removes downpouring malign blood, wards off gu toxins, malign ghosts and inauspicious influences, calms the heart qi, and one never has bad nightmares. It is found in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Shicheng and Mount Huayin. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on lingyangjiao (antelope horn).
- 羚, 弘 and 羚羊 in Sun's note are emended to 麢 (see the emendations); Sun's note regards 羚 as a vulgar form of 麢.
