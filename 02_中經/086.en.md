# 神農本草經 中經 086 犀角 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xijiao (rhinoceros horn). Bitter, cold. Governs the hundred toxins and worm infixation, evil ghosts and miasmic qi; it kills the toxins of gouwen, zhen feathers and snakes, and [makes one] not deluded and free of nightmares [base: 除不迷或厭寐]. Taken long, it lightens the body. It is found in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Yongchang and Yizhou.

## Notes

The entry on xijiao (rhinoceros horn).
- 軽血 in the base is emended to 軽身 ("lightens the body").
- 除不迷或厭寐 is interpreted following 除不迷惑不厭寐 in the Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition, which also reads 百毒蠱注 (variants).
