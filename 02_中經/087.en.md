# 神農本草經 中經 087 燕屎 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yanshi (swallow droppings). Acrid, neutral. Governs gu toxins and ghost infixation; it drives out inauspicious influences and evil qi, breaks the five kinds of dribbling urinary blockage and frees urination. It is found in level valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from high mountains.

## Notes

The entry on yanshi (swallow droppings), a middle-grade bird drug.
