# 神農本草經 中經 088 天鼠屎 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tianshushi (bat droppings). Acrid, cold. Governs welling-abscesses and swellings of the face, shivering of the skin with intermittent pain, and blood and qi in the intestines; it breaks cold and heat and accumulations and dispels fright palpitations. Also called shuyun or shigan. It is found in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Hepu. Taken in the tenth and twelfth months.
