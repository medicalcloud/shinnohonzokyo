# 神農本草經 中經 089 猥皮 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Weipi (hedgehog skin). Bitter, neutral. Governs the five kinds of hemorrhoids and genital erosion, bleeding in five colors, red and white, unceasing bloody fluid, genital swelling, and pain radiating to the lumbus and back. It is boiled in wine to kill it. It is found in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from the fields of Mount Chu. Taken at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on weipi (hedgehog skin); Sun writes the heading 猥皮.
- 要 in 要背 is the archaic form of 腰. The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 蝟皮, 血汗 and 腰背 (variants).
- 酒煮殺之 may also be understood as boiling in wine to kill [its toxicity].
