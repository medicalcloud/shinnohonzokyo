# 神農本草經 中經 090 露蜂房 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lufengfang (hornet nest). Bitter, neutral. Governs fright epilepsy with tugging and slackening, cold and heat with evil qi, madness, ghostly spectres and gu toxins, and intestinal haemorrhoids. Dry-roasted over fire it is good. Also called fengchang. It is found in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called baichuan or fengchao (𭄰). It comes from Zangke. Collected on the seventh day of the seventh month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on lufengfang (hornet nest), the first middle-grade insect drug.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 癲疾 (variant).
