# 神農本草經 中經 091 鼈甲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Biejia (soft-shelled turtle shell). Salty, neutral. Governs concretions and hard accumulations of the heart and abdomen and cold and heat; it removes glomus, polyps, genital erosion, haemorrhoids and malign flesh. It is found in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Danyang. Taken at no fixed time.
