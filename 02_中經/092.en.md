# 神農本草經 中經 092 蟹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xie (crab). Salty, cold. Governs evil qi in the chest, bound heat with pain, and deviation of the mouth with facial swelling. Burned with spoiled lacquer, it attracts rats [?]. It is found in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from the waters of the Yi and Luo. Taken at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on xie (crab).
- 喎僻而腫 in the base is emended to 喎僻面腫 ("deviation of the mouth and facial swelling").
- 敗漆焼之，致鼠 is said to relate to lore about crabs and lacquer and about burning crabs to attract rats, but the sense is unclear (marked [?]).
