# 神農本草經 中經 093 柞蝉 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhachan (cicada). Salty, cold. Governs fright epilepsy and night crying in children, madness, and cold and heat. It lives on willows.

The *Mingyi* says: Collected in the fifth month, steamed and dried.

## Notes

The entry on zhachan (cicada).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 癲病 (variant).
