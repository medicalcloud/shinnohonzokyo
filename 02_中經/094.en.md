# 神農本草經 中經 094 蠐螬 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qicao (grub). Salty, slightly warm. Governs malign blood and blood stasis (the *Taiping yulan* reads "blood miasma") and impediment qi; it breaks [the effects of] fractures with blood below the ribs, hard, full and painful, menstrual blockage, excess flesh in the eyes, and blue nebulae and white membranes. Also called feiqi. It lives in plains and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called feiqi (written 蜰斉) or boqi. It lives in heaps of dung and grass in households in Henei. Taken at no fixed time. Those that move backwards are good.
