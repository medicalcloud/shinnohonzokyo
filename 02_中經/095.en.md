# 神農本草經 中經 095 烏賊魚骨 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuzeiyugu (cuttlefish bone). Salty, slightly warm. Governs spotting with red and white menstrual fluid, blood blockage, genital erosion with swelling and pain, cold and heat with concretions, and childlessness. It is found in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from the Eastern Sea. Taken at no fixed time.
