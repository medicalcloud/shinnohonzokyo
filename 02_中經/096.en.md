# 神農本草經 中經 096 白殭蚕 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baijiangcan (stiff silkworm). Salty. Governs fright epilepsy and night crying in children; it removes the three worms, effaces dark spots, improves the complexion, and [treats] genital ulcers in men. It is found in plains and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Yingchuan. Those that have died of themselves are taken in the fourth month.

## Notes

The entry on baijiangcan (silkworm that has died stiff from disease).
- 減黒皯 in the base is emended to 滅黒皯 ("effaces dark spots").
- The text gives no thermal nature.
