# 神農本草經 中經 097 鮀魚甲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tuoyujia (alligator scale). Acrid, slightly warm. Governs concretions, hidden hardness, accumulations and cold and heat of the heart and abdomen, flooding in women, bleeding of five colours, pain drawing between the lower abdomen and the vagina, and sores, scabies and dead flesh. It is found in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Nanhai. Taken at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on tuoyujia, considered the scale of a crocodilian.
