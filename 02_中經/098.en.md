# 神農本草經 中經 098 樗鶏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chuji (chokei; lanternfly). Bitter, neutral. Governs pathogenic qi of the heart and abdomen and impotence; it augments essence, strengthens the will, lets one beget children, [increases] sexual desire, supplements the center and lightens the body. It lives in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It lives on tree-of-heaven trees in Henei. Collected in the seventh month and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on chuji (a lanternfly living on the tree of heaven).
