# 神農本草經 中經 099 蛞蝓 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kuoyu (slug). Salty, cold. Governs deviation of the mouth from bandit wind, dislocated sinews and prolapse of the rectum, and fright epilepsy with cramping. Also called lingli. It lives in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called tuwo or fuwo. It lives on Mount Da and under sandy and stony walls in shady places. Taken in the eighth month.
