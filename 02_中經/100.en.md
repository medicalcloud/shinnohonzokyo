# 神農本草經 中經 100 石竜子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shilongzi (skink). Salty, cold. Governs the five kinds of dribbling urinary blockage and bound evil qi; it breaks stone strangury, brings down blood and frees urination and the waterways. Also called xiyi. It lives in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shilongzi, also called shougong, shiyi or shilongzi (*Taiping yulan*). The *Mingyi* says: Also called shanlongzi, shougong or shiyi. It lives among rocks in Pingyang and on Mount Jing. Taken in the fifth month and dried on rocks.

## Notes

The entry on shilongzi (a lizard).
- In the base the *Wu Pu* and *Mingyi* quotations stand in the same paragraph.
