# 神農本草經 中經 101 木虻 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mumeng (wood gadfly). Bitter, neutral. Governs red and painful eyes, injury to the canthus with tearing, static blood and blood blockage, cold and heat with aching [base: 酸𢠹], and childlessness. Also called hunchang. It lives in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Hanzhong. Taken in the fifth month.

## Notes

The entry on mumeng (a gadfly).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 木䖟 (variant).
