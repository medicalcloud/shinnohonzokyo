# 神農本草經 中經 102 蜚虻 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Feimeng (gadfly). Bitter, slightly cold. Governs expelling static blood; it breaks and brings down blood accumulations, hard stuffiness, concretions and cold and heat, and frees the blood vessels and nine orifices. It lives in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Jiangxia. Taken in the fifth month. Those with blood in the belly are good.

## Notes

The entry on feimeng (gadfly, mengchong (bochu)), used in Didang tang (Teitoto) of the *Shanghan lun*.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 蜚䖟 (variant).
