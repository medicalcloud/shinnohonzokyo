# 神農本草經 中經 103 蜚廉 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Feilian (cockroach). Salty, cold. Governs blood stasis (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "expels downward bleeding"), hard concretions and cold and heat; it breaks accumulations, and [treats] impediment of the throat, internal cold and childlessness. It lives in rivers and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Feilianchong. Shennong and Huangdi say: it treats cold and heat in women (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It lives in Jinyang and in houses. Collected at the Beginning of Autumn.

## Notes

The entry on feilian (cockroach), different from the upper-grade herb feilian.
