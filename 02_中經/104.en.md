# 神農本草經 中經 104 䗪虫 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhechong (ground beetle). Salty, cold. Governs cold and heat of the heart and abdomen with shivering and blood accumulation with concretions; it breaks hardness, brings down blood blockage and is very good for begetting children. Also called tubie. It lives in rivers and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Zhechong, also called tubie (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called tubie. It lives in Hedong and in sand, under the walls of houses and in damp places in the soil. Dried in the sun in the tenth month.

## Notes

The entry on zhechong (tubiechong).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 蟅虫 and 地鼈 (variants).
