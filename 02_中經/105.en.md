# 神農本草經 中經 105 伏翼 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fuyi (bat). Salty, neutral. Governs dim eyes; it brightens the eyes and gives a bright light to vision at night. Taken long, it makes one joyful, charming and free of worry. Also called bianfu. It lives in river valleys (formerly in the bird section; now moved).

The *Wu Pu* says: Fuyi may live in houses. Dried in the shade after the Beginning of Summer. It treats dim eyes and gives one light in seeing at night (*Yiwen leiju*).

The *Mingyi* says: It lives on Mount Tai and in houses. Collected after the Beginning of Summer and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on fuyi (bat), which Sun moved from the birds to the insects and fish.
- 旧部禽部 in the parenthesis is taken as an error for 旧在禽部. The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 熹楽 (variant).
