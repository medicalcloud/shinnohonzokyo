# 神農本草經 中經 106 梅実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Meishi (baijitsu; mume fruit). Sour, neutral. Governs bringing down qi; it dispels heat, vexation and fullness, calms the heart, [treats] pain of the limbs and body, hemiplegia with numbness and dead flesh, and removes blue-black moles and malign diseases. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Meishi (the *Daguan bencao* reads "kernel") brightens the eyes, augments qi (*Taiping yulan*) and prevents hunger (the *Wu shi bencao* quoted in the *Daguan bencao*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hanzhong. Collected in the fifth month and dried over fire.

## Notes

The entry on meishi (wumei (ubai), smoked mume), the middle-grade fruit.
- 志 in 青黒志 is taken as 痣 (moles). The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 偏活 and 青黒土 (variants).
