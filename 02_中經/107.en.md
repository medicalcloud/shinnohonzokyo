# 神農本草經 中經 107 大豆黄巻 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dadou huangjuan (daizuoken; dried soybean sprouts). Sweet, neutral. Governs damp impediment, cramping of the sinews and knee pain.

Fresh soybean: applied to welling-abscesses and swellings; its juice, boiled and drunk, kills ghost toxins and stops pain.

Chixiaodou (sekishozu; adzuki bean): governs draining water and expelling pus and blood from welling-abscesses and swellings. It grows in plains and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Dadou huangjuan. Shennong, Huangdi, Leigong: non-toxic. Collected at no fixed time. It removes dark spots on the face. It is good combined with qianhu, wuzhuo, almonds, oyster shell, tianxiong (tenyu) and rat droppings, mixed with honey. It does not want haizao (kaiso) or longdan (ryutan). In this method, it is the sprout of soybeans first emerging from yellow earth. Fresh soybean: Shennong, Qibo: cold whether raw or cooked. Collected in the ninth month. It kills the toxin of black beans, and is not used with yuanshen (genjin). Chixiaodou: Shennong, Huangdi: salty; Leigong: sweet. Collected in the ninth month (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Da. Collected in the ninth month.

## Notes

The entry on dadou huangjuan (dried soybean sprouts), with fresh soybean and adzuki bean appended; Sun's tally counts the three as one item.
- The base heading 大黄豆巻 is emended to 大豆黄巻.
