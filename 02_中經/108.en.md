# 神農本草經 中經 108 粟米 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sumi (foxtail millet). Salty, slightly cold. Governs nourishing the kidney qi; it removes heat in the stomach and spleen and augments qi. The aged grain is bitter and governs stomach heat and dispersion-thirst and frees urination (the *Daguan bencao* has it in black characters; added on the basis of the *Wu Pu*).

The *Wu Pu* says: Aged millet. Shennong, Huangdi: bitter, non-toxic. It treats spleen heat and thirst. Millet nourishes the kidney qi (*Taiping yulan*).

## Notes

The entry on sumi (foxtail millet), which Sun added to the Classic on the basis of the *Wu Pu*.
- There is no *Mingyi* quotation.
