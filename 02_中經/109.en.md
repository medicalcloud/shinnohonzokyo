# 神農本草經 中經 109 黍米 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shumi (broomcorn millet). Sweet, warm. Governs augmenting qi and supplementing the centre. It is very heating and causes vexation (the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters; added on the basis of the *Wu Pu*).

The *Wu Pu* says: Shu. Shennong: sweet, non-toxic. Taken in the seventh month and dried in the shade. It augments the centre and supplements qi (*Taiping yulan*).

## Notes

The entry on shumi (broomcorn millet), which Sun added to the Classic on the basis of the *Wu Pu*.
