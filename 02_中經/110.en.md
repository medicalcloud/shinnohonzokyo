# 神農本草經 中經 110 蓼実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Liaoshi (knotweed seed). Acrid, warm. Governs brightening the eyes and warming the center; it lets one withstand wind and cold, and drains water qi, puffy swelling of the face and eyes, and abscesses and ulcers. Maliao removes leech-worms in the intestines and lightens the body. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Liaoshi, also called tianliao, yeliao or zeliao (*Yiwen leiju*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Leize.

## Notes

The entry on liaoshi (Polygonum seed), the first middle-grade vegetable, with maliao appended.
