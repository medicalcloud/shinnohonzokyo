# 神農本草經 中經 111 葱実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Congshi (sojitsu; scallion seed). Acrid, warm. Governs brightening the eyes and supplements insufficiency of the center. Its stem can be made into a decoction; it governs cold and heat of cold damage, brings out sweat, and [treats] wind-stroke with swelling of the face and eyes.

## Notes

The entry on congshi (scallion seed), with the stem (congbai) appended. Sun holds that it should share an entry with xie (see the tally).
- The text has no clause on where it grows, and there is no *Mingyi* quotation.
