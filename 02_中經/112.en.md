# 神農本草經 中經 112 䪥 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xie (Chinese chive). Acrid, warm. Governs metal wounds and ruined sores; it lightens the body, prevents hunger and lets one withstand aging. It grows in plains and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Lu.

## Notes

The entry on xie (Allium chinense).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 不肌 (variant, probably an error).
