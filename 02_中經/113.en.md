# 神農本草經 中經 113 水蘇 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shuisu (woundwort). Acrid, slightly warm. Governs bringing down qi; it wards off bad breath, removes toxins and wards off malign influences. Taken long, it gives access to the spirit light, lightens the body and lets one withstand ageing. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Jiezu, also called shuisu or laozu (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called jisu, laozu, jiezu or jieju. It grows in Jiuzhen. Collected in the seventh month.

## Notes

The entry on shuisu (Stachys), the last drug of the Middle Classic.
