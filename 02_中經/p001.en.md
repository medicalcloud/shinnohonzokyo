# 神農本草經 中經 p001 中薬一百二十種為臣，主養性以応人。無毒、… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The middle drugs number one hundred and twenty. They serve as ministers, govern the nourishing of one's nature and correspond to humankind. Some are without toxicity and some toxic; one weighs what is appropriate. Those who wish to check illness and supplement emaciation should base themselves on the Middle Classic.

## Notes

The introductory statement at the head of the Middle Classic, describing the nature of the middle drugs.
