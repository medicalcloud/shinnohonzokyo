# 神農本草經 中經 p002 雄黄、石流黄、雌黄、水銀、石膏、磁石、凝… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xionghuang (yuo; realgar), shiliuhuang (sulfur), cihuang (shio; orpiment), shuiyin (suigin; mercury), shigao (sekko; gypsum), cishi (magnetite), ningshuishi (gyosuiseki), yangqishi (yokiseki), konggongnie, yinnie, tiejing, lishi, changshi (choseki), fuqing (the above: fourteen middle-grade jade and stone drugs; the old [edition] has sixteen). Ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger), xi'ershi (xanthium fruit), gegen (kakkon; pueraria root), gualougen (karokon; trichosanthes root), kushen (kujin; sophora root), danggui (toki; angelica sinensis), mahuang (mao; ephedra), tongcao (tsuso), shaoyao (shakuyaku; peony root), lishi (iris seed), qumai (kubaku; dianthus), yuanshen (genjin; scrophularia), qinjiao (gentiana macrophylla), baihe (byakugo; lily bulb), zhimu (chimo; anemarrhena), beimu (baimo; fritillaria), baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica), yinyanghuo (epimedium), huangqin (ogon; scutellaria), gouji (kuseki; cibotium), shilongrui, maogen (chigaya; imperata root), ziwan (aster root), zicao (shiso; lithospermum), baijiang (patrinia), baixian (dictamnus bark), suanjiang (physalis), zishen (shisan), gaoben (kohon), shiwei (sekii; pyrrosia), bixie (dioscorea), baiwei (byakubi; cynanchum atratum), shuiping (suihyo; duckweed), wanggua, diyu (chiyu; sanguisorba), haizao (kaiso; sargassum), zelan (takuran; lycopus), fangji (boi; stephania), kuandonghua (kantoka; coltsfoot flower), mudan (botan; moutan), maxianhao, jixuecao (sekisetsuso), nüwan, wangsun, shuyangquan, juechuang, jiasu (schizonepeta), qiaogen (gyokon; the above: forty-nine middle-grade herbs; the old [edition] has forty-six). Sanggen baipi (mulberry root bark), zhuye (chikuyo; bamboo leaf), wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia), zhizi (shishi; gardenia), wuyi, zhishi (kijitsu; immature bitter orange), houpo (koboku; magnolia bark), qinpi (shinpi; ash bark), qinjiao (Sichuan pepper), shanzhuyu (sanshuyu; cornus), ziwei (campsis), zhuling (chorei; polyporus), baiji, longyan (ryugan; longan), songluo (usnea), weimao (nishikigi), hehuan (gokan; albizia) (the above: seventeen middle-grade trees; the same as in the old [edition]). Majing, lurong (rokujo; deer velvet), niujiaosai, guyangjiao, mugou yinjing, lingyangjiao (reiyokaku; antelope horn), xijiao (saikaku; rhinoceros horn) (the above: seven middle-grade animal drugs; the same as in the old [edition]). Yanshi, tianshushi (tensoshi; bat droppings) (the above: two middle-grade birds; the old [edition] has three). Weipi (hedgehog skin), lufengfang (rohobo; hornet nest), biejia (bekko; turtle shell), xie (crab), zhachan (cicada), qicao (grub), wuzeiyugu (uzokugyokotsu; cuttlefish bone), baijiangcan (byakkyosan; silkworm), tuoyujia, chuji (chokei), kuoyu (slug), shilongzi, mumeng, feimeng (gadflies), feilian (cockroach), zhechong (shachu; ground beetle), fuyi (fukuyoku; bat) (the above: seventeen middle-grade insects and fish; the old [edition] has sixteen). Meishi (baijitsu; mume) (the above: one middle-grade fruit; the same as in the old [edition]). Dadou huangjuan (daizuoken; soybean sprout), chixiaodou (sekishozu; adzuki bean), sumi (awabei; millet), shumi (shobei; glutinous millet) (the above: three middle-grade grains; the old [edition] has two). Liaoshi, congshi (sojitsu; scallion seed), xie (Chinese chive), shuisu (suiso) (the above: three middle-grade vegetables; the same as in the old [edition]).

## Notes

The list of middle-grade drug names with the tallies for each section (in parentheses).
- Many names in the list are written differently from the headings in the text (e.g. 蕪萸, 紫薇, 牛角腮, 燕尿, 天鼠尿, 作蝉, 蛇魚甲, 椿鶏 and 活蝓 for 蕪荑, 紫葳, 牛角䚡, 燕屎, 天鼠屎, 柞蝉, 鮀魚甲, 樗鶏 and 蛞蝓); the Japanese translation gives them in the heading forms.
- The tally for birds says "the old edition has three", whereas the tally in the text (paragraph 7) says "the same".
