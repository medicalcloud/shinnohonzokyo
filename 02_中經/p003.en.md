# 神農本草經 中經 p003 上玉石，中品一十四種。旧十六種。考鉄落、… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: fourteen middle-grade jade and stone drugs; the old [edition] has sixteen. On examination, tieluo and tie (iron) should be combined with tiejing.

## Notes

The tally for middle-grade jade and stone drugs. Sun combined tiejing, tieluo and tie into one entry.
