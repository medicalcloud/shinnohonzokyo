# 神農本草經 中經 p004 上草，中品四十九種。旧四十六種。考菜部仮… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: forty-nine middle-grade herbs; the old [edition] has forty-six. On examination, jiasu of the vegetable section and qiaogen (gyokon) among those set aside in the *Tang bencao* should be put here.

## Notes

The tally for middle-grade herbs.
