# 神農本草經 中經 p008 上虫、魚，中品一十七種。旧十六種。考禽部… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: seventeen middle-grade insects and fish; the old [edition] has sixteen. On examination, fuyi (fukuyoku) of the bird section should be put here.

## Notes

The tally for middle-grade insects and fish.
