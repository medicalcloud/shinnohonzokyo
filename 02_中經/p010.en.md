# 神農本草經 中經 p010 上米、穀，中品三種。旧二種，大、小豆為二… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: three middle-grade grains; the old [edition] has two, counting the large and small beans as two, and lacking sumi and shumi. Now they are added.

## Notes

The tally for middle-grade grains: dadou huangjuan (with the fresh soybean and adzuki bean attached), sumi and shumi.
