# 神農本草經 中經 p011 上菜，中品三種。旧四種，考葱実，宜与䪥同… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: three middle-grade vegetables; the old [edition] has four. On examination, congshi should be in the same entry as xie, and they are now combined; jiasu should be put in the herb section.

## Notes

The tally for middle-grade vegetables. The drugs of the Middle Classic end here.
