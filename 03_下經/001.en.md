# 神農本草經 下經 001 石灰 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shihui (lime). Acrid, warm. Governs flat-abscesses and ulcers, scabies itch, heat qi, malign sores, lai disease, dead flesh and falling eyebrows; it kills haemorrhoid worms and removes moles and polyps. Also called ehui. It is found in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called xichen. It comes from Zhongshan.

## Notes

The entry on shihui (lime), the first lower-grade drug.
- The alternative name 悪疢 in the base is emended to 悪灰. The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 疥掻 (variant).
