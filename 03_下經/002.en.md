# 神農本草經 下經 002 礜石 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yushi (arsenopyrite). Acrid, very hot. Governs cold and heat, scrofulous fistulas and erosive sores, dead flesh, wind impediment and hardness in the abdomen. Also called qingfenshi, lizhishi or guyangshi (the *Taiping yulan* quotes: "it dispels heat and kills the hundred beasts"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters). It comes from mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Baigongshi, also called shuxiang. Shennong, Qibo: acrid, toxic; Tongjun: toxic; Huangdi: sweet, toxic; Mr Li says it may come from Weixing or Shaoshi. Collected in the twelfth month (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called taibai, zeru or shiyan", and also "Mr Li: very cold; governs warm heat").

The *Mingyi* says: Also called baigongshi, taibaishi, zeru or shiyan. It comes from Hanzhong and Shaoshi. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on yushi, considered an arsenic-bearing mineral.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 塞熱 (variant, probably an error).
