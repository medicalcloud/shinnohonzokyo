# 神農本草經 下經 003 鉛丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qiandan (minium). Acrid, slightly cold. Governs upward counterflow [base: 土逆] and stomach reflux, and fright epilepsy with madness; it dispels heat and brings down qi. Refined and transformed, it returns to become jiuguang. Taken long, it gives access to the spirit light (the *Taiping yulan* quotes 吐下 and says "taken long, one becomes an immortal"). It is found in plains and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called qianhua. It comes from Shu Commandery.

## Notes

The entry on qiandan (red lead).
- 土逆 is translated following 上逆 in the Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition (the base is kept); that text also writes 癲疾 (variants).
- Jiuguang ("nine lights") is said to be the name of an elixir.
