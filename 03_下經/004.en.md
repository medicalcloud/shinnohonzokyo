# 神農本草經 下經 004 粉錫 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fenxi (lead white). Acrid, cold. Governs hidden corpse [disease] and toxic stings, and kills the three worms. Also called jiexi. Xijingbi (the knob of a tin mirror) governs blood blockage, concretions, hidden intestinal [disorders] and cessation of conception in women. It is found in mountain valleys (formerly two kinds; now combined).

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Guiyang.

## Notes

The entry on fenxi (hufen, lead white), with xijingbi appended.
