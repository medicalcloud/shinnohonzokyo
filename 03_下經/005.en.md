# 神農本草經 下經 005 代赭 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Daizhe (haematite). Bitter, cold. Governs ghost infixation, bandit wind and gu toxins; it kills spectral things, malign ghosts and toxic evil qi in the abdomen, and [treats] red discharge and spotting in women. Also called xuwan. It is found in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called xueshi. It comes from the state of Qi; reddish and bluish like a cock's comb, and lustrous. That which stains the nails without fading is good. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on daizhe (haematite).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 代赭石 (variant).
