# 神農本草經 下經 007 白堊 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bai'e (chalk). Bitter, warm. Governs cold and heat, concretions, menstrual blockage and accumulations in women. It is found in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Bai'e, also called baishan (*Yiqiejing yinyi*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called baishan (written 善). It comes from Handan. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on bai'e (chalk).
- 目閉 in the base is emended to 月閉 ("menstrual blockage").
