# 神農本草經 下經 008 冬灰 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Donghui (plant ash). Acrid, slightly warm. Governs moles and removes warts, polyps, erosive flat-abscesses and scabies itch. Also called lihui. It is found in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Fanggu.

## Notes

The entry on donghui (ash of plants).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 肬 and 疥掻 (variants).
