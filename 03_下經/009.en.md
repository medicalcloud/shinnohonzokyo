# 神農本草經 下經 009 青琅玕 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qinglanggan (blue coral-like stone). Acrid, neutral. Governs itching of the body, burns, abscess injuries, scabies itch and dead flesh. Also called shizhu. It is found in plains and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called qingzhu. It comes from Shu Commandery. Collected at no fixed time.
