# 神農本草經 下經 012 天雄 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tianxiong (tenyu; long aconite root). Acrid, warm. Governs great wind, cold-damp impediment, pain running through the joints, and hypertonicity with slackening and tension; it breaks accumulations, [treats] pathogenic qi and metal wounds, strengthens the sinews and bones, lightens the body and makes one walk vigorously. Also called baimu (the *Taiping yulan* quotes: "it increases yin qi, strengthens the will, makes one martial and brave, and one does not tire from hard labor"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Shaoshi. The root is collected in the second month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on tianxiong (a long aconite root without lateral roots).
- 瀝節痛 in the base is emended to 歴節痛 ("pain running through the joints").
