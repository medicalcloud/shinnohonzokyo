# 神農本草經 下經 013 半夏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Banxia (pinellia). Acrid, neutral. Governs cold and heat of cold damage and hardness below the heart; it brings down qi, [treats] swelling and pain of the throat, dizziness with distension of the chest, and cough with counterflow and rumbling intestines, and stops sweating. Also called diwen or shuiyu (the eight characters above are in black characters in the original edition). It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Banxia, also called hegu. It grows on Weiqiu or in the wild. The leaves are paired in threes; it begins to grow in the second month, with white flowers, round [?] (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called shigu. It grows in Huaili. The root is collected in the fifth and eighth months and dried in the sun.
