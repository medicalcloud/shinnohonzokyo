# 神農本草經 下經 014 虎掌 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huzhang (kosho; arisaema). Bitter, warm. Governs heart pain with cold and heat, bound qi, accumulations and hidden beam, injured sinews, wilting, and contraction and slackness; it frees the waterways. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Huzhang. Shennong, Leigong: bitter, non-toxic; Qibo, Tongjun: acrid, toxic. Collected at the Beginning of Autumn and in the ninth month (the *Taiping yulan* quotes: "it may grow on Mount Tai or in Yuanqu").

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hanzhong and Yuanju. Collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on huzhang (an Arisaema, akin to tiannanxing).
- "Hidden beam" (伏梁) is a kind of accumulation below the heart.
