# 神農本草經 下經 015 鳶尾 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuanwei (roof iris). Bitter, neutral. Governs gu toxins and pathogenic qi, ghost infixation and the various toxins; it breaks concretions and accumulations, removes water and expels the three worms. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Yuanwei treats gu toxins (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called wuyuan. It grows on Mount Jiuyi. Collected in the fifth month.
