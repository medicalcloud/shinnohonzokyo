# 神農本草經 下經 017 亭歴 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tingli (descurainia seed) (formerly written 葶藶; the *Taiping yulan* writes 亭歴). Acrid, cold. Governs concretions, accumulations and bound qi, [stagnation of] food and drink, and cold and heat; it breaks hardness. Also called dashi or dashi (written 適). It grows in plains, marshes and fields.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called xiali or dianhao. It grows in Gaocheng. The seeds are collected after the Beginning of Summer and dried in the shade. It is good with wine.
