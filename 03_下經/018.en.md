# 神農本草經 下經 018 桔梗 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiegeng (platycodon). Acrid, slightly warm. Governs pain of the chest and ribs like a knife stab, abdominal fullness, gurgling intestines, and fright, fear and palpitating qi (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called liru"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Jiegeng, also called fuhu, baishao, liru, gengcao or luru. Shennong, Yi He: bitter, non-toxic; Bian Que, Huangdi: salty; Qibo, Leigong: sweet, non-toxic; Mr Li: very cold. The leaves are like jini, the stem like a brush handle, purplish-red. It sprouts in the second month (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called liru, fangtu, baiyao, gengcao or jini. It grows on Mount Song and in Yuanju. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on jiegeng (platycodon).
- 蒿高 in the *Mingyi* quotation is taken as an error for 嵩高 (Mount Song).
