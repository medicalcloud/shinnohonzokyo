# 神農本草經 下經 019 莨蕩子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Langdangzi (henbane seed). Bitter, cold. Governs toothache with emerging worms and flesh impediment with hypertonicity; it makes one walk vigorously and see ghosts. Eaten in quantity, it makes one run about madly. Taken long, it lightens the body, one runs as fast as a galloping horse, the will is strengthened, strength augmented, and one has access to the spirits. Also called hengtang. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called xingtang. It grows by the seashore and in Yongzhou. The seeds are collected in the fifth month.

## Notes

The entry on langdangzi (henbane seed), describing its hallucinogenic action.
