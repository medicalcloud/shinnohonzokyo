# 神農本草經 下經 020 草蒿 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Caohao (sweet wormwood). Bitter, cold. Governs scabies itch, itchy scabs and malign sores; it kills worms, [dispels] heat lodged between the bones and joints, and brightens the eyes. Also called qinghao or fangkui. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Huayin.

## Notes

The entry on caohao (qinghao, Artemisia).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 疥掻 and 殺蝨 (variants).
