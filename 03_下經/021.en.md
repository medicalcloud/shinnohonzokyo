# 神農本草經 下經 021 旋覆花 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xuanfuhua (inula flower). Salty, warm. Governs bound qi, fullness below the ribs and fright palpitations; it dispels water, removes cold and heat between the five zang, supplements the centre and brings down qi. Also called jinfeicao or shengshen. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called daishen. It grows in plains and marshes. The flowers are collected in the fifth month and dried in the sun; ready in twenty days.

## Notes

The entry on xuanfuhua (inula flower), a different drug from xuanhua in the Upper Classic.
