# 神農本草經 下經 022 藜芦 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lilu (veratrum). Acrid, cold. Governs gu toxins, cough with counterflow, diarrhoea with intestinal afflux, scalp sores and scabies itch, and malign sores; it kills the various gu toxins and removes dead flesh. Also called congran. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Lilu, also called congkui, fenglu or huikui (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called shancong or gongran"). Shennong, Leigong: acrid, toxic (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "Huangdi: toxic"); Qibo: salty, toxic; Mr Li: very cold, very toxic; Bian Que: bitter, toxic, very cold. The leaves and root are small and joined (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "the root is collected in the second month").

The *Mingyi* says: Also called congtan or shancong. It grows on Mount Tai. The root is collected in the third month and dried in the shade.
