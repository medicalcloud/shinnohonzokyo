# 神農本草經 下經 023 鉤吻 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gouwen (gelsemium). Acrid, warm. Governs metal wounds and tetany in childbirth, being struck by malign wind, cough with counterflow and rising qi and water swelling; it kills ghost infixation (formerly written 疰; the *Taiping yulan* writes 注, which is correct) and gu toxins. Also called yege. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: [In] Qin [it is called] gouchang, also called dugen or yege. Shennong: acrid; Leigong: toxic, kills people. It grows in the mountains of Nanyue or in Yizhou. The leaves are like kudzu; the red stems are as large as arrows and square; the root is yellow. It may grow in Dongzhi of Kuaiji. Collected in the first month (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Fugao and in the eastern wilds of Kuaiji.

## Notes

The entry on gouwen, a highly toxic plant.
- 乳痓 is understood as convulsions in childbirth.
