# 神農本草經 下經 024 射干 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shegan (yakan; belamcanda rhizome). Bitter, neutral. Governs cough with counterflow and rising qi, throat impediment and sore throat with inability to breathe; it disperses tense qi and [treats] pathogen counterflow in the abdomen and great heat from food and drink. Also called wushan (usen) or wupu. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shegan, also called huangyuan (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called wusha, wuchui or caojiang. It grows in the fields of Nanyang. The root is collected on the third day of the third month and dried in the shade.
