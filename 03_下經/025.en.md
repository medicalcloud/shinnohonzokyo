# 神農本草經 下經 025 蛇合 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shehan (cinquefoil). Bitter, slightly cold. Governs fright epilepsy with cold and heat and evil qi; it dispels heat and [treats] metal wounds, flat-abscesses, haemorrhoids and scrofulous fistulas, malign sores and scalp sores. Also called shexian. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Yizhou. Collected in the eighth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on shehan (written 蛇合; the original note says 合 stands for 含).
