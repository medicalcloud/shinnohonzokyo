# 神農本草經 下經 026 恒山 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hengshan (dichroa root) (formerly written 常山; the *Taiping yulan* writes 恒山, which is correct). Bitter, cold. Governs cold and heat of cold damage, warm malaria with outbursts of heat, ghost toxins, and phlegm binding in the chest with vomiting counterflow. Also called hucao. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Hengshan, also called qiye. Shennong, Qibo: bitter; Mr Li: very cold; Tongjun: acrid, toxic. Collected in the second and eighth months.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Yizhou and Hanzhong. The root is collected in the eighth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on hengshan (changshan, dichroa root), regarded as a drug for malaria.
- 常 replaces 恒 to avoid a Han imperial taboo; Sun restores 恒山.
