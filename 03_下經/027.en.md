# 神農本草經 下經 027 蜀漆 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shuqi (shokushitsu; dichroa leaf). Acrid, neutral. Governs malaria and cough with counterflow with cold and heat, hard concretions, stuffiness binding and accumulations in the abdomen, and pathogenic qi, gu toxins and ghost infixation (formerly written 疰; the *Taiping yulan* writes 蛀). It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: The leaves of shuqi, also called hengshan. Shennong, Qibo, Leigong: acrid, toxic; Huangdi: acrid; one classic: sour. It resembles lacquer leaves and blue indigo. Collected in the fifth month (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Jiangling and on Mount Chang in Hanzhong of Shu. It is the sprout [of hengshan]. The leaves are collected in the fifth month and dried in the shade.
