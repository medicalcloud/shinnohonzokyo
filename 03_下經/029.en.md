# 神農本草經 下經 029 白蘞 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bailian (ampelopsis root). Bitter, neutral. Governs welling-abscesses and swellings and flat-abscesses and sores; it disperses bound qi, stops pain and dispels heat, and [treats] redness in the eyes, fright epilepsy in children, warm malaria, and swelling and pain of the vagina. Also called tuhe or baicao. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called baigen or kunlun. It grows on Mount Heng. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the sun.
