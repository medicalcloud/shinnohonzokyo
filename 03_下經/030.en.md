# 神農本草經 下經 030 青葙子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qingxiangzi (celosia). Bitter, slightly cold. Governs evil qi, heat in the skin and wind itch with itching of the body, and kills the three worms. The seed, called cao jueming, treats blueness of the lips and mouth. Also called caohao or qihao. It grows in level valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows beside roads. The stems and leaves are collected on the third day of the third month and dried in the shade; the seeds are collected on the sixth day of the fifth month.
