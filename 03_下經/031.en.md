# 神農本草經 下經 031 雚菌 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guanjun (reed fungus). Salty, neutral. Governs heart pain; it warms the centre and removes long worms, baizong [?], pinworms, snake-bite and sting toxins, concretions and the various worms. Also called guanlu. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Donghai, Bohai and Zhangwu. Collected in the eighth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on guanjun, considered a fungus growing on reeds.
- 長患 in the base is emended to 長虫 ("long worms"). The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 白㿅 (variant).
