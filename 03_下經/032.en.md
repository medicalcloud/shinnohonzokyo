# 神農本草經 下經 032 白芨 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baiji (bletilla). Bitter, neutral. Governs welling-abscesses and swellings, malign sores and ruined flat-abscesses, damaged genitals, dead flesh, evil qi in the stomach, bandit wind and ghost strike, and paralysis with slackness that does not retract. Also called gangen or lianjicao. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shennong: bitter; Huangdi: acrid; Mr Li: very cold; Leigong: acrid, non-toxic. The stem and leaves resemble fresh ginger and veratrum. In the tenth month the flowers grow straight up, purplish-red; the root is white and joined. Collected in the second, eighth and ninth months.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on the northern mountains, in Yuanju and on Mount Yue.

## Notes

The entry on baiji (Bletilla rhizome).
- 痱緩不収 is understood as paralysis and slackness of the limbs after wind-stroke.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 賦風 and 甘草 (variants, probably errors).
