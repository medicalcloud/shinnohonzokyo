# 神農本草經 下經 033 大戟 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Daji (euphorbia root). Bitter, cold. Governs gu toxins, the twelve kinds of water, swelling and fullness with acute pain, accumulations, wind-stroke, aching pain of the skin, and vomiting counterflow. Also called qiongju (note: that this entry lacks the three characters "grows in rivers and marshes" suggests that in antiquity it may have formed one entry with zeqi).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Chang. The root is collected in the twelfth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on daji (Euphorbia pekinensis root).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 邛鉅 (variant).
