# 神農本草經 下經 034 沢漆 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zeqi (sun spurge). Bitter, slightly cold. Governs heat of the skin, abdominal enlargement with water qi, puffy swelling of the four limbs, face and eyes, and insufficiency of yin qi in men. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called qijing; it is the sprout of daji (daigeki). It grows on Mount Tai. The stems and leaves are collected on the third day of the third month and the seventh day of the seventh month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on zeqi (sun spurge).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 丈大 (variant, probably an error).
