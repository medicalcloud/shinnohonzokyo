# 神農本草經 下經 035 茵芋 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yinyu (skimmia). Bitter, warm. Governs evil qi of the five zang, cold and heat of the heart and abdomen, emaciation like malaria occurring at fixed times, and wind-damp impediment pain of the various joints. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Yinyu, also called beigong. Slightly warm, toxic. In form like mangcao but finer and softer (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called guancao or beigong. It grows on Mount Tai. The leaves are collected on the third day of the third month and dried in the shade.
