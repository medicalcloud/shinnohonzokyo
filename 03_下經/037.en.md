# 神農本草經 下經 037 蕘花 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Raohua (joka; wikstroemia flower). Bitter, neutral, cold. Governs cold damage and warm malaria; it drains the twelve kinds of water, breaks accumulations, great hardness and concretions, flushes out aggregations, food and drink lodged in the intestines and stomach, and cold, heat and pathogenic qi, and frees the waterways. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Xianyang and in Zhongmou of Henan. The flowers are collected in the sixth month and dried in the shade.
