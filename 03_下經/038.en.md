# 神農本草經 下經 038 牙子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yazi (agrimony root bud). Bitter, cold. Governs evil qi and heat qi, scabies itch, malign ulcers and haemorrhoidal sores, and removes white worms. Also called langya. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Langya, also called zhilan, langchi, quanya or baozi. Shennong, Huangdi: bitter, toxic; Tongjun: perhaps salty; Qibo, Leigong, Bian Que: non-toxic. It grows in Yuanju. The leaves are blue-green and the root yellow-red; flowers in the sixth and seventh months; the fruit is black in the eighth month. The root is collected in the first and eighth months (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called langchi, langzi or quanya. It grows in Huainan and Yuanju. The root is collected in the eighth month and dried in the sun.
