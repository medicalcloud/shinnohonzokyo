# 神農本草經 下經 039 羊躑躅 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangzhizhu (Chinese azalea flower). Acrid, warm. Governs bandit wind in the skin with creeping pain, warm malaria, malign toxins and the various impediments. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: The flower of yangzhizhu. Shennong, Leigong: acrid, toxic. It grows in Huainan. It treats bandit wind, malign toxins and the various evil qi (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called yuzhi. It grows on the Taihang Mountains and the mountains of Huainan. The flowers are collected in the third month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on yangzhizhu (Rhododendron molle flower), a toxic drug.
