# 神農本草經 下經 040 商陸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shanglu (pokeweed root). Acrid, neutral. Governs water distension, mounting-conglomeration and impediment; applied hot, it removes welling-abscesses and swellings, and it kills ghostly spectral things. Also called zhugen or yehu. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: That shaped like a walking person has spiritual power. It grows in Xianyang.
