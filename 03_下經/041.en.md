# 神農本草經 下經 041 羊蹄 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangti (yotei; dock root). Bitter, cold. Governs baldness and scabies itch, dispels heat, and [treats] genital erosion in women (the *Taiping yulan* has 無 for these four characters). Also called dongfangsu, lianchonglu or guimu. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: [Also] called xu. It grows in Chenliu.

## Notes

The entry on yangti (Rumex root).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 疥掻 (variant).
