# 神農本草經 下經 042 萹蓄 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bianxu (knotgrass). Acrid, neutral. Governs spreading sores, scabies itch, and flat-abscesses and haemorrhoids, and kills the three worms (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called pianzhu"; not in the *Daguan* edition). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Bianxu, also called xubian or bianman (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Donglai. Collected in the fifth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes
