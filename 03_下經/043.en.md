# 神農本草經 下經 043 狼毒 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Langdu (wolfsbane). Acrid, neutral. Governs cough with counterflow and rising qi; it breaks accumulations, [stagnant] food and drink, cold and heat, water qi, malign sores, scrofulous fistulas and erosive flat-abscesses, ghostly spectres and gu toxins, and kills flying birds and running beasts. Also called xudu. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Qinting and Fenggao. The root is collected in the second and eighth months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on langdu, a toxic drug.
