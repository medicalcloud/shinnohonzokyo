# 神農本草經 下經 044 白頭翁 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baitouweng (pulsatilla root). Bitter, warm. Governs warm malaria, madness, cold and heat, concretions and accumulations and goitre qi; it drives out blood, stops pain and treats metal wounds. Also called yezhangren or huwang shizhe. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Baitouweng, also called yezhangren or naihecao. Shennong, Bian Que: bitter, non-toxic. It grows in the river valleys of Mount Song. It breaks qi, madness and cold and heat, and stops pain (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called naihecao. It grows on high mountains and in fields. Collected in the fourth month.

## Notes

The entry on baitouweng (pulsatilla root).
- 易狂 is read as 狂易 ("madness"). The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition repeats 易 and reads 生川谷 (variants).
