# 神農本草經 下經 045 鬼臼 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guijiu (dysosma). Acrid, warm. Governs killing gu toxins, ghost infixation and spectral things; it wards off malign qi and inauspicious influences, drives out pathogen and resolves the hundred toxins. Also called juexi, mamu dugong or jiujiu. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Also called jiujiu, tianjiu, quexi, mamugong or jiedu. It grows in the mountain valleys of Jiuzhen and in Yuanju. The root is collected in the second and eighth months (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called tianjiu or jiedu. It grows in Jiuzhen and Yuanju. The root is collected in the second and eighth months.
