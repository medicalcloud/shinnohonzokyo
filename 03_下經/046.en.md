# 神農本草經 下經 046 羊桃 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangtao (yoto; kiwi vine). Bitter, cold. Governs blazing heat with sudden redness of the body, wind edema and accumulations, and malign ulcers, and dispels heat in children. Also called guitao or yangchang. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called changchu, yuyi or yaoyi. It grows in mountain forests and fields. Collected in the second month and dried in the shade.
