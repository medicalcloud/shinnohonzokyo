# 神農本草經 下經 047 女青 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Nüqing. Acrid, neutral. Governs gu toxins; it drives out evil and malign qi, kills ghosts and warm malaria and wards off inauspicious influences. Also called quepiao (the *Taiping yulan* reads 閭).

The *Wu Pu* says: Nüqing, also called huoyouzhi. Shennong, Huangdi: acrid (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It is the root of shexian. It grows in Zhuya. Collected in the eighth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on nüqing; its identification is uncertain.
- The text has no clause on where it grows.
