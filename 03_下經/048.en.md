# 神農本草經 下經 048 連翹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lianqiao (forsythia fruit). Bitter, neutral. Governs cold and heat with scrofulous fistulas, scrofula and welling-abscesses and swellings, malign sores, goitre and tumours, and bound heat with gu toxins. Also called yiqiao, lanhua, zhi or sanlian. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called zhegen. It grows on Mount Tai. Collected in the eighth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on lianqiao (forsythia fruit).
- 蘭花 in the base is emended to 蘭華.
