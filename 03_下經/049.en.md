# 神農本草經 下經 049 蘭茹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lüru (euphorbia ebracteolata) (the *Taiping yulan* writes 閭, which is correct). Acrid, cold. Governs eating away malign flesh, ruined sores and dead flesh; it kills scabies worms, expels pus and malign blood, dispels great wind and heat qi, and [treats] forgetfulness with unhappiness. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Lüru, also called lilou or quju. Shennong: acrid; Qibo: sour, salty, toxic; Mr Li: very cold. Collected in the second month. The leaves are round and yellow; it is four or five chi tall; the leaves face one another in fours. In the fourth month the flowers are yellow, in the fifth month the fruit black; the root is yellow and has juice, also yellow. The root is collected in the third and fifth months. That with a black head is good (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called quju (written 拠) or lilou. It grows in Dai Commandery. Collected in the fifth month and dried in the shade.
