# 神農本草經 下經 050 烏韭 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wujiu (moss). Sweet, cold. Governs alternating fever and chills of the skin and frees the qi of the small intestine and bladder. It grows on rocks in mountain valleys.

## Notes

The entry on wujiu, considered a moss.
- There is no *Mingyi* quotation; Sun's note is not translated.
