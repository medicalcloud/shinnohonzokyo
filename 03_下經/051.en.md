# 神農本草經 下經 051 鹿藿 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Luhuo (rhynchosia). Bitter, neutral. Governs gu toxins, pain of the lumbus and abdomen in women, unhappiness, intestinal abscess, scrofula (the *Taiping yulan* reads 歴) and ulcer qi. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Wen.
