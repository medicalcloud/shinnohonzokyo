# 神農本草經 下經 052 蚤休 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zaoxiu (paris rhizome). Bitter, slightly cold. Governs fright epilepsy with shaking of the head and playing with the tongue, heat qi in the abdomen, madness, abscesses and sores and genital erosion; it expels the three worms and removes snake venom. Also called chixiu. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Shan[yang?] and Yuanju.

## Notes

The entry on zaoxiu (Paris rhizome).
- 生山 in the *Mingyi* quotation seems to lack a character (marked [?]). The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 癲疾 (variant).
