# 神農本草經 下經 053 石長生 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shichangsheng (fern). Salty, slightly cold. Governs cold and heat, malign sores and fire heat, and wards off ghostly qi and inauspicious influences (the *Taiping yulan* reads "wards off malign qi, inauspicious influences and ghost toxins"). Also called dancao (the *Taiping yulan* quotes danshacao). It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shichangsheng. Shennong: bitter; Leigong: acrid; one classic: sweet. It grows in Xianyang (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Xianyang.

## Notes

The entry on shichangsheng, considered a fern.
