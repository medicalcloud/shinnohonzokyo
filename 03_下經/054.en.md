# 神農本草經 下經 054 陸英 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Luying (elder). Bitter, cold. Governs the various impediments between the bones, hypertonicity, aching and soreness of the four limbs, cold pain of the knees, impotence, shortness of breath with insufficiency, and swelling of the legs. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Xiong'er and in Yuanju. Collected at the Beginning of Autumn. It also says: Shuodiao is sour, warm and toxic. Also called jin (current editions wrongly write 堇) or ji. It grows in fields. The leaves are collected in spring and summer, the stems and roots in autumn and winter.

## Notes

The entry on luying (Sambucus), with the *Mingyi*'s shuodiao (a related plant) appended.
- In 一名堇（今本誤作堇）, the "correct" and "wrong" characters appear the same; the original character is unclear.
