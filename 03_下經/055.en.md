# 神農本草經 下經 055 藎草 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jincao (hairy grass). Bitter, neutral. Governs long-standing cough with rising qi and dyspnea counterflow, chronic cold, fright palpitations, scabs and scabies, white baldness and ulcer qi, and kills small worms in the skin. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Wangchu, also called huangcao. Shennong and Leigong say: it grows in the mountain valleys of Mount Tai; it treats body heat and pathogenic qi and body heat qi in children (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It can dye yellow and make jingong. It grows in Qingyi. Collected in the ninth and tenth months.
