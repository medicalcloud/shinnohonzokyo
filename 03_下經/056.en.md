# 神農本草經 下經 056 牛扁 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Niubian. Bitter, slightly cold. Governs sores and heat qi of the skin; it can be made into a bath, kills cattle lice and small worms, and also treats cattle diseases. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Guiyang.

## Notes

The entry on niubian.
- 味甘 in the base is emended to 味苦 ("bitter").
