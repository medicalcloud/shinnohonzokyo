# 神農本草經 下經 057 夏枯草 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiakucao (kagoso; prunella). Bitter, acrid. Governs cold and heat, scrofula, scrofulous fistulas and head sores; it breaks concretions and disperses goitre, bound qi, swollen legs and damp impediment. It lightens the body. Also called xiju or naidong. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called yanmian. It grows in Shu Commandery. Collected in the fourth month.

## Notes

The entry on xiakucao (Prunella).
- The text gives no thermal nature.
