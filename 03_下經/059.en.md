# 神農本草經 下經 059 巴豆 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Badou (croton seed). Acrid, warm. Governs cold damage, warm malaria and cold and heat; it breaks concretions, bound gatherings, hard accumulations, lodged fluids and phlegm aggregations, [treats] abdominal enlargement with water distension, flushes and refines the five zang and six fu, opens blockages, frees the pathways of water and grain, removes malign flesh, dispels ghost toxins, gu infixation and evil things (the *Taiping yulan* reads "ghost toxins and evil infixation"), and kills insects and fish. Also called bashu (written 叔; [some editions] write 椒, the *Taiping yulan* 菽). It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Badou, also called bashu. Shennong, Qibo, Tongjun: acrid, toxic; Huangdi: sweet, toxic; Mr Li: governs warm, hot, cold [?]. The leaves are like soybean. Collected in the eighth month (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Ba Commandery. Collected in the eighth month and dried in the shade. For use, the core and skin are removed.

## Notes

The entry on badou (croton seed), a drastic purgative.
- 悪内 in the base is emended to 悪肉 ("malign flesh"). The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 淡癖 and 蕩練 (variants).
- Wu Pu's 李氏：主温熱寒 is hard to construe (marked [?]).
