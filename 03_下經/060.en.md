# 神農本草經 下經 060 蜀菽 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shujiao (Sichuan pepper). Acrid, warm. Governs evil qi and cough with counterflow; it warms the centre, drives out [pain of] the bones and joints, [treats] dead flesh of the skin and cold-damp impediment pain, and brings down qi. Taken long, the hair does not turn white, the body is lightened and years are added. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called bajiao or tangyi. It grows in Wudu and Ba Commandery. The fruit is collected in the eighth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on shujiao (Sichuan pepper), written 蜀菽 in Sun's heading.
