# 神農本草經 下經 061 皂莢 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zaojia (sokyo; gleditsia pod). Acrid, salty, warm. Governs wind impediment, dead flesh and pathogenic qi, and head wind with tearing; it frees the nine orifices and kills spectral things. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Yongzhou and Zou County of Lu. Those like pig's teeth are good. Collected in the ninth and tenth months and dried in the shade.
