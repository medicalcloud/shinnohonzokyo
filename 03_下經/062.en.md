# 神農本草經 下經 062 柳花 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Liuhua (willow catkin). Bitter, cold. Governs wind water with jaundice and heat and blackness of the face. Also called liuxu. The leaves govern scabies and scabby sores of horses; the fruit governs ruptured abscesses and expels pus and blood; the juice of the seeds treats thirst. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Langye.

## Notes

The entry on liuhua (willow catkin), with the leaves, fruit and seed juice appended.
