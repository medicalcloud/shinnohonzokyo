# 神農本草經 下經 063 楝実 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lianshi (melia fruit). Bitter, cold. Governs warm disease and cold damage, great heat with vexation and mania; it kills the three worms and scabies ulcers and frees urination and the waterways. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Jing.
