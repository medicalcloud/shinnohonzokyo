# 神農本草經 下經 064 郁李仁 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuliren (ikurinin; bush cherry seed). Sour, neutral. Governs abdominal enlargement with water swelling and puffy swelling of the face, eyes and four limbs, and frees urination and the waterways. The root governs swollen gums and caries and firms the teeth. Also called jueli. It grows in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Yuli, also called queli, chexiali or di (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called chexiali or di. It grows on high mountains and hills. The root is collected in the fifth and sixth months.

## Notes

The entry on yuliren (bush cherry seed).
