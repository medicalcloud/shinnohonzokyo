# 神農本草經 下經 065 莽草 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mangcao (star anise leaf). Acrid, warm. Governs head wind, welling-abscesses and swellings, breast abscess and mounting-conglomeration; it dispels bound qi and scabies itch (the *Taiping yulan* has the two characters 癰瘡) and kills insects and fish. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Mangcao, also called chuncao. Shennong: acrid; Leigong, Tongjun: bitter, toxic. It grows in the mountain valleys of Shanggu or in Yuanju. Collected in the fifth month. It treats wind (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called mi or chuncao. It grows in Shanggu and Yuanju. The leaves are collected in the fifth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on mangcao (Illicium leaf), a toxic drug.
