# 神農本草經 下經 067 桐葉 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tongye (toyo; paulownia leaf). Bitter, cold. Governs malign erosion and sores on the skin of the genitals, governs the five kinds of hemorrhoids and kills the three worms. The flowers govern being applied to sores of pigs; fed to pigs, they make them three times as fat and large. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Tongbai.

## Notes

The entry on tongye (paulownia leaf), with the flowers appended.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 華 and 傅豬創 (variants).
