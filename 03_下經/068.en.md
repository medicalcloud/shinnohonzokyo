# 神農本草經 下經 068 梓白皮 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zibaipi (shihakuhi; catalpa bark). Bitter, cold. Governs heat and removes the three worms. The leaves, pounded, are applied to sores of pigs; fed to pigs, they make them three times as fat and large. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Henei.

## Notes

The entry on zibaipi (catalpa root bark), with the leaves appended.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 傅豬創 (variant).
