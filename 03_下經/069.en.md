# 神農本草經 下經 069 石南 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shinan (photinia leaf). Acrid, bitter. Governs nourishing the kidney qi, internal damage and weakness of yin, and benefits the sinews, bones, skin and hair. The fruit kills gu toxins, breaks accumulations and drives out wind impediment. Also called guimu. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Huayin. The fruit is collected in the second and fourth months and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on shinan (Photinia leaf).
- The text gives no thermal nature. The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition has the heading 石南辛 (variant).
