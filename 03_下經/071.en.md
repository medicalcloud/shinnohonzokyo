# 神農本草經 下經 071 溲疏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Soushu (deutzia). Acrid, cold. Governs heat in the skin of the body; it dispels evil qi and stops enuresis. It can be made into a bath. It grows in mountain valleys, fields and vacant ground on old mounds.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called jugu. It grows on Mount Xiong'er. Collected in the fourth month.
