# 神農本草經 下經 072 鼠李 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shuli (buckthorn). Governs cold and heat and scrofulous sores. It grows in fields.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shuli, also called niuli (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called niuli, shuzi or bi. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on shuli (Rhamnus).
- The text gives no flavour or nature.
