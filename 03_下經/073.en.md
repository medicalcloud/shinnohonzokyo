# 神農本草經 下經 073 薬実根 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yaoshigen. Acrid, warm. Governs pathogenic qi and the various impediments with soreness and aching; it joins severed injuries and supplements the bones and marrow. Also called lianmu. It grows in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Shu Commandery. Collected at no fixed time.

## Notes

The entry on yaoshigen; its identification is unknown.
