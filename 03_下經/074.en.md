# 神農本草經 下經 074 欒花 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Luanhua (goldenrain flower). Bitter, cold. Governs eye pain, tearing and injury to the canthus, and disperses swelling of the eyes. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hanzhong. Collected in the fifth month.

## Notes

The entry on luanhua (Koelreuteria flower).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 欒華 (variant).
