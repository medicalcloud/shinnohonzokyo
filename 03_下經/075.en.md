# 神農本草經 下經 075 蔓椒 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Manjiao (zanthoxylum nitidum). Bitter, warm. Governs wind-cold-damp impediment and aching running through the joints, and dispels reversal qi of the four limbs and knee pain. Also called shijiao. It grows in river valleys and among hills and tombs [base: 家].

The *Mingyi* says: Also called zhujiao, zhijiao or goujiao. It grows in Yunzhong. The stems and roots are collected and used to brew wine.

## Notes

The entry on manjiao.
- 癧節 and 家椒 in the base are emended to 歴節 and 豕椒 (see the emendations).
- 邱家間 is translated "among hills and tombs" following 邱冢間 in the Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition (the base is kept).
