# 神農本草經 下經 076 豚卵 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tunluan (pig testicle). Bitter, warm. Governs fright epilepsy and madness and ghost infixation and gu toxins; it dispels cold and heat, running piglet and the five kinds of dribbling urinary blockage, and pathogenic qi with cramping. Also called tundian. The hanging hoof governs the five kinds of hemorrhoids, hidden heat in the intestines, intestinal abscess and internal erosion.

## Notes

The entry on tunluan (pig testicle), with the hoof appended; a lower-grade animal drug.
- The text has no clause on where it is found, and there is no *Mingyi* quotation.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 癲疾 (variant).
