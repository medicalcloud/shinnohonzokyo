# 神農本草經 下經 077 麋脂 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mizhi (elk fat). Acrid, warm. Governs welling-abscesses and swellings, malign sores and dead flesh, impediment from cold, wind and damp, contraction and slackness of the four limbs that do not retract, head wind and swelling qi, and opens the interstices. Also called guanzhi. It is found in mountain valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Nanshan and the borders of Huainan. Taken in the tenth month.

## Notes

The entry on mizhi (elk fat).
- 腠理 in the base is emended to 湊理, in line with Sun's usage (see the emendation).
- 雀 in the *Mingyi*'s 雀淮南辺 seems superfluous and is omitted in translation (the base is kept).
