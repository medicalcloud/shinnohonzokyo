# 神農本草經 下經 078 鸓鼠 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leishu (flying squirrel). Governs causing abortion and makes childbirth easy. It is found in level valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Shandu.

## Notes

The entry on leishu (a flying squirrel).
- The text gives no flavor or nature. The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 鼺鼠 (variant).
