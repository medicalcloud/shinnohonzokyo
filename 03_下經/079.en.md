# 神農本草經 下經 079 六畜毛蹄甲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Liuchu maotijia (hair and hooves of the six domestic animals). Salty, neutral. Governs ghost infixation and gu toxins, cold and heat with fright epilepsy, and madness with tetany and manic running. Camel hair is even better.

## Notes

The entry on the hair and hooves of the six domestic animals.
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition writes 癲痓 (variant).
