# 神農本草經 下經 080 蝦蟆 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hama (toad). Acrid, cold. Governs pathogenic qi; it breaks hard concretions, blood [accumulations], welling-abscesses and swellings and genital sores. Taking it, one does not suffer from febrile illness. It lives in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called chanchu, qiu, qufu or kulong. It lives in rivers and lakes. Taken on the fifth day of the fifth month and dried in the shade. Those moving eastwards are good.

## Notes

The entry on hama (toad), the first lower-grade insect or fish.
