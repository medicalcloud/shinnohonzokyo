# 神農本草經 下經 081 馬刀 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Madao (freshwater mussel). Acrid, slightly cold (the *Taiping yulan* has the two characters "supplements the centre"; the *Daguan* edition has them in black). Governs spotting with red and white discharge and cold and heat; it breaks stone strangury and kills birds, beasts and thieving rats. It lives in pools and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Madao, also called qige. Shennong, Qibo, Tongjun: salty, toxic; Bian Que: slightly cold, very toxic. It lives in pools and marshes, rivers and seas. Collected at no fixed time (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called mage. It lives in rivers and lakes and in the Eastern Sea. Collected at no fixed time.
