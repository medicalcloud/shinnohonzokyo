# 神農本草經 下經 082 蛇蛻 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shetui (snake slough). Salty, neutral. Governs the hundred and twenty kinds of fright epilepsy in children, tugging and slackening, madness, cold and heat, intestinal haemorrhoids, worm toxins and snake epilepsy. Dry-roasted over fire it is good. Also called longziyi, shefu, longzi danyi or gongpi. It is found in river valleys and fields.

The *Wu Pu* says: Shetui, also called longzi danyi, gongpi, shefu (written 附), shejin, longpi or long danyi (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called longzipi. It comes from Jingzhou. It is good taken on the fifth and fifteenth days of the fifth month.
