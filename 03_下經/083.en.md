# 神農本草經 下經 083 蚯蚓 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qiuyin (earthworm). Salty, cold. Governs snake conglomeration; it removes the three worms, hidden corpse [disease], ghost infixation and gu toxins, and kills long worms. It still turns into water of itself. It lives in flat earth.

The *Wu Pu* says: Qiuyin, also called baijing tangyin or fuyin (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called tulong. Taken in the second month and dried in the shade.
