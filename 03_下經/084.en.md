# 神農本草經 下經 084 蠮螉 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yeweng (potter wasp). Acrid, neutral. Governs long-standing deafness, cough with counterflow and toxic qi; it draws out thorns and brings out sweat. It lives in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called tufeng. It lives on Mount Xiong'er, in Zangke or in houses.

## Notes

The entry on yeweng (a mud-dauber wasp).
- The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 生山谷 (variant).
