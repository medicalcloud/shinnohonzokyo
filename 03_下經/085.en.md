# 神農本草經 下經 085 蜈蚣 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wugong (goko; centipede). Acrid, warm. Governs ghost infixation and gu toxins; it devours the toxins of the various snakes, worms and fish, kills ghostly things, old spectres and warm malaria, and removes the three worms (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called zhizhang"; in the *Daguan* edition this is under shuizhi (suitetsu)). It lives in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Dawu and Jiangnan. Those with red heads and feet are good.

## Notes

The entry on wugong (centipede).
