# 神農本草經 下經 086 水蛭 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shuizhi (leech). Salty, neutral. Governs expelling malign blood, static blood and menstrual blockage (the *Taiping yulan* reads "water blockage"); it breaks blood conglomerations and accumulations, [treats] childlessness and frees the waterways. It lives in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called ji or zhizhang. It lives in Leize. Collected in the fifth and sixth months and dried in the sun.

## Notes

The entry on shuizhi (leech), used in Didang tang of the *Shanghan lun*.
