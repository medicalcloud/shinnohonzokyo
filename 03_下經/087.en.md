# 神農本草經 下經 087 班苗 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Banmao (blister beetle). Acrid, cold. Governs cold and heat, ghost infixation and gu toxins, scrofulous fistulas and malign sores, and erosive flat-abscesses with dead flesh, and breaks stone dribbling blockage. Also called longwei. It lives in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Banmao, also called banhao, longhao, banmiao, shengfa, panqiong or yanqing. Shennong: acrid; Qibo: salty; Tongjun: toxic; Bian Que: sweet, very toxic. It lives in the river valleys of Henei or among waters and stones.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Hedong. Taken in the eighth month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on banmao (blister beetle), a toxic drug.
