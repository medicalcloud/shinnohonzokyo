# 神農本草經 下經 088 貝子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Beizi (cowrie shell). Salty, neutral. Governs nebulae of the eyes, ghost infixation and gu toxins, abdominal pain, bleeding and the five kinds of dribbling urinary blockage, and frees the waterways. Burned before use it is good. It lives in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called beichi. It comes from the Eastern Sea.

## Notes

The entry on beizi (cowrie shell).
- 虫毒 in the base is emended to 蠱毒 ("gu toxins").
