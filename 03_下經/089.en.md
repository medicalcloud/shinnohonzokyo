# 神農本草經 下經 089 石蚕 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shican (caddisfly larva). Salty, cold. Governs the five kinds of dribbling urinary blockage; it breaks the five strangury conditions, causes abortion, internally resolves bound qi, frees the waterways and dispels heat. Also called shashi. It lives in pools and marshes. The *Wu Pu* says: Shican, also called shashi. Shennong, Leigong: sour, non-toxic. It lives in Hanzhong. It treats the five strangury conditions, breaks [base: 破随] internal bound qi, frees the waterways and dispels heat (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from the Jiang and Han.

## Notes

The entry on shican (considered a caddisfly larva).
- The alternative name 水蝨 in the base is emended to 沙蝨. The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 破石淋 (variant).
- In the base, Wu Pu's text stands in the same paragraph as the main text.
