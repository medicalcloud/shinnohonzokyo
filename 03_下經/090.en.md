# 神農本草經 下經 090 雀甕 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Queweng (jakuo; slug-moth cocoon). Sweet, neutral. Governs fright epilepsy in children, cold and heat with bound qi, and gu toxins and ghost infixation. Also called zaoshe.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Hanzhong. It is collected and steamed. It grows among the branches of trees; it is the cocoon of the gusi [slug moth]. Taken in the eighth month.

## Notes

The entry on queweng (cocoon of a slug moth).
- The text has no clause on where it is found.
