# 神農本草經 下經 091 蜣螂 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qianglang (dung beetle). Salty, cold. Governs fright epilepsy with tugging and slackening in children, abdominal distension with cold and heat, and [madness] and mania in adults. Also called jieqiang. Dry-roasted over fire it is good. It lives in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Changsha. Taken on the fifth day of the fifth month, steamed and stored.

## Notes

The entry on qianglang (dung beetle).
- In 大人疾狂易, "madness" is supplied following 大人癲疾狂易 in the Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition (the base is kept); that text also writes 蜣蜋, 瘈瘲 and 腹張 (variants).
