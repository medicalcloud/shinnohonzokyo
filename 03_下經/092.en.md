# 神農本草經 下經 092 螻蛄 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lougu (mole cricket). Salty, cold. Governs difficult childbirth; it draws out thorns in the flesh (the *Taiping yulan* reads "thorns lodged in the flesh"), ruptures welling-abscesses and swellings, brings down obstruction of the gullet (the *Taiping yulan* reads "throat"), resolves toxins and dispels malign sores. Also called huigu (the *Taiping yulan* reads 蟪蛄), tianlou or hu. Those that come out at night are good. It lives in plains and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It comes from Dongcheng. Taken at the Summer Solstice and dried in the sun.
