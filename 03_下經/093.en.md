# 神農本草經 下經 093 馬陸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Malu (millipede). Acrid, warm. Governs large hard concretions in the abdomen and breaks accumulations, polyps, malign sores and white baldness. Also called baizu. It lives in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Also called mazhou (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called mazhou. It comes from Yuantu.
