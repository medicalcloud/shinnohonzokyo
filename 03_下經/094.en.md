# 神農本草經 下經 094 地胆 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Didan (oil beetle). Acrid, cold. Governs ghost infixation, cold and heat, scrofulous fistulas [base: 鼠螻], malign sores and dead flesh; it breaks concretions and causes abortion. Also called yuanqing. It lives in river valleys.

The *Wu Pu* says: Didan, also called yuanqing (written 元青), dulong or qinghong (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called qing[?]. It comes from Mount Wen. Taken in the eighth month.

## Notes

The entry on didan (an oil beetle), a toxic drug.
- 鼠螻 is translated following 鼠瘻 in the Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition (the base is kept).
