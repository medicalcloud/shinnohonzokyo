# 神農本草經 下經 095 鼠婦 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shufu (woodlouse). Sour, warm. Governs qi dribbling blockage with inability to urinate, menstrual blockage and blood concretions in women, epilepsy and concretions [?], and cold and heat, and frees the waterways. Also called fupan or yiwei. It lives in level valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Also called yiwei (written 𧑓蜲). It lives in Wei Commandery and on the ground in houses. Taken on the fifth day of the fifth month.

## Notes

The entry on shufu (woodlouse).
- 女人 in the base is emended to 婦人 ("women").
- 癇癥 may be an error, since the Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 癇痓 (marked [?]); that text also writes 𧉅威 (variant).
