# 神農本草經 下經 096 熒火 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yinghuo (firefly). Acrid, slightly warm. Governs brightening the eyes, burns in children and heat qi, gu toxins and ghost infixation, and gives access to the spirits. Also called yeguang (the *Taiping yulan* quotes "also called yiyao or jizhao"; the *Daguan* edition has it in black characters). It lives in pools and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: Yinghuo, also called yezhao, yiyao, jiuhuo, jingtian, juhuo or xiehuo (*Yiwen leiju*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called fangguang, yiyao or jizhao. It lives in Jiedi. Gathered on the seventh day of the seventh month and dried in the shade.
