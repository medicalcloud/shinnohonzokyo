# 神農本草經 下經 097 衣魚 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yiyu (silverfish). Salty, warm, non-toxic. Governs mounting-conglomeration in women, inhibited urination (the *Taiping yulan* reads "diarrhoea"), and wind-stroke (the *Taiping yulan* reads "head wind") with stiff neck (the *Taiping yulan* writes 疆) in children; it is rubbed on an arching back. Also called baiyu. It lives in plains and marshes.

The *Wu Pu* says: The white fish in clothes, also called tan (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: Also called tan. It lives in Xianyang.

## Notes

The entry on yiyu (silverfish).
- 背起摩之 is taken to mean rubbing it on a back arched in spasm.
