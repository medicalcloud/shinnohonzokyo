# 神農本草經 下經 098 桃核仁 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taoheren (peach kernel). Bitter, neutral. Governs static blood, blood blockage and conglomeration evil, and kills small worms. Peach flowers kill infixation and malign ghosts and improve the complexion. Taofu: slightly warm; governs killing the hundred ghosts and spectral things (the *Chuxue ji* quotes: "Owl peaches that stay on the tree without falling kill the hundred ghosts"). Peach down governs bringing down blood conglomeration with cold and heat, and accumulated cold with childlessness. Peach borers kill ghostly evil, malign and inauspicious influences. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: Peach stones are collected in the seventh month; the kernels are taken out and dried in the shade. The flowers are collected on the third day of the third month and dried in the shade. Taofu, also called taonu or xiaojing, is fruit that stays on the tree without falling; that in the middle of the fruit is collected in the first month. Taodu is a worm that eats peach trees. It grows on Mount Tai.

## Notes

The entry on taoheren (peach kernel), with the flowers, taofu (fruit dried on the tree), down and borers appended; a lower-grade fruit.
- 癥血 in the base is emended to 瘀血 ("static blood").
