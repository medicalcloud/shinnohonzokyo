# 神農本草經 下經 099 杏核仁 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xingheren (kyokakunin; apricot kernel). Sweet, warm. Governs cough with counterflow and rising qi, thunderous rumbling and throat impediment; it brings down qi and [treats] childbirth, metal wounds, cold heart and running piglet. It grows in river valleys.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows on Mount Jin.

## Notes

The entry on xingheren (apricot kernel).
- 雷鳴 is understood as thunderous rumbling of the intestines.
