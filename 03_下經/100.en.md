# 神農本草經 下經 100 腐婢 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fubi (adzuki bean flower). Acrid, neutral. Governs intermittent malaria, cold and heat with evil qi, diarrhoea, impotence, illness from wine and headache. It grows in Hanzhong.

The *Wu Pu* says: The flower of the small bean, also called fubi (formerly written 付月, which is wrong). Shennong: sweet, [non-]toxic. Collected in the seventh month and dried in the shade for forty days. It treats headache and stops thirst (*Taiping yulan*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Hanzhong. It is the flower of the small bean. Collected in the seventh month and dried in the shade.

## Notes

The entry on fubi (adzuki bean flower), the lower-grade grain.
- "Grows in Hanzhong" in the main text gives a commandery name, which does not fit Sun's principle that place names are later additions; it is kept as in the base.
