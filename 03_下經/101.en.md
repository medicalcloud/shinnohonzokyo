# 神農本草經 下經 101 苦瓠 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kugu (bitter gourd). Bitter, cold. Governs great water with puffy swelling of the face, eyes and four limbs; it drains water and makes one vomit. It grows in rivers and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in the land of Jin.

## Notes

The entry on kugu (bitter gourd), a lower-grade vegetable.
- 令人葉 in the base is emended to 令人吐 ("makes one vomit").
