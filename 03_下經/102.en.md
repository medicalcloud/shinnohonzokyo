# 神農本草經 下經 102 水斳 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shuiqin (water dropwort). Sweet, neutral. Governs red discharge in women; it stops bleeding, nourishes essence, protects the blood vessels, augments qi, makes one plump and strong and gives an appetite. Also called shuiying. It grows in pools and marshes.

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Nanhai.

## Notes

The entry on shuiqin (Oenanthe).
- The base heading 水靳 is emended to 水斳. The Executive Yuan text of Sun's edition reads 味甘，草 (variant, probably an error).
