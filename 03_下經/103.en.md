# 神農本草經 下經 103 彼子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bizi (torreya seed). Sweet, warm. Governs evil qi in the abdomen, and removes the three worms, snake stings, gu toxins, ghost infixation and hidden corpse [disease]. It grows in mountain valleys (formerly among those set aside in the *Tang bencao*).

The *Mingyi* says: It grows in Yongchang.

## Notes

The entry on bizi, generally identified with later feizi (Torreya seed); the last drug of the Lower Classic, marked "not yet clear" in Sun's list.
- Sun's note is not translated (it cites Tao Hongjing's remark that physicians did not use it and its form was unknown).
