# 神農本草經 下經 p001 下薬，一百二十五種，為左使。主治病以応地… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The lower drugs number one hundred and twenty-five. They serve as assistants and envoys, govern the treatment of illness and correspond to earth. They are often toxic and must not be taken for long. Those who wish to dispel cold and heat and evil qi, break accumulations and cure disease should base themselves on the Lower Classic.

## Notes

The introductory statement at the head of the Lower Classic, describing the nature of the lower drugs.
- 左使 is read as 佐使 ("assistants and envoys").
