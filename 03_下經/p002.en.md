# 神農本草經 下經 p002 石灰　礬石　鉛丹　粉錫錫鏡鼻　代赭　戎塩… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shihui (lime), fanshi (alum stone), qiandan (minium), fenxi (lead powder) and xijingbi (tin mirror knob), daizhe (haematite), rongyan (rock salt) and dayan (salt), luyan (bittern), bai'e (chalk), donghui (plant ash), qinglanggan (the above: eight lower-grade jade and stone drugs; the old [edition] has twelve). Fuzi (aconite lateral root), wutou (aconite main root), tianxiong, banxia (pinellia), huzhang, yuanwei (iris), dahuang (rhubarb), tingli (descurainia), jiegeng (platycodon), langdangzi (henbane seed), caohao (sweet wormwood), xuanfuhua (inula flower), lilu (veratrum), gouwen (gelsemium), shegan (belamcanda), shehe, hengshan (dichroa root), shuqi (dichroa leaf), gansui (kansui), bailian (ampelopsis), qingxiangzi (celosia seed), guanjun, baiji (bletilla), daji (euphorbia), zeqi (sun spurge), yinyu, guanzhong, raohua, yazi, yangzhizhu (rhododendron), shanglu (pokeweed), yangti (dock), bianxu (knotgrass), langdu, baitouweng (pulsatilla), guijiu, yangtao, nüqing, lianqiao (forsythia), lüru, wujiu, luhuo, zaoxiu (paris), shichangsheng, luying, jincao, niubian, xiakucao (prunella), yuanhua (genkwa) (the above: forty-nine lower-grade herbs; the old [edition] has forty-eight). Badou (croton seed), shujiao (Sichuan pepper), zaojia (gleditsia pod), liuhua (willow catkin), lianshi (melia fruit), yuliren (bush cherry seed), mangcao (star anise leaf), leiwan (omphalia), tongye (paulownia leaf), zibaipi (catalpa bark), shinan (photinia), huanghuan, soushu, shuli (buckthorn), yaoshigen, luanhua (koelreuteria flower), manjiao (the above: seventeen lower-grade trees; the old [edition] has eighteen). Tunluan (pig testicle), mizhi (elk fat), leishu (flying squirrel), liuchu maotijia (hair and hooves of the six domestic animals) (the above: four lower-grade animal drugs; the same as in the old [edition]). Hama (toad), madao (razor clam), shetui (snake slough), qiuyin (earthworm), yeweng (potter wasp), wugong (centipede), shuizhi (leech), banmao (blister beetle), beizi (cowrie), shican, queweng, qianglang (dung beetle), lougu (mole cricket), malu (millipede), didan, shufu (woodlouse), yinghuo (firefly), yiyu (silverfish) (the above: nineteen lower-grade insects and fish; the old [edition] has eighteen). Taoheren (peach kernel), xingheren (apricot kernel) (the above: three [?] lower-grade [fruits]; the same as in the old [edition]). Fubi (the above: one lower-grade grain; the same as in the old [edition]). Kugu (bitter gourd), shuiqin (water dropwort) (the above: two lower-grade vegetables; the same as in the old [edition]). Bizi (the above one kind: not yet clear).

## Notes

The list of lower-grade drug names, with tallies for each section in parentheses.
- Some counts in the list do not agree with the tallies in the text (jade and stone: eight in the list, nine in the tally; insects and fish: nineteen and eighteen; fruits: "three" though two are named). The translation keeps the text.
- The separating space is missing in 萹蓄狼毒 and the names are separated in translation. 未祥 is read as 未詳 ("not yet clear").
