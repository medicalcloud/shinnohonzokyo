# 神農本草經 下經 p002 石灰　礬石　鉛丹　粉錫錫鏡鼻　代赭　戎塩… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shihui (sekkai; lime), fanshi (banseki; alum stone), qiandan (minium), fenxi (lead powder) and xijingbi (tin mirror knob), daizhe (taisha; hematite), rongyan (rock salt) and dayan (salt), luyan (bittern), bai'e (chalk), donghui (plant ash), qinglanggan (the above: eight lower-grade jade and stone drugs; the old [edition] has twelve). Fuzi (bushi; aconite lateral root), wutou (uzu; aconite main root), tianxiong (tenyu), banxia (hange; pinellia), huzhang (kosho), yuanwei (iris), dahuang (daio; rhubarb), tingli (descurainia), jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon), langdangzi (rotoshi; henbane seed), caohao (soko; sweet wormwood), xuanfuhua (senpukuka; inula flower), lilu (veratrum), gouwen (kofun; gelsemium), shegan (yakan; belamcanda), shehe, hengshan (dichroa root), shuqi (shokushitsu; dichroa leaf), gansui (kanzui; kansui), bailian (ampelopsis), qingxiangzi (celosia seed), guanjun, baiji (bletilla), daji (daigeki; euphorbia), zeqi (sun spurge), yinyu, guanzhong (kanju), raohua (joka), yazi, yangzhizhu (rhododendron), shanglu (shoriku; pokeweed), yangti (yotei; dock), bianxu (knotgrass), langdu (rodoku), baitouweng (hakutoo; pulsatilla), guijiu, yangtao (yoto), nüqing, lianqiao (rengyo; forsythia), lüru, wujiu, luhuo, zaoxiu (paris), shichangsheng, luying (rikuei), jincao, niubian, xiakucao (kagoso; prunella), yuanhua (genka; genkwa) (the above: forty-nine lower-grade herbs; the old [edition] has forty-eight). Badou (hazu; croton seed), shujiao (shokusho) (Sichuan pepper), zaojia (sokyo; gleditsia pod), liuhua (willow catkin), lianshi (renjitsu; melia fruit), yuliren (ikurinin; bush cherry seed), mangcao (star anise leaf), leiwan (raigan; omphalia), tongye (toyo; paulownia leaf), zibaipi (shihakuhi; catalpa bark), shinan (sekinan; photinia), huanghuan, soushu, shuli (buckthorn), yaoshigen, luanhua (koelreuteria flower), manjiao (the above: seventeen lower-grade trees; the old [edition] has eighteen). Tunluan (pig testicle), mizhi (elk fat), leishu (flying squirrel), liuchu maotijia (hair and hooves of the six domestic animals) (the above: four lower-grade animal drugs; the same as in the old [edition]). Hama (toad), madao (razor clam), shetui (dasei; snake slough), qiuyin (kyuin; earthworm), yeweng (potter wasp), wugong (goko; centipede), shuizhi (suitetsu; leech), banmao (blister beetle), beizi (baishi; cowrie), shican (sekisan), queweng (jakuo), qianglang (dung beetle), lougu (mole cricket), malu (baroku; millipede), didan (jitan), shufu (woodlouse), yinghuo (firefly), yiyu (silverfish) (the above: nineteen lower-grade insects and fish; the old [edition] has eighteen). Taoheren (tokakunin; peach kernel), xingheren (kyokakunin; apricot kernel) (the above: three [?] lower-grade [fruits]; the same as in the old [edition]). Fubi (the above: one lower-grade grain; the same as in the old [edition]). Kugu (bitter gourd), shuiqin (water dropwort) (the above: two lower-grade vegetables; the same as in the old [edition]). Bizi (the above one kind: not yet clear).

## Notes

The list of lower-grade drug names, with tallies for each section in parentheses.
- Some counts in the list do not agree with the tallies in the text (jade and stone: eight in the list, nine in the tally; insects and fish: nineteen and eighteen; fruits: "three" though two are named). The translation keeps the text.
- The separating space is missing in 萹蓄狼毒 and the names are separated in translation. 未祥 is read as 未詳 ("not yet clear").
