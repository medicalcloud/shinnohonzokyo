# 神農本草經 下經 p004 上玉、石，下品九種。旧十二種，粉錫、錫鏡… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: nine lower-grade jade and stone drugs; the old [edition] has twelve, counting fenxi and xijingbi as two, and [separating] rongyan, dayan and luyan, which is wrong; on examination the three should each be [combined into] one.

## Notes

The tally for lower-grade jade and stone drugs.
- 戎塩、大塩、滷塩為非，三考当各為一 is hard to construe; it is taken as explaining why the three salts are combined into one entry (the rongyan entry), but this is uncertain.
