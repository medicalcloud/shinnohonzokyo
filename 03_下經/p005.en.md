# 神農本草經 下經 p005 上草，下品四十九種。旧四十八種，考木部芫… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: forty-nine lower-grade herbs; the old [edition] has forty-eight. On examination, yuanhua of the tree section should be put here.

## Notes

The tally for lower-grade herbs.
