# 神農本草經 下經 p006 上木，下品一十七種。旧十八種，今移芫花入… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: seventeen lower-grade trees; the old [edition] has eighteen. Now yuanhua has been moved to the herbs.

## Notes

The tally for lower-grade trees.
