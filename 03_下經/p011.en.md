# 神農本草經 下經 p011 上菜，下品二種。旧同。 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above: two lower-grade vegetables; the same as in the old [edition].

## Notes

The tally for lower-grade vegetables. It is followed by the bizi entry and the concluding statements of the Lower Classic.
