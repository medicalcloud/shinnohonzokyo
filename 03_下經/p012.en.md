# 神農本草經 下經 p012 三品，合三百六十五種，法三百六十度，一度… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The three grades together number three hundred and sixty-five kinds, modeled on the three hundred and sixty [-five] degrees, one degree corresponding to one day, making up one year (doubling the number gives seven hundred and thirty names in all).

## Notes

The beginning of the concluding statements of the Lower Classic, matching the number of drugs to the days of the year.
- 法三百六十度 presumably means 365 degrees (the base is kept). The parenthesis is the phrase discussed in the next paragraph.
