# 神農本草經 下經 p013 掌禹錫曰：本草例，《神農本経》以朱書，《… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhang Yuxi says: By the conventions of the *bencao*, the *Shennong benjing* is written in red and the *Mingyi bielu* in black. The drugs of the *Shennong* number three hundred and sixty-five; the statement here that "doubling the number gives seven hundred and thirty names" refers to them together with the supplementary items of the *Mingyi bielu*. Thus this phrase belongs to the *Bielu* and should be written in black. It must have resulted from the confusion of red and black over a long transmission.

## Notes

A quotation of Zhang Yuxi (editor of the *Jiayou bencao*), who holds that the parenthetical phrase in the preceding paragraph is *Mingyi bielu* text.
