# 神農本草經 下經 p015 薬有君、臣、佐、使，以相宣摂合和宜。用一… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Drugs have sovereigns, ministers, assistants and envoys, which should mutually spread and regulate and be combined in harmony. One uses one sovereign, two ministers, three assistants and five envoys; or one sovereign, three ministers and nine assistants and envoys.

## Notes

A statement of the introduction on combining sovereign, minister, assistant and envoy in a prescription.
- The translation follows the base punctuation (合和宜。用一君).
