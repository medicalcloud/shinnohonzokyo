# 神農本草經 下經 p016 薬有陰陽配合，子母兄弟，根茎花実，草石骨… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Drugs have yin-yang pairings, mother-child and sibling [relations], roots, stems, flowers and fruits, and herbs, stones, bones and flesh. Some act alone, some mutually reinforce, some mutually assist, some mutually fear, some mutually abhor, some mutually oppose and some mutually kill. In combining, one [observes] these seven relations [base: 七精]: it is good to use those that reinforce and assist each other, and one must not use those that abhor or oppose each other. If there is toxicity to be controlled, one may use those that fear or kill each other. Otherwise they must not be combined.

## Notes

A statement of the introduction on the seven relations of drugs (acting alone, mutual reinforcement, assistance, fear, abhorrence, opposition and killing).
- 七精 is taken as 七情 and 合和時之 as 合和時視之 (the base is kept).
