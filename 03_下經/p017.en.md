# 神農本草經 下經 p017 薬有酸、鹹、甘、苦、辛五味，又有寒、熱、… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Drugs have the five flavors, sour, salty, sweet, bitter and acrid, and the four qi, cold, hot, warm and cool, and may be toxic or non-toxic; they are dried in the shade or the sun; there are times and months for collecting and preparing them, fresh or processed, the regions where they are produced, genuine or false, old or new—each has its rules.

## Notes

A statement of the introduction: the five flavors, four qi, toxicity, and the rules of collection and preparation.
