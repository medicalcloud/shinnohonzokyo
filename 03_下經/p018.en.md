# 神農本草經 下經 p018 薬性有宜丸者，宜散者，宜水煮者，宜酒漬者… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

By nature some drugs are suited to pills, some to powders, some to boiling in water, some to steeping in wine, some to cooking into ointments; some substances suit several forms, and some must not be put into decoctions or wine. All follow the nature of the drug and must not be transgressed.

## Notes

A statement of the introduction on suiting the preparation form to the nature of the drug.
