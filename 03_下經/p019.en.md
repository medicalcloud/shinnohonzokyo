# 神農本草經 下經 p019 欲療病，先察其原，先候病機。五臓未虚，六… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

To treat illness, first examine its origin and first assess the mechanism of the illness. If the five zang are not yet deficient, the six fu not yet exhausted, the blood vessels not yet disordered and the essence and spirit not yet scattered, taking drugs will surely save life. If the illness is already formed, one may achieve half a cure; if the force of the illness has already passed [its point], life can hardly be preserved.

## Notes

A statement of the introduction on the timing of treatment.
