# 神農本草經 下經 p020 若用毒薬療病，先起如黍粟，病去，即止。不… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If toxic drugs are used to treat illness, begin with [a dose] like a grain of millet and stop as soon as the illness goes. If it does not go, double it; if it still does not go, multiply it tenfold. Removal [of the illness] is the measure.

## Notes

A statement of the introduction on dosing toxic drugs.
