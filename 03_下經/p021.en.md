# 神農本草經 下經 p021 療寒，以熱薬；療熱，以寒薬；飲食不消，以… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Treat cold with hot drugs and heat with cold drugs; undigested food and drink with emetics and purgatives; ghost infixation and gu toxins with toxic drugs; welling-abscesses, swellings, sores and tumors with drugs for sores; and wind-damp with drugs for wind-damp. Each according to what is appropriate.

## Notes

A statement of the introduction on choosing drugs according to the illness.
