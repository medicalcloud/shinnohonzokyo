# 神農本草經 下經 p022 病在胸膈以上者，先食，後服薬；病在心腹以… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the illness is above the chest and diaphragm, eat first and take the drug afterwards; when it is below the heart and abdomen, take the drug first and eat afterwards. When it is in the four limbs and blood vessels, it is best [taken] on an empty stomach in the morning; when it is in the bones and marrow, it is best [taken] when full, at night.

## Notes

A statement of the introduction on the timing of medication.
