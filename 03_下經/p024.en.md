# 神農本草經 下經 p024 上薬，令人身安命延，昇天神仙，遨遊上下，… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The upper drugs make the body secure and prolong life; one ascends to heaven as an immortal, roams above and below, commands the myriad spirits, grows feathers on the body, and a traveling kitchen arrives at once (the *Shennong jing* quoted in the inner chapters of the *Baopuzi*, collated with the *Taiping yulan*).

## Notes

Fragments of the *Shennong jing* collected by Sun from other books (through paragraph 36). This one describes the effects of the upper drugs.
