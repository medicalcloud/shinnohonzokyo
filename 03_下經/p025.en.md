# 神農本草經 下經 p025 中薬，養性；下薬，除病。能令毒虫不加，猛… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The middle drugs nourish one's nature; the lower drugs remove illness. They can keep poisonous insects from attacking, fierce beasts from violating, malign qi from spreading, and ward off all the demons (the *Shennong jing* quoted in the inner chapters of the *Baopuzi*).

## Notes

A fragment on the effects of the middle and lower drugs.
