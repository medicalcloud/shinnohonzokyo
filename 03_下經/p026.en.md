# 神農本草經 下經 p026 太一子曰：凡薬，上者，養命；中者，養性；… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyizi said: Of drugs in general, the upper nourish life, the middle nourish one's nature and the lower nourish [the treatment of] illness (the *Bencao jing* quoted in the *Yiwen leiju*).

## Notes

A fragment giving Taiyizi's saying.
