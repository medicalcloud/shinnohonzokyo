# 神農本草經 下經 p027 太一子曰：凡薬，上者，養命；中薬，養性；… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyizi said: Of drugs in general, the upper nourish life, the middle drugs nourish one's nature and the lower drugs nourish [the treatment of] illness. Shennong then made a red whip and a hook-knife (pronounced chi-zhi). Following the six yin and yang, he went with Taiyi around (外 stands for 巡) the five sacred mountains and four great rivers, and whipped and questioned all the myriad kinds that the earth produces—herbs and stones, bones, flesh, hearts, ashes, skins, feathers and plumes—learned what they could treat and govern, matched them to the five flavours, and in one day [encountered] seventy toxins (一 was formerly miswritten 百) (the *Bencao jing* quoted in the *Taiping yulan*).

## Notes

A fragment on the legend of Shennong examining drugs with his red whip.
- Some characters (e.g. 心灰) are hard to construe; the translation keeps them.
