# 神農本草經 下經 p028 神農稽首再拝，問於太乙子曰：曽聞之時寿過… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shennong bowed his head to the ground twice and asked Taiyizi: "I have heard that in [former] times longevity exceeded a hundred years; what qi causes the misfortune of passing away?" Taiyizi said: "Heaven has nine gates; the middle way is best." Shennong then took to tasting drugs in order to save people's lives (the *Shennong bencao* quoted in the *Taiping yulan*).

## Notes

A fragment of a dialogue between Shennong and Taiyizi.
