# 神農本草經 下經 p029 按：此諸条，与今《本経》巻上文略相似，諸… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Note: These passages are broadly similar to the text of the first volume of the present *Benjing*, but what the various books quote is more than the *Benjing* text. That the sayings of Taiyizi are now missing is presumably because they were later abridged. "Red whip" and "hook-knife" are phonetic loans for "boiling and distinguishing" and "examining and regulating"; "whipping and questioning" means "distinguishing and examining". It is not a strange tale.

## Notes

Sun's comment on the preceding fragments, explaining the "red whip" legend rationally as phonetic loans.
