# 神農本草經 下經 p030 薬物有大毒，不可入口鼻耳目者，即殺人。一… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Among drug substances there are some so highly toxic that they must not enter the mouth, nose, ears or eyes, for they kill at once. The first is gouwen (Mr Lu says: it is the huangjing of shady ground whose roots and shoots grow singly, not joined); the second is the chi bird (shaped like a hen, living in the mountains); the third is yinming (red, attached to trees with its young hanging, living in the sea); the fourth is neitong (shaped like a goose, also living in the sea); the fifth is the zhen feather (like a sparrow, with black head and red beak); the sixth is the junji (living in the sea; the male is called ji and the female jun) (the *Shennong jing* quoted in the *Bowu zhi*).

## Notes

A fragment listing six highly toxic substances.
