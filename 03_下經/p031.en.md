# 神農本草經 下經 p031 薬種有五物：一曰狼毒，占斯解之；二曰巴頭… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

There are five kinds of drug [toxins]: first, langdu (rodoku), which zhansi resolves; second, batou [badou (hazu)], which the juice of huo resolves; third, li, which a decoction of lu resolves; fourth, tianxiong (tenyu) and wutou (uzu), which soybeans resolve; fifth, banmao, which rongyan resolves. When a toxic vegetable harms a child, breast milk resolves it; first give two sheng to eat and drink (the *Shennong jing* quoted in the *Bowu zhi*).

## Notes

A fragment listing toxins and their antidotes.
- 三曰黎，盧湯解之 is sometimes read as "a decoction of lilu", but the base punctuation is followed.
