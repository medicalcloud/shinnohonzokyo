# 神農本草經 下經 p032 五芝及餌丹砂、玉札、曽青、雄黄、雌黄、雲… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The five zhi, and dansha, yuzha, cengqing, xionghuang (yuo), cihuang (shio), yunmu (unmo) and taiyi yuyuliang (uyoryo) taken as food, may each be taken alone, and all make one fly and live long (the *Shennong sijing* quoted in the inner chapters of the *Baopuzi*).

## Notes

A fragment on taking immortality drugs singly.
