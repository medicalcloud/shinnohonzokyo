# 神農本草經 下經 p035 黄精与朮，餌之却粒；或遇凶年，可以絶粒。… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangjing and zhu, taken as food, let one give up grain; in a year of famine one can abstain from grain altogether. They are called "rice jerky" (the *Baopuzi* and *Shennong jing* quoted in the *Taiping yulan*).

## Notes

A fragment on huangjing and zhu as food for abstaining from grain.
