# 神農本草經 下經 p036 五味，養精神，強魂魄。五石，養髄，肌肉肥… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The five flavors nourish the essence and spirit and strengthen the hun and po. The five stones nourish the marrow and make the flesh plump and glossy. Among the drugs, those sour in flavor supplement the liver, nourish the heart and remove kidney illness; those bitter supplement the heart, nourish the spleen and remove liver illness; those sweet supplement the lung [spleen], nourish [the lung] and remove heart illness; those acrid supplement the lung, nourish the kidney and remove spleen illness; those salty supplement the lung [kidney] and remove liver illness. Thus the five flavors correspond to the five phases and the four limbs to the four seasons. Human nature is born of the four seasons and then receives its life from the five phases. Supplementing the body with the One, one does not die and one's life becomes spirit. Nourishing the child with the mother, one lives long and prolongs the years; guarding the mother with the child, one removes illness and lives out one's years (the *Yangsheng yaolüe* and *Shennong jing* quoted in the *Taiping yulan*).

## Notes

A fragment on the relation between the five flavors and the supplementation of the five zang.
- The organs assigned to sweet and salty do not fit the five-phase sequence and may contain errors; the translation keeps the text and gives the five-phase alternatives in brackets.
