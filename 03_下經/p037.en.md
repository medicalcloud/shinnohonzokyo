# 神農本草經 下經 p037 案：此諸条，当是玉石、草木三品前総論，而… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Note: These passages must have been the general introduction preceding the three grades of jade, stone, herbs and trees, which later people abridged and removed.

## Notes

Sun's comment, regarding the preceding fragments as lost parts of the general introduction. The Lower Classic ends here.
