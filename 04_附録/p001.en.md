# 神農本草經 附録 p001 附：《呉氏本草》十二条 竜眼　一名益智，一名比目（《斉民要術》）… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Longyan (ryugan; longan). Also called yizhi or bimu (*Qimin yaoshu*).

## Notes

Fragments of the *Wu shi bencao* (*Wu Pu bencao*) concerning drugs not in Sun's Classic (through paragraph 12). Longyan is also among the middle-grade drugs.
