# 神農本草經 附録 p003 附：《呉氏本草》十二条 満陰実　生平谷或圃中。延蔓如瓜葉，実如桃… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Manyinshi. It grows in level valleys or in gardens. It spreads with vines, the leaves like melon and the fruit like peach. Collected in the seventh month. It stops thirst and prolongs the years (*Taiping yulan*).

## Notes

A *Wu shi bencao* fragment; manyinshi is unidentified.
