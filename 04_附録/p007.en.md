# 神農本草經 附録 p007 附：《呉氏本草》十二条 穀樹皮　治喉閉。一名楮（《太平御覧》）。 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gushupi (paper mulberry bark). It treats throat blockage. Also called chu (*Taiping yulan*).

## Notes

A *Wu shi bencao* fragment on the bark of the paper mulberry.
