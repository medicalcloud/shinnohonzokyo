# 神農本草經 附録 p008 附：《呉氏本草》十二条 桜桃　味甘。主調中益気，令人好顔色，美志… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yingtao (cherry). Sweet. Governs regulating the centre and augmenting qi; it improves the complexion and enhances the will and qi. Also called zhutao or maiying (*Yiwen leiju*).

## Notes

A *Wu shi bencao* fragment.
