# 神農本草經 附録 p010 附：《呉氏本草》十二条 大麦　一名穬麦。五穀之大盛，無毒，治消渇… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Damai (barley). Also called kuangmai. The greatest of the five grains, non-toxic; it treats wasting and thirst, dispels heat and augments qi. Honey serves as its envoy. Maizhong, also called xiaomai (shobaku; wheat), is non-toxic; it treats diarrhea but does not [...] (the character is missing) (*Taiping yulan*).

## Notes

A *Wu shi bencao* fragment; 囗 at the end marks a character missing in the base.
