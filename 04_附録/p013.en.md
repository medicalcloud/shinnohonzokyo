# 神農本草經 附録 p013 附：諸薬制使 唐慎微曰：《神農本経》相使，正各一種，冀… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tang Shenwei says: In the *Shennong benjing* the mutual envoys are properly one kind each; but when supplemented by the *Yaodui* there are two or three.

## Notes

The opening statement of the appended list of drug relations, explaining its origin.
