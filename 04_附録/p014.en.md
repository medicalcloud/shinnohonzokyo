# 神農本草經 附録 p014 附：諸薬制使／玉、石，上部 玉泉　畏款冬花。 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

yuquan: it fears kuandonghua (kantoka).

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi [kijitsu]): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
