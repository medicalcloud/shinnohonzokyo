# 神農本草經 附録 p019 附：諸薬制使／玉、石，上部 鍾乳　蛇床子為使，悪牡丹、牡蒙、元石、畏… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

zhongru: shechuangzi (jashoshi) serves as envoy; it abhors mudan (botan), mumeng and yuanshi; it fears zishiying (murasakisekiei) and rangcao.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
