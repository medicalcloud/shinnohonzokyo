# 神農本草經 附録 p046 附：諸薬制使／草薬，上部 六芝　薯蕷為使；得髪良；悪常山；畏扁青、… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

liuzhi: shuyu (shoyo) serves as envoy; it is good with fa; it abhors changshan; it fears bianqing and yinchen.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
