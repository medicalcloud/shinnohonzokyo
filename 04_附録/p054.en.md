# 神農本草經 附録 p054 附：諸薬制使／草薬，上部 遠志　得茯苓、冬葵子、竜骨，良；殺天雄、… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

yuanzhi (onji): it is good with fuling (bukuryo), dongkuizi (tokishi) and longgu (ryukotsu); it kills the toxin of tianxiong (tenyu) and fuzi (bushi); it fears zhenzhu, feilian and lilu.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
