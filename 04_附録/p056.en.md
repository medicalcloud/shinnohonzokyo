# 神農本草經 附録 p056 附：諸薬制使／草薬，上部 石斛　陸英為使；悪凝水石、巴豆；畏白殭蚕… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

shihu: luying serves as envoy; it abhors ningshuishi and badou; it fears baijiangcan and leiwan.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
