# 神農本草經 附録 p058 附：諸薬制使／草薬，上部 甘草　朮、乾漆、苦参為使；悪遠志；反甘遂… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

gancao (kanzo): zhu, ganqi (kanshitsu) and kushen (kujin) serve as envoy; it abhors yuanzhi (onji); it opposes gansui (kanzui), daji (daigeki), yuanhua (genka) and haizao (kaiso).

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi (kijitsu)): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
