# 神農本草經 附録 p058 附：諸薬制使／草薬，上部 甘草　朮、乾漆、苦参為使；悪遠志；反甘遂… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

gancao: zhu, ganqi and kushen serve as envoy; it abhors yuanzhi; it opposes gansui, daji, yuanhua and haizao.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
