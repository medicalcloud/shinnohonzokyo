# 神農本草經 附録 p061 附：諸薬制使／草薬，上部 細辛　曽青、東根為使；悪狼毒、山茱萸、黄… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

xixin (saishin): cengqing and donggen serve as envoy; it abhors langdu (rodoku), shanzhuyu (sanshuyu) and huangqi (ogi); it fears huashi (kasseki) and xiaoshi; it opposes lilu.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi [kijitsu]): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
