# 神農本草經 附録 p067 附：諸薬制使／草薬，上部 菟糸子　得酒良；薯蕷、松脂為使；悪雚茵。 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

tusizi (toshishi): it is good with jiu; shuyu (shoyo) and songzhi (matsuyani) serve as envoy; it abhors guanyin.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
