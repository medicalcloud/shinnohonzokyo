# 神農本草經 附録 p068 附：諸薬制使／草薬，上部 巴戟天　覆盆子為使；悪朝生、雷丸、丹参。 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

bajitian (hagekiten): fupenzi serves as envoy; it abhors chaosheng, leiwan (raigan) and danshen (tanjin).

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
