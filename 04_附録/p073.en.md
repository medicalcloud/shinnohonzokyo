# 神農本草經 附録 p073 附：諸薬制使／草薬，上部 黄連　黄芩、竜骨、理石為使；悪菊花、芫花… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

huanglian: huangqin, longgu and lishi serve as envoy; it abhors juhua, yuanhua, yuanshen and baixianpi; it fears kuandong; it overcomes wutou; it resolves the toxin of badou.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
