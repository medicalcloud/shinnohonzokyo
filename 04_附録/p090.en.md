# 神農本草經 附録 p090 附：諸薬制使／草薬，中部 乾薑　秦椒為使；悪黄連、黄芩、天鼠屎；殺… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

ganjiang (kankyo): qinjiao serves as envoy; it abhors huanglian (oren), huangqin (ogon) and tianshushi (tensoshi); it kills the toxin of banxia (hange) and langdang.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi [kijitsu]): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
