# 神農本草經 附録 p110 附：諸薬制使／草薬，中部 款冬花　杏仁為使；得紫菀，良；悪皂莢、硝… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

kuandonghua: xingren serves as envoy; it is good with ziwan; it abhors zaojia, xiaoshi and yuanshen; it fears beimu, xinyi, mahuang, huangqin, huanglian, huangqi and qingxiang.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
