# 神農本草經 附録 p112 附：諸薬制使／草薬，中部 防己　殷孽為使；悪細辛；畏萆薢；殺雄黄毒… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

fangji: yinnie serves as envoy; it abhors xixin; it fears bixie; it kills the toxin of xionghuang.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
