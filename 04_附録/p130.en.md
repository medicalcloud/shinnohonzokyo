# 神農本草經 附録 p130 附：諸薬制使／草薬，下部 半夏　射干為使；悪皂莢；畏雄黄、生薑、乾… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

banxia (hange): shegan (yakan) serves as envoy; it abhors zaojia (sokyo); it fears xionghuang (yuo), shengjiang (shokyo), ganjiang (kankyo), qinpi (shinpi) and guijia (kiko); it opposes wutou (uzu).

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi [kijitsu]): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
