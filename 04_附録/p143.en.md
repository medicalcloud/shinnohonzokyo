# 神農本草經 附録 p143 附：諸薬制使／木薬，上部 茯苓，茯神　馬間為使；悪白蘞；畏牡蒙、地… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

fuling (bukuryo) and fushen: majian serves as envoy; it abhors bailian; it fears mumeng, diyu (chiyu), xionghuang (yuo), qinjiao and guijia (kiko).

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
