# 神農本草經 附録 p164 附：諸薬制使／木薬，下部 巴豆　芫花為使；悪蘘草；畏大黄、黄連、藜… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

badou (hazu): yuanhua (genka) serves as envoy; it abhors rangcao; it fears dahuang (daio), huanglian (oren) and lilu; it kills the toxin of banmao.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
