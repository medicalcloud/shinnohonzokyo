# 神農本草經 附録 p184 附：諸薬制使／虫、魚，上部 牡蠣　貝母為使；得甘草、牛膝、遠志、蛇床… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

muli (borei): beimu (baimo) serves as envoy; it is good with gancao (kanzo), niuxi (goshitsu), yuanzhi (onji) and shechuang; it abhors mahuang (mao), wuzhuyu (goshuyu) and xinyi (shini).

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi [kijitsu]): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
