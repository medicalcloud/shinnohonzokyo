# 神農本草經 附録 p184 附：諸薬制使／虫、魚，上部 牡蠣　貝母為使；得甘草、牛膝、遠志、蛇床… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

muli: beimu serves as envoy; it is good with gancao, niuxi, yuanzhi and shechuang; it abhors mahuang, wuzhuyu and xinyi.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
