# 神農本草經 附録 p190 附：諸薬制使／虫、魚，中部 露蜂房　悪乾薑、丹参、黄芩、芍薬、牡蠣。 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

lufengfang (rohobo): it abhors ganjiang (kankyo), danshen (tanjin), huangqin (ogon), shaoyao (shakuyaku) and muli (borei).

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
