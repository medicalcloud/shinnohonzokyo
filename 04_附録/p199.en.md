# 神農本草經 附録 p199 附：諸薬制使／虫、魚，下部 斑蝥　馬刀為使；畏巴豆、丹参、空青；悪膚… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

banmao: madao serves as envoy; it fears badou (hazu), danshen (tanjin) and kongqing; it abhors fuqing.

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined).
