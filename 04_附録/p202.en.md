# 神農本草經 附録 p202 附：諸薬制使／果，上部 大棗　殺烏頭，毒。 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dazao (taiso): it kills the toxin of wutou (uzu).

## Notes

An entry in the list of drug relations (zhuyao zhishi): which drugs serve as its envoys (assistants), and which it fears, abhors or opposes (relations that weaken effects or must not be combined). The base misplaces the comma (殺烏頭，毒).
