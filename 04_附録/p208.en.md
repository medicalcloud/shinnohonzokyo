# 神農本草經 附録 p208 附：諸薬制使／米，中部 上二百三十一種，有相制使，其餘皆無（三十… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The above two hundred and thirty-one kinds have relations of control and envoy; the rest all have none (thirty-four kinds were added later; note: it should say thirty-five).

## Notes

The closing note of the list of drug relations.
