# 神農本草經 附録 p209 附：諸薬制使／米，中部 立冬之日，菊、巻柏先生；時為陽起石、桑螵… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the day of the Beginning of Winter, chrysanthemum and juanbai first grow; at that time they [serve as envoys for] yangqishi and sangpiaoxiao. They are envoys for ten things, govern two hundred herbs and are their chief.

## Notes

A doctrine attributed to the *Yaodui* linking seasons and drugs (through paragraph 213); parts are hard to construe.
