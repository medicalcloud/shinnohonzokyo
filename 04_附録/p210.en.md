# 神農本草經 附録 p210 附：諸薬制使／米，中部 立春之日，木蘭、射干先生。為柴胡、半夏使… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the day of the Beginning of Spring, mulan and shegan first grow. They serve as envoys for chaihu and banxia. They govern headache; forty-five joints.

## Notes

The *Yaodui* doctrine of seasons and drugs; what 四十五節 counts is unclear.
