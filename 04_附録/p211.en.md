# 神農本草經 附録 p211 附：諸薬制使／米，中部 立夏之日，蜚蠊先生。為人参、茯苓使。主腹… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the day of the Beginning of Summer, feilian first appears. It serves as envoy for renshen (ninjin) and fuling (bukuryo). It governs the abdomen; seven joints; it protects the spirit and guards the center.

## Notes

The *Yaodui* doctrine of seasons and drugs.
