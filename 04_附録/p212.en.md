# 神農本草經 附録 p212 附：諸薬制使／米，中部 夏至之日，豕道、茱萸先生；為牡蠣、烏喙使… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the day of the Summer Solstice, shidao and zhuyu first grow. They serve as envoys for muli and wuhui. They govern the four limbs; thirty-two joints.

## Notes

The *Yaodui* doctrine of seasons and drugs; shidao is unidentified.
