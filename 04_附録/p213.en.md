# 神農本草經 附録 p213 附：諸薬制使／米，中部 立秋之日，白芷、防風先生。為細辛、蜀漆使… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the day of the Beginning of Autumn, baizhi and fangfeng first grow. They serve as envoys for xixin and shuqi. They govern the chest and back; twenty-four joints.

## Notes

The *Yaodui* doctrine of seasons and drugs.
