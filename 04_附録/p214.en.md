# 神農本草經 附録 p214 附：諸薬制使／米，中部 （原注：上此五条，出《薬対》中，義旨淵深… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

(Original note: the above five passages come from the *Yaodui*. Their meaning is profound and beyond common understanding. Although they cannot be followed in practice, they are the root of the system of governance, and so they are also recorded.)

## Notes

The original note (probably Tao Hongjing's), explaining why the five passages were recorded. Sun's edition ends here.
